Heart Rate Zones Calculator

Five training zones built the proper way: maximum heart rate from the Tanaka formula (more accurate than 220 − age), then each zone calculated on your heart-rate reserve — the gap between resting and maximum — so the zones fit your fitness, not just your age.

Max HR (Tanaka) = 208 − 0.7 × age · Zone target = resting + % × (max − resting) (Karvonen)

Worked example

Worked example: age 30, resting 60 → max HR 187, reserve 127. Zone 2 (60–70%) is 136–149 bpm — the easy aerobic pace most endurance training lives in.
