Time Card Calculator

Start time, end time, break — the three numbers on every timesheet. This tool turns them into decimal hours, weekly totals and gross pay, and handles night shifts that cross midnight.

Hours = (end − start) − break · Decimal hours for payroll · Weekly = daily × days worked

Worked example

Worked example: 09:00 to 17:30 with a 30-minute break → 8.0 hours; five such days → 40.0 hours; at $20 an hour → $800 gross for the week.

How to use

  1. Type your numbers in the boxes above.
  2. Tap Calculate — or just type, the answer updates as you go.
  3. Read the big answer and the breakdown table below it.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
