Pendulum Period Calculator

A pendulum's period depends on its length and gravity — famously, not on its mass or (for small swings) its amplitude. Enter a length for the period and frequency, or see why a 'seconds pendulum' is just under a metre long.

Period T = 2π × √(L ÷ g) · Frequency = 1 ÷ T
