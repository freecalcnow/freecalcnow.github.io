Yield to Call Calculator

Issuers call bonds when rates fall, cutting your income short. Yield to call assumes exactly that: coupons until the call date, then the call price back. For premium bonds it is usually lower than yield to maturity — and the more honest number.

Price = coupons discounted at the yield + call price discounted at the yield (solved numerically)

Worked example

Worked example: a $1,000 bond with a 6% coupon priced at $1,050, callable in 5 years at $1,020 → a yield to call of about 5.2%.
