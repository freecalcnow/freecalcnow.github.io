Flooring Calculator

Flooring is sold by the box, and every room wastes some to cuts — pros add 10% for straight layouts, 15% for diagonal. This tool totals your area, adds the waste allowance, and rounds up to whole boxes, because half a box is not a thing shops sell.

Boxes = ceil( room area × (1 + waste %) ÷ coverage per box )

Worked example

Worked example: a 200 sq ft room with 10% waste needs 220 sq ft of flooring; at 22 sq ft a box, that is exactly 10 boxes.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
