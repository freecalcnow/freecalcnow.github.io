Cell Seeding Calculator

Experiments live or die on seeding density: too sparse and cells sulk, too dense and they overgrow before the assay. From your counted concentration and the density each well needs, this tool gives the exact pipetting volumes — including the overage every experienced hand adds.

Volume per well = cells needed per well ÷ stock concentration · Total suspension = per-well volume × wells (× 1.1 for pipetting losses) · If diluting: cells to take = total cells needed ÷ concentration

Worked example

Worked example: a stock at 4×10⁵ cells/ml, seeding 2×10⁴ cells per well across a 96-well plate, needs 0.05 ml per well — 4.8 ml for the plate, 5.3 ml with 10% overage. One 10 ml flask of stock covers the plate with room to spare.
