Polynomial Calculator

Type a polynomial's coefficients, highest power first — for x² − 5x + 6, type 1, -5, 6 — and this evaluates it at your x, differentiates it, and integrates it from 0 to x, showing all three polynomials written out.

P(x) = aₙxⁿ + … + a₁x + a₀ · P′(x): each term's power drops by 1, coefficient × old power · ∫P: power rises by 1, coefficient ÷ new power

Worked example

Worked example: coefficients 1, -5, 6 (the polynomial x² − 5x + 6) at x = 4 → P(4) = 2, P′(4) = 3, and the integral from 0 to 4 ≈ 5.333.
