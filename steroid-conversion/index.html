Steroid Dose Converter (Glucocorticoid Equivalence)

Steroids are prescribed in milligrams that are not remotely interchangeable: 0.75 mg of dexamethasone does the anti-inflammatory work of 5 mg of prednisone or 20 mg of hydrocortisone. This converter moves a dose between the common glucocorticoids on the standard equivalence table.

Standard equivalents (anti-inflammatory): hydrocortisone 20 mg = cortisone 25 = prednisone 5 = prednisolone 5 = methylprednisolone 4 = triamcinolone 4 = dexamethasone 0.75 = betamethasone 0.6

Worked example

Worked example: dexamethasone 6 mg — the RECOVERY trial dose for severe COVID-19 — equals 6 ÷ 0.75 × 5 = 40 mg of prednisone, or 160 mg of hydrocortisone. The small number is not a small dose.
