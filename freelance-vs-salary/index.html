Freelance ↔ Salary Converter

“$65 an hour freelance — is that like a $100k job?” Roughly, yes — and this tool shows why: the freelance gross must also cover tax, expenses and unpaid time before it can be compared with a salary.

Freelance gross = rate × billable hrs/week × weeks · Comparable salary ≈ gross − expenses, before the tax each route pays

Worked example

Worked example: $65 an hour × 30 billable hours × 48 weeks = $93,600 gross. Take off $5,000 of expenses and the salary it most resembles is about $88,600 — before remembering that a salary also carries paid leave and employer contributions on top.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn’t $65/hr just $135,200 a year?

$135,200 assumes 40 paid hours × 52 weeks. Freelancers are not paid for holidays, admin or gaps between clients — 30 billable hours for 48 weeks is a realistic full book, and it changes the answer by tens of thousands.

Going the other way — salary to freelance?

Use the freelance hourly rate calculator: it starts from the take-home you want and builds the rate up properly.
