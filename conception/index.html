Conception Date Calculator

Obstetric maths runs backwards from the due date: conception is typically 266 days before it, and the last period 280 days before. This tool finds both — and today's stage of pregnancy between them.

Conception ≈ due date − 266 days · Last period (LMP) ≈ due date − 280 days · Gestational age = today − LMP
