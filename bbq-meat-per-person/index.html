BBQ Meat Per Person Calculator

The barbecue buying rule: plan raw weight per person by appetite, then correct for bone (bone-in cuts yield less edible meat). Sides shrink the number more than hosts expect — with plenty of sides, the light figure is plenty.

Total raw = guests × grams per person × bone factor
