Pythagorean Triples Generator

3-4-5 is just the first of an infinite family: whole-number triples a² + b² = c². Euclid's formula — a = m² − n², b = 2mn, c = m² + n² — generates every primitive triple, and scaling gives the rest. This tool lists them all up to your hypotenuse limit.

Euclid: for m > n, coprime, of opposite parity → a = m² − n², b = 2mn, c = m² + n² (primitive) · Multiples k·(a, b, c) give the non-primitive triples

Worked example

Worked example: up to a hypotenuse of 25 there are exactly 8 triples — (3, 4, 5), (5, 12, 13), (6, 8, 10), (7, 24, 25), (8, 15, 17), (9, 12, 15), (12, 16, 20) and (15, 20, 25) — three of them primitive.
