Hyperfocal Distance Calculator

Landscape photographers do not focus at infinity — they focus at the hyperfocal distance, the point that maximises depth of field so everything from half that distance to the horizon is acceptably sharp. It depends on focal length, aperture and the sensor's circle of confusion.

H = f² ÷ (aperture × circle of confusion) + f · Sharp from H ÷ 2 to infinity when focused at H

Worked example

Worked example: a 24 mm lens at f/8 on full frame (CoC 0.029 mm) → H = 576 ÷ (8 × 0.029) + 24 ≈ 2,507 mm ≈ 2.51 m. Focus at 2.5 m and everything from about 1.25 m to infinity is sharp.
