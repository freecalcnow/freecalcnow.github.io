Hydraulic Press Calculator

Pressure in a sealed fluid is the same everywhere — so force scales with piston area. Push gently on a small piston and the large piston pushes back with the area ratio times your force. The catch, as always, is distance: the big piston moves proportionally less.

F₂ = F₁ × (A₂ ÷ A₁) = F₁ × (d₂ ÷ d₁)² · d₂ moves d₁ × (A₁ ÷ A₂)

Worked example

Worked example: 100 N on a 2 cm piston driving a 10 cm piston → area ratio 25, output force 2,500 N — but the big piston rises only 1 mm for every 25 mm you push.
