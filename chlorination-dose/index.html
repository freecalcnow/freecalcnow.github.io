Pool & Water Chlorination Dose Calculator

Disinfection is prescribed in mg/L (ppm) but chemicals come in kilograms and percent strengths. This tool bridges them: pick a target dose — a routine 1–3 ppm, a shock of 10, or drinking-water disinfection — and get the grams of your actual product to weigh out.

Pure chlorine needed (g) = volume (L) × dose (mg/L) ÷ 1,000 · Product needed = pure chlorine ÷ product strength % · Calcium hypochlorite is typically 65–70% · Liquid bleach 5–12.5% · Chlorine gas 100%

Worked example

Worked example: shocking a 50,000-litre pool to 10 ppm needs 500 g of pure chlorine — from 65% calcium hypochlorite that is 500 ÷ 0.65 = 769 g of product. The same pool's daily 2 ppm top-up is just 154 g.
