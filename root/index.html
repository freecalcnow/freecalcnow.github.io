Root Calculator

Roots undo powers: the 2nd root of 144 is 12 because 12² = 144. This tool finds any root, checks whether the answer is a whole number, and shows the two whole powers your number sits between.

nth root of x = x^(1/n) · Perfect root when x = k^n for a whole number k

Worked example

Worked example: the cube root of 27 is exactly 3. The square root of 144 is exactly 12. The square root of 10 is 3.1623 — between 3² = 9 and 4² = 16.
