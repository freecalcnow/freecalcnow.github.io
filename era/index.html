Baseball ERA Calculator

ERA scales a pitcher's earned runs to a full nine-inning game, so a reliever's single inning and a starter's seven can be compared fairly. WHIP adds the traffic he allows — walks plus hits per inning. Together they are the two numbers on every broadcast graphic.

ERA = earned runs × 9 ÷ innings pitched · WHIP = (walks + hits) ÷ innings pitched. Enter innings as full innings plus outs (e.g. 54⅓ innings = 54.1 in the box below using the outs field).
