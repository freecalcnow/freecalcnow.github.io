Sunscreen Amount Calculator

SPF is tested at 2 milligrams per square centimetre of skin — about 35 ml (a full shot glass) for an average adult body. Most people apply half that and get a fraction of the labelled protection. This works out your real amount from your body surface area.

Body surface (Mosteller) = √(height cm × weight kg ÷ 3600) · Sunscreen per full-body application = BSA (m²) × 20 ml

Worked example

Worked example: 175 cm and 70 kg → BSA ≈ 1.85 m² → about 37 ml per full application — and a 200 ml bottle therefore holds only about 5 full-body applications.
