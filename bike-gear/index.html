Bike Gear Calculator

Gear inches is the classic single number for a bike gear: the gear ratio multiplied by the wheel diameter — effectively the size of the penny-farthing wheel you are mimicking. From it, speed at any pedalling cadence follows directly.

Gear inches = (chainring ÷ cog) × wheel diameter (in) · Speed = gear in × π × cadence × 60 ÷ 63,360 (mph)

Worked example

Worked example: 50 × 15 on a 27-inch wheel → 90 gear inches; at 90 rpm that is 24.10 mph (38.78 km/h).
