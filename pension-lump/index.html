Lump Sum vs Pension Calculator

Employers offering a lump-sum buyout are asking you to price longevity yourself. The fair comparison: grow the lump sum at an assumed return, and grow the pension payments as if each were invested on arrival — then see which pile is bigger at the end of the period.

Lump grown = lump × (1 + r)^t · Pension grown = monthly pension × [((1 + i)^n − 1) ÷ i], i = r ÷ 12, n = years × 12

Worked example

Worked example: a $400,000 lump sum against $2,200 a month for 25 years at a 6% return — the lump grows to about $1,716,700, the invested pension payments to about $1,524,600. The lump is ahead at these assumptions, before tax and longevity risk.
