Henry's Law Calculator

The amount of gas dissolved in a liquid is directly proportional to the gas's pressure above it — open a soda and the pressure drops, so the CO₂ leaves. Henry's law also governs oxygen dissolving for fish and nitrogen loading in divers. Give the constant and pressure for the concentration, or work backwards.

Concentration = kH × partial pressure

Worked example

Worked example: CO₂ over a drink at 3 atm with kH = 29.4 L·atm/mol → concentration = 3 ÷ 29.4 ≈ 0.102 mol/L — about 4.5 g of CO₂ per litre, a typical soda.
