Freelancer Rate Calculator — Fiverr & Upwork Fees, PKR & Hourly Rate

How much do you really keep from a freelance job? Enter the gross amount, pick the platform, and this takes off the platform fee, converts the rest to PKR at the rate you enter, and works out your real hourly rate — plus the hourly rate you need to hit your monthly income goal.

Net = gross − platform fee. Net PKR = net USD × your PKR rate. Hourly rate = net ÷ billable hours.

Worked example

$1,000 a month on Fiverr at Rs 280 per dollar. Fiverr takes 20% ($200), so you keep $800 — Rs 224,000. At 100 billable hours a month that is $8.00 an hour (Rs 2,240). To reach a Rs 300,000 monthly goal at 100 hours, you need Rs 3,000 an hour ($10.71).

How to use

  1. Enter your gross monthly amount and pick the platform.
  2. Enter today's USD → PKR rate and your billable hours.
  3. Read what you keep, your real hourly rate, and the rate your goal needs.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the Fiverr fee 20%?

Fiverr takes a flat 20% service fee on every order. Upwork takes 10% on most new contracts. Direct clients take nothing — but you do your own invoicing and chasing.

Should I use today's PKR rate?

Yes — the rupee moves. Enter the rate your bank or payment app actually gives you, not the headline rate, because the two are often different.

What counts as a billable hour?

Only hours a client pays for. Time spent finding work, writing proposals and learning does not count — so your real hourly rate is lower than your quoted rate.
