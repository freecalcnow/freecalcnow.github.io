Cantilever Beam Deflection Calculator

A cantilever — fixed at one end, loaded at the other — is the diving board, the balcony and the aircraft wing in miniature. Its tip deflection follows one of engineering's most-used formulas, driven by the load, the cube of the length, and the beam's stiffness EI.

Tip deflection δ = F × L³ ÷ (3 × E × I) · Tip slope = F × L² ÷ (2 × E × I) · Max bending moment = F × L

Worked example

Worked example: 1,000 N at the end of a 2 m steel beam (E = 200 GPa) with I = 1,000 cm⁴ → δ = 1.33 mm, tip slope 0.001 rad, root moment 2,000 N·m. Double the length and the sag would grow eightfold — the cube is merciless.
