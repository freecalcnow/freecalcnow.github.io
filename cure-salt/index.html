Curing Salt Calculator

Curing salt (Prague Powder #1: 6.25% sodium nitrite, the rest table salt) is dosed as a percentage of meat weight — 0.25% is the standard for most bacon, ham and sausage. Too little and the cure fails; too much is genuinely toxic. This weighs it exactly, and separates it from the rest of your salt.

Cure #1 = meat weight × 0.25% · Ingoing nitrite = cure % × 6.25% ≈ 156 ppm at the standard dose · Total salt target usually 2–2.5% including the cure

Worked example

Worked example: 2 kg of pork belly at 0.25% → 5.0 g of Cure #1. Aiming at 2.5% total salt means 50 g of salt overall, so add 45 g of plain salt alongside. The ingoing nitrite is about 156 ppm — inside the range regulators set for cured meats.
