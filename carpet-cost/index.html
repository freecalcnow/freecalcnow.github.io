Carpet & Flooring Cost Calculator

Flooring is sold by area but cut from rolls, so quotes include a waste allowance — typically 10%. Enter room dimensions and the fitted price per square unit for the material quantity and total cost.

Area to buy = length × width × (1 + waste %) · Cost = area to buy × price per unit area
