Retirement Drawdown Calculator

Retirement maths runs the compound interest film backwards: a pot of savings, still earning a return, with a fixed monthly withdrawal taken out. If the withdrawal is smaller than the monthly growth, the pot never empties — if it is bigger, this shows the month it does.

Months n = −ln(1 − rB ÷ W) ÷ ln(1 + r) · r = monthly return, B = pot, W = monthly withdrawal · if W ≤ rB the pot never depletes

Worked example

Worked example: $300,000 at 5% growth with $2,000 a month withdrawn lasts about 236 months — just under 20 years — paying out roughly $472,000 in total.
