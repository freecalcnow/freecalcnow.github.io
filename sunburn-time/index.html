Time to Sunburn Calculator

Sunburn is a dose: your skin type sets the dose (the minimal erythemal dose), and the UV index sets the delivery rate. Divide one by the other for the unprotected burn time — then see what sunscreen's SPF multiplies it to, in theory and in practice.

Minutes ≈ MED (J/m²) ÷ (UV index × 0.025 W/m²) ÷ 60

Worked example

Worked example: UV 8 with type II skin (MED ≈ 250 J/m²) → about 21 minutes unprotected. Perfectly applied SPF 30 stretches that to roughly 10 hours on paper — real life applies it thinner.
