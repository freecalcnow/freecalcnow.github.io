Lens Maker's Equation Calculator

Why is one lens stronger than another? Because of its glass and how sharply its surfaces curve. The lens maker's equation turns the two radii and the refractive index into the focal length — the calculation every spectacle and camera lens starts from.

1/f = (n − 1) × (1/R₁ − 1/R₂) · R is positive when the surface bulges toward the incoming light · Power (dioptres) = 1 ÷ f in metres

Worked example

Worked example: a biconvex lens in glass of n = 1.5 with R₁ = +20 cm and R₂ = −20 cm → 1/f = 0.5 × (1/20 + 1/20) = 0.05, so f = 20 cm and the power is +5.00 dioptres.
