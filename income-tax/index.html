Income Tax Calculator (US Federal)

Your US federal income tax for 2025, worked bracket by bracket so you can see exactly where each dollar is taxed. Enter your income and filing status; the standard deduction is pre-filled for 2025 — edit it if you itemise or yours differs.

Taxable income = income − deductions · Tax = each bracket slice × its rate, summed

Worked example

Worked example: $85,000 single with the $15,750 standard deduction → taxable $69,250, federal tax $10,149, an effective rate of 11.9% — even though the marginal bracket is 22%.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my effective rate lower than my bracket?

Brackets are marginal: only the slice of income inside each band pays that band’s rate. The first slice always pays 10%, the next 12%, and so on — the table above shows your own slices.

What is the standard deduction for 2025?

$15,750 single, $31,500 married filing jointly, $23,625 head of household. If you itemise deductions above those amounts, enter your itemised total instead.

Does this include Social Security and Medicare?

No — those are FICA payroll taxes, calculated per paycheck. The paycheck calculator on this site includes them.
