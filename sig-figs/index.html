Significant Figures Calculator

Significant figures are the digits that carry real information: leading zeros are just placeholders, trailing zeros after a decimal point are real measurements. This tool counts them by the standard rules and rounds your number to any count.

Rules: nonzero digits always count · zeros between digits count · leading zeros never count · trailing zeros count only after a decimal point

Worked example

Worked example: 0.004560 has 4 significant figures — 4, 5, 6 and the final 0 (leading zeros never count; a trailing zero after a decimal point does). 123.456 rounded to 3 sig figs is 123.
