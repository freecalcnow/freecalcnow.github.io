Running Pace Calculator

Distance and finish time in; the two numbers runners live by out — your pace per kilometre and per mile — plus your average speed in both systems.

Pace = time ÷ distance · Speed = distance ÷ time

Worked example

Worked example: 10 km in 50 minutes → 5:00 per km, 8:03 per mile, 12 km/h. A 3-hour marathon needs 4:16 per km, held 42 times in a row.
