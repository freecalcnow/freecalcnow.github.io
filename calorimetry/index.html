Calorimetry (q = mcΔT) Calculator

Dissolve it, burn it, mix it — if the temperature of the surrounding solution moves, the heat of the reaction is hiding in that move: q = mcΔT. This computes the heat exchanged and, given the moles reacted, the molar enthalpy change with the correct sign.

q = mass × specific heat capacity × ΔT · ΔH = −q ÷ moles (negative when the temperature rises: exothermic)

Worked example

Worked example: 200 g of solution (c = 4.18 J/g°C) warming by 5.5 °C absorbs 4.60 kJ — so the reaction released 4.60 kJ. For 0.05 mol reacted, ΔH = −92.0 kJ/mol.
