Party Drinks Calculator

Hosts almost always under-buy ice and over-buy mixers. The catering rule of thumb is one drink per guest per hour; this turns your guest list and party length into a shopping list — drinks, cans, bottles and the ice everyone forgets.

Total drinks = guests × hours × drinks per guest-hour (about 1) · Wine bottle ≈ 5 glasses · Ice ≈ 0.5 kg per guest

Worked example

Worked example: 20 guests for 4 hours → about 80 drinks: roughly 40 cans of beer, 4 bottles of wine (20 glasses) and 20 spirit-and-mixer serves if split evenly by thirds, plus about 10 kg of ice.
