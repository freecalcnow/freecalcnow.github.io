Savings Goal Calculator

Name the goal and the date; this works backwards to the exact monthly amount. It is the same compounding formula, run in reverse.

Monthly deposit = (Goal − FV of starting amount) × i ÷ [(1 + i)^n − 1], where i = rate ÷ 12 and n = months

Worked example

Worked example: want $10,000 in 3 years, starting from $0, at 4%? You need $261.91 a month. Start with $1,000 already saved and it drops to about $232 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What if I can’t afford that monthly amount?

Extend the years or shrink the goal and watch the number move — the trade-off is the useful part. Even one extra year usually cuts the monthly amount sharply.

Which rate should I use?

The actual rate on the account the money will sit in. A goal you need in 3 years usually belongs in a savings account, not the stock market, so the rate will be modest.
