Maximum Heart Rate Calculator (All Formulas)

'220 minus age' was never measured on real athletes — it was a rough line through old data, and it can be off by 10+ beats for an individual. Newer studies produced better formulas. This shows the four standard estimates together, their average, and the training zones that follow from it.

Fox: 220 − age · Tanaka: 208 − 0.7 × age · Gellish: 206.9 − 0.67 × age · Nes: 211 − 0.64 × age

Worked example

Worked example: age 40 → Fox 180, Tanaka 180, Gellish 180.1, Nes 185.4 — an average of about 181, putting Zone 2 (60–70%) at roughly 109–127 bpm.
