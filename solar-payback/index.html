Solar Panel Payback Calculator

Solar is a small power station bought with one cheque and repaid in monthly instalments of not-buying electricity. This works out the net cost after incentives, the yearly saving, the payback year, and the 25-year position — the four numbers every quote should be judged by.

Net cost = system cost × (1 − incentive %) · Yearly saving = yearly production × electricity price · Payback = net cost ÷ yearly saving

Worked example

Worked example: an $18,000 system with a 30% incentive nets to $12,600. Making 9,000 kWh a year against a $0.17 price saves $1,530 a year → payback in about 8.2 years, and roughly $25,650 ahead over 25 years.
