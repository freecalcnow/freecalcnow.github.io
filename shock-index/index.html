Shock Index Calculator

A heart rate of 110 with a pressure of 95 can look 'not too bad' number by number — but the ratio between them, the shock index, tells the real story. Above about 0.9 the body is compensating for something serious: bleeding, sepsis or dehydration, often before the pressure itself crashes.

Shock index = heart rate ÷ systolic BP · Modified shock index = heart rate ÷ mean arterial pressure

Worked example: heart rate 110 and blood pressure 95/60 → shock index 1.16 — clearly elevated, the kind of figure that should raise concern even if neither number alone looks extreme.
