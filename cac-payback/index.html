CAC Payback Period Calculator

Growth teams celebrate signups; cash flow asks when each signup stops being a debt. CAC payback divides the acquisition cost by the monthly gross profit per customer — the months until a customer has paid for their own arrival.

CAC = total sales & marketing spend ÷ new customers · Payback months = CAC ÷ (revenue per customer per month × gross margin %)

Worked example

Worked example: spending $50,000 to win 500 customers is a $100 CAC. At $25 a month per customer and a 70% margin, each customer returns $17.50 of gross profit a month — payback in about 5.7 months, comfortably inside the 12-month line investors like.
