Safety Stock & Reorder Point Calculator

Safety stock is insurance measured in units: enough extra inventory to cover demand being hotter than average while a delivery is late. The reorder point then tells you the shelf level at which the next order must already be on its way.

Safety stock = Z × √(lead time × σ demand² + average demand² × σ lead time²) · Reorder point = average daily demand × lead time + safety stock · Z: 90% = 1.28, 95% = 1.65, 99% = 2.33

Worked example

Worked example: selling 100 units a day (σ 20), a 7-day lead time (σ 1 day) and a 95% service level needs Z = 1.65 × √(7 × 400 + 10,000 × 1) ≈ 187 units of safety stock, and a reorder point of 700 + 187 = 887 units.
