Vaccine Efficacy & NNV Calculator

Efficacy is a relative reduction: a vaccine that cuts cases from 2% to 0.2% is '90% effective' — and prevents 18 cases per 1,000 people. Both numbers matter. This tool converts attack rates in the trial arms into efficacy, absolute risk reduction and the number needed to vaccinate.

Efficacy = 1 − (attack rate in vaccinated ÷ attack rate in unvaccinated) · ARR = unvaccinated rate − vaccinated rate · NNV = 1 ÷ ARR

Worked example

Worked example: disease in 200 of 10,000 unvaccinated people (2%) versus 20 of 10,000 vaccinated (0.2%) gives an efficacy of 90% — and an NNV of about 56: vaccinate 56 people to prevent one case over the trial period.
