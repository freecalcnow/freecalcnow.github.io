Voltage Drop Calculator (Wire)

Every metre of wire resists, and the current must travel out and back — so long runs to sheds, boats, solar arrays and EV chargers can lose serious voltage. This uses copper's resistivity with the round-trip length, and shows the drop, the percentage, and the watts wasted as heat.

Drop = 2 × length × current × resistivity ÷ cross-section · copper ρ = 0.0175 Ω·mm²/m

Worked example

Worked example: 20 A over a 25 m run of 2.5 mm² copper → drop = 2 × 25 × 20 × 0.0175 ÷ 2.5 = 7.0 V — 3% of a 230 V supply, and 140 W warming the cable.
