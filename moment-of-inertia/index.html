Moment of Inertia Calculator

Moment of inertia is rotational mass: it says how much torque a shape needs to change its spin, and mass far from the axis counts far more than mass near it. Pick a standard shape, give its mass and size, and get I about its central axis.

Rod (centre) ML²/12 · Disc ½MR² · Ring MR² · Solid sphere ⅖MR² · Hollow sphere ⅔MR² · Cylinder ½M(R₁² + R₂²)

Worked example

Worked example: a 2 kg disc of radius 0.3 m → I = ½ × 2 × 0.09 = 0.09 kg·m². The same mass as a ring would be 0.18 — twice as stubborn.
