True Cost of Car Ownership Calculator

The sticker price is the down payment on a decade of bills. This tool adds depreciation, fuel, insurance, maintenance, tyres, tax and fees into one honest figure — total cost over your ownership, and the cost of every mile you drive.

Depreciation = purchase price − resale value · Cost per mile = total of all costs ÷ total miles · Fuel = miles per year ÷ economy × fuel price

Worked example

Worked example: a $30,000 car kept 6 years, worth $12,000 at the end, driven 12,000 miles a year at 35 mpg with $3.60 fuel costs about $62,600 all-in — roughly $0.87 a mile, of which the purchase price was less than half.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
