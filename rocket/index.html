Rocket Equation (Delta-v) Calculator

Rockets are ruled by one unforgiving logarithm: velocity change grows only with the LOG of the mass ratio, so doubling your delta-v demands squaring the fuel fraction. This computes delta-v from exhaust velocity (or specific impulse) and the wet/dry masses — the first calculation of every mission design.

Δv = vₑ × ln(m₀ ÷ m_f) · vₑ = Isp × 9.80665 m/s

Worked example

Worked example: exhaust velocity 3,000 m/s with a mass ratio of 4 (1,000 kg wet, 250 kg dry) → Δv = 3,000 × ln 4 ≈ 4,159 m/s. Reaching orbit needs about 9,400 m/s including losses — hence staging.
