Invoice Factoring Fee Calculator

Factoring feels cheap because the fee is quoted as a small percentage. But the fee is charged on the whole invoice while you only receive the advance — and it repeats every few weeks. This tool converts the quote into the only number that matters: the effective annual cost.

Fee = invoice × fee % × (days ÷ 30) · You receive now = invoice × advance % · Effective annual cost = fee ÷ advance × 365 ÷ days

Worked example

Worked example: a $50,000 invoice, a 90% advance, a 2% fee per 30 days and a customer who pays in 45 days costs $1,500 in fees on a $45,000 advance — an effective annual cost of about 27%, credit-card territory.
