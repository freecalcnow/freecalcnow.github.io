Anion Gap Calculator

The anion gap compares the measured positive and negative ions in a blood panel. The difference — the 'gap' — is made of unmeasured anions, and when it rises it points clinicians toward specific causes of metabolic acidosis. Enter the three electrolytes from the same blood draw.

Anion gap = sodium − (chloride + bicarbonate), all in mmol/L (mEq/L)

Worked example

Worked example: sodium 140, chloride 104, bicarbonate 24 → 140 − 128 = an anion gap of 12, inside the usual 8–16 reference range.
