Beep Test VO₂max Calculator

The beep test's shuttle speed climbs half a kilometre per hour each level. Léger's equation turns your final level and age into a VO₂max estimate — the version validated on school and youth populations, where the test is most used.

Speed = 8.5 + 0.5 × (level − 1) km/h · VO₂max = 31.025 + 3.238 × speed − 3.248 × age + 0.1536 × speed × age
