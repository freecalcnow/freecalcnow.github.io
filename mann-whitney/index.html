Mann–Whitney U Test Calculator

When data are skewed, ordinal, or just few, the t-test's normality assumption breaks. Mann–Whitney pools both groups, ranks everything, and asks whether one group's ranks sit suspiciously high — a test about typical values that no outlier can bully.

U = n₁n₂ + n₁(n₁+1)÷2 − R₁ (R₁ = rank sum of group 1) · For n > 8, U is approximately normal with mean n₁n₂÷2 — the p-value here uses that approximation with tie and continuity corrections

Worked example

Worked example: recovery days of 5, 7, 6, 8, 9 (treatment) against 9, 12, 11, 14, 10 (control) give U = 2 and p ≈ 0.03 — the treatment group's days rank consistently lower, evidence of a real shift, not just luck.
