Freezing Point Depression Calculator

Dissolve anything in a solvent and its freezing point drops while its boiling point rises — in proportion to how many particles you added, not what they are. Road salt and antifreeze both live on this formula.

ΔTf = Kf × molality × i · ΔTb = Kb × molality × i (i = particles per formula unit)

Worked example

Worked example: a 1 molal solution in water (Kf 1.86) freezes at −1.86 °C; with salt (i ≈ 2) the same molality reaches about −3.7 °C — why salt melts ice.
