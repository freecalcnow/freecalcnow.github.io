Cohen's Kappa Calculator

Two judges agreeing 85% of the time sounds strong — until chance alone would give them 70%. Kappa strips out the luck: it is the agreement actually achieved as a fraction of the agreement that was possible beyond chance.

κ = (observed agreement − chance agreement) ÷ (1 − chance agreement) · Chance agreement comes from each rater's own category rates

Worked example

Worked example: two doctors classifying 100 scans agree on 85. They both say 'clear' about 60% of the time, so chance agreement is 0.6² + 0.4² = 52% — and κ = (0.85 − 0.52) ÷ 0.48 = 0.69, 'substantial' agreement, a more honest headline than 85%.
