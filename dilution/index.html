Dilution Calculator (C₁V₁ = C₂V₂)

Diluting never changes the amount of solute, only its concentration — so C₁V₁ always equals C₂V₂. Choose the value you need and enter the other three.

C₁ × V₁ = C₂ × V₂ — solve for the missing one

Worked example

Worked example: diluting 50 mL of a 10 M stock to 2 M needs a final volume of 250 mL (so add 200 mL of solvent). Check: 10 × 50 = 2 × 250 = 500. ✓
