Waist-to-Height Ratio Calculator

Waist-to-height ratio catches the risk BMI misses — fat carried around the middle. The public-health message fits on a poster: keep your waist to less than half your height.

WHtR = waist ÷ height (same units) · Under 0.5 is the healthy zone for adults and children alike
