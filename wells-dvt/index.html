Wells Score for DVT Calculator

Not every swollen leg is a clot, and scanning everyone wastes the scanner. The Wells score weighs nine clinical features — cancer, immobility, tenderness, swelling, previous DVT and whether anything else explains the leg better — into low, moderate or high probability, each with its own next step.

Points: active cancer +1 · paralysis/recent cast +1 · bedridden 3+ days or major surgery in 12 weeks +1 · tenderness along deep veins +1 · entire leg swollen +1 · calf 3+ cm bigger +1 · pitting oedema (symptomatic leg) +1 · collateral veins +1 · previous DVT +1 · alternative diagnosis as likely −2

Worked example: a post-surgical patient, bedridden four days (+1), with deep-vein tenderness (+1) and a calf 4 cm larger (+1) scores 3 — high probability: straight to ultrasound and treatment-dose anticoagulation while waiting.
