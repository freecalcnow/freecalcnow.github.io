YouTube Earnings Estimator

Creators quote CPM, but channels are paid RPM — what is left per thousand views after YouTube's cut and the unmonetised views are counted. Pick a niche, set your views, and see a realistic range instead of a fantasy number.

Monthly revenue = monthly views ÷ 1,000 × RPM · RPM varies hugely by niche, audience country and season

Worked example

Worked example: a finance channel with 500,000 monthly views at a typical $22 RPM earns about $11,000 a month. The same views on a comedy channel at $4 RPM earn about $2,000 — the niche is worth more than the view count.
