Inverse Square Law Calculator

Double the distance from a point source and the intensity quarters — the energy spreads over four times the area. It governs lighting, sound, radiation and gravity alike. Enter a known intensity at one distance and read the intensity at any other.

I₂ = I₁ × (d₁ ÷ d₂)² · Change in decibels = 10 × log₁₀(I₂ ÷ I₁)

Worked example

Worked example: 800 lux at 1 metre becomes 800 ÷ 9 = 88.9 lux at 3 metres — a fall of 9.54 dB.
