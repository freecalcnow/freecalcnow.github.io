Lottery: Lump Sum vs Annuity Calculator

Winners choose between a smaller lump sum now or the full jackpot paid over decades. The honest comparison taxes both, then asks what each becomes if invested — because a lump sum invested for 30 years and an annuity invested payment by payment are the only fair pair.

Lump = jackpot × cash-option % · Annuity payment = jackpot ÷ years · Both taxed, then grown at your investment return, payments invested as they arrive

Worked example

Worked example: a $100M jackpot with a 60% cash option, 37% tax and a 6% investment return — the lump sum nets $37.8M and could grow to about $217M in 30 years; the annuity nets $2.1M a year and, invested as it arrives, about $166M. The lump wins on maths; the annuity wins on not being able to spend it all.
