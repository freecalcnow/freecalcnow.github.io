Nominal ↔ Effective Rate (EAR) Converter

A '12% loan, compounded monthly' does not cost 12% a year — it costs 12.68%, because each month's interest itself earns interest. The effective annual rate (EAR) is the single honest number that lets you compare loans and savings with different compounding.

EAR = (1 + nominal rate ÷ periods per year)^periods − 1

Worked example

Worked example: 12% nominal compounded monthly → an effective 12.68% a year. Compounded continuously, the same 12% becomes 12.75%.
