Stanine Score Calculator

Stanines compress a normal distribution into nine bands, 1 to 9, with 5 in the middle — coarse on purpose, so small score differences don't pretend to be real ones. Enter a z-score (or work one out from the test's mean and spread) and read the band.

Stanine bands by z: <−1.75→1, <−1.25→2, <−0.75→3, <−0.25→4, <0.25→5, <0.75→6, <1.25→7, <1.75→8, above→9
