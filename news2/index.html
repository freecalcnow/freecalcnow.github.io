NEWS2 Score Calculator

NEWS2 turns six routine observations — breathing rate, oxygen saturation, blood pressure, pulse, consciousness and temperature — into a single score that tells staff how closely to watch and how fast to escalate. It is the standard early-warning language of UK hospitals and spreads worldwide.

Each parameter scores 0–3 against fixed bands · Total 0–4: routine monitoring · 5–6: urgent review · 7+: emergency assessment · Any single parameter scoring 3 also triggers review

Worked example

Worked example: a patient breathing 22/min (2), on air with saturations of 95% (1), systolic 105 (1), pulse 95 (1), fully alert so 0 for consciousness, and temperature 38.5 °C (1) scores 6 — an urgent response: hourly observations and a review by a clinician competent in acute illness.
