Roman Numeral Converter

Roman numerals use subtractive pairs (IV not IIII, CM not DCCCC). Convert a number up to 3,999, or paste a numeral to decode it — invalid forms are flagged rather than silently accepted.

Symbols: I 1 · V 5 · X 10 · L 50 · C 100 · D 500 · M 1000 · Subtractive pairs: IV, IX, XL, XC, CD, CM
