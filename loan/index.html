Loan / EMI Calculator

One page, both vocabularies: “monthly payment” in the US, “EMI” (equated monthly instalment) across much of the world. Same maths, same answer.

Monthly payment = P × r(1 + r)^n ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1], where r = annual rate ÷ 12 and n = months

Worked example

Worked example: borrow $20,000 at 8% for 5 years and the payment is $405.53 a month. Total repaid $24,331.80 — so the loan costs $4,331.80 in interest. At 0% the same loan is simply $20,000 ÷ 60 = $333.33 a month.

Frequently asked questions

Is EMI the same as a monthly payment?

Yes — EMI is simply the name used in India and many other markets for the fixed monthly loan payment this formula produces.

Does a longer term save money?

It lowers the monthly payment but almost always raises the total interest, often by a lot. Compare the “Total interest” row at 3 years versus 7 years on the same loan.

What about fees?

This tool uses the interest rate you enter. If a lender quotes an APR that already includes fees, use that APR for a fairer total-cost picture.
