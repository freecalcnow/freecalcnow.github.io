401(k) Growth Calculator

The employer match is part of your pay — contributing less than the full match is handing some back. This projects the account with your contribution, the match (a % of what you contribute, capped at a % of salary), salary growth and investment return.

Yearly in = salary × your % + min(match) · balance = balance × (1 + return) + yearly in · salary grows yearly
