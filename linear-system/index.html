System of Equations Solver (2×2)

Two straight-line equations, one crossing point. Cramer's rule replaces each variable in turn with the constants and divides by the same determinant — if that determinant is zero, the lines are parallel (no solution) or identical (endless solutions).

x = (c₁b₂ − c₂b₁) ÷ (a₁b₂ − a₂b₁) · y = (a₁c₂ − a₂c₁) ÷ (a₁b₂ − a₂b₁)
