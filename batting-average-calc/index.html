Batting Average & Slugging Calculator

Batting average is hits per at-bat; slugging weights hits by bases reached. Enter a batting line for both, plus on-base-plus-slugging components you can build on — the .300 average and 1.000 OPS landmarks included for scale.

AVG = hits ÷ at-bats · SLG = total bases ÷ at-bats (single 1, double 2, triple 3, HR 4)
