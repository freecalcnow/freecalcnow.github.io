Password Entropy Calculator

Password strength is measured in bits of entropy: every bit doubles the guesses an attacker needs. This works out the entropy of a password of your length and character set — and what that means against a fast offline cracking rig.

Entropy = length × log₂(charset size) · Combinations = charset ^ length · Average crack = half the combinations

Worked example

Worked example: 12 characters from all 94 keyboard symbols → 12 × log₂(94) ≈ 78.7 bits — about 1.6 million years of average cracking time at ten billion guesses a second.
