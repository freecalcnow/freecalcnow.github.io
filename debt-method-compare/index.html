Snowball vs Avalanche Calculator

Snowball says pay the smallest debt first for the wins; avalanche says pay the highest rate first for the maths. Both use the same money — minimums on everything, every spare dollar aimed at one target. This simulates both, month by month, on your two biggest debts.

Monthly simulation: interest accrues, minimums are paid on both, the extra goes to the target debt; freed payments roll into the next target

Worked example

Worked example: a $2,000 store card at 8% beside a $9,000 credit card at 24%, with $200 a month extra. Snowball kills the small card within the year for the motivational win; avalanche attacks the expensive card first and pays less interest overall. The table gives both totals for your exact numbers — the best plan is the one you will still be following in month nine.
