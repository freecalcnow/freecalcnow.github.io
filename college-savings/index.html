College Savings (529) Calculator

College costs inflate faster than almost anything else — around 5% a year historically. This tool projects what four years will cost when your child turns 18, then works backwards to the monthly saving that gets there.

Cost at 18 = today's yearly cost × (1 + inflation)^years away, summed over 4 growing years · Monthly saving from the sinking-fund formula

Worked example

Worked example: a 2-year-old, $30,000 a year in today's money, 5% cost inflation, 7% returns, $5,000 already saved → about $282,000 needed at 18, requiring roughly $759 a month.

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
