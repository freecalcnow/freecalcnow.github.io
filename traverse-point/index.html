Survey Traverse Point Calculator

Surveying moves point to point by bearing and distance: the northward piece is the latitude, the eastward piece the departure, and adding them to a known station gives the next one. This tool runs one leg — or checks a closed loop leg by leg.

Latitude (ΔN) = distance × cos(bearing) · Departure (ΔE) = distance × sin(bearing) · Bearings are measured clockwise from north · New N = old N + ΔN · New E = old E + ΔE

Worked example

Worked example: from a station at N 1000.00, E 2000.00, a leg of 85.40 m at a bearing of 63°15′ runs ΔN = +38.52 and ΔE = +76.28 — landing at N 1038.52, E 2076.28. Bearings in the second quadrant (90–180°) make ΔN negative automatically; the cosine handles it.
