House Flip ROI Calculator

A flip makes money on the spread between the after-repair value and every cost in between — not just purchase plus rehab. This counts holding and selling costs too, and checks the 70% rule.

Total cost = purchase + rehab + holding + selling · Profit = sale price − total cost · ROI = profit ÷ cash invested × 100
