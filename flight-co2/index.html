Flight CO₂ Calculator

Flying is the biggest single line in most personal carbon budgets. This estimates one passenger's share using an average economy factor of about 0.15 kg CO₂e per kilometre — published factors run 0.08–0.25 depending on aircraft, load and whether high-altitude effects are counted — then scales it by cabin class, because a business seat's floor space is emissions too.

CO₂e = distance × 0.15 kg/km × cabin multiplier (economy 1 · premium 1.6 · business 2.9 · first 4)

Worked example

Worked example: 1,500 km in economy, one way → 225 kg of CO₂e — roughly what a tree absorbs over 11 years.
