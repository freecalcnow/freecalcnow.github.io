P/E Ratio Calculator

The price-to-earnings ratio is the market's favourite shorthand: how many dollars investors pay for one dollar of yearly profit. This adds the two numbers that make it useful — earnings yield (the flip side) and PEG, which judges the P/E against the growth rate.

P/E = share price ÷ earnings per share · Earnings yield = EPS ÷ price · PEG = P/E ÷ yearly earnings growth %

Worked example

Worked example: a $150 share with $6 of earnings per share has a P/E of 25.0 and an earnings yield of 4.00%. Growing earnings at 12% a year, its PEG is 2.08.
