Cap Rate Calculator

The capitalisation rate strips a rental property down to one yield: the year's net operating income as a percentage of its value, ignoring the mortgage entirely. It lets you compare a duplex in Ohio with a flat in Florida on one honest scale.

NOI = yearly rent − yearly operating expenses · Cap rate = NOI ÷ property value

Worked example

Worked example: $2,200 a month rent is $26,400 a year; minus $7,400 of expenses leaves NOI of $19,000. On a $320,000 property that is a 5.94% cap rate.
