Frequently asked questions

Why is a US gallon different?

The US gallon (3.785 L) and the UK imperial gallon (4.546 L) are genuinely different units with the same name. This tool uses the US gallon — if a UK recipe or car manual says gallons, check which one it means.

How exact are these?

The pound, inch, foot and mile are defined in terms of metric units by international agreement (1959), so these factors are exact definitions, not measurements.
