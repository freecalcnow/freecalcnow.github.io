Pregnancy Weight Gain Range Calculator

Recommended pregnancy weight gain depends on where you start: the Institute of Medicine ranges are set by pre-pregnancy BMI. Enter yours (and whether twins) for the total range and the typical weekly pace for the second and third trimesters.

Ranges by pre-pregnancy BMI (IOM): <18.5 → 12.5–18 kg · 18.5–24.9 → 11.5–16 kg · 25–29.9 → 7–11.5 kg · ≥30 → 5–9 kg
