Boiled Egg Time Calculator

Egg timing is heat travelling to a centre: bigger eggs, fridge-cold starts and altitude (lower boiling point) all add time. Pick the yolk you want and your egg's situation for the minutes — timed from when the water returns to the boil, eggs lowered in.

Base (large, room temp, sea level): soft 6 · jammy 7.5 · hard 10 min · Adjust: fridge +0.5–1 · XL +0.5 · altitude +~1 per 1,500 m
