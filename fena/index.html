FeNa Calculator (Fractional Excretion of Sodium)

When urine output falls, the first question is whether the kidney is under-perfused (pre-renal — it hoards sodium, so FeNa is low) or injured itself (ATN — it wastes sodium, FeNa high). FeNa compares sodium clearance with creatinine clearance from one spot urine and one blood sample.

FeNa % = (urine Na × plasma Cr) ÷ (plasma Na × urine Cr) × 100

Worked example

Worked example: urine Na 20, plasma Na 140, urine Cr 100, plasma Cr 2 → FeNa = (20 × 2) ÷ (140 × 100) = 0.29% — a pre-renal pattern.
