Net Operating Income (NOI) Calculator

NOI is what a rental property earns after running costs but before the mortgage payment — deliberately, because financing is your choice, not the property's. It is the numerator of the cap rate and the figure lenders test against your loan.

NOI = (rent × 12 × (1 − vacancy %)) − (tax + insurance + maintenance + management)

Worked example

Worked example: $2,500 a month rent, 5% vacancy, $3,600 tax, $1,800 insurance, $2,400 upkeep and 8% management → NOI of $18,420 a year. On a $300,000 price that is a 6.14% cap rate.
