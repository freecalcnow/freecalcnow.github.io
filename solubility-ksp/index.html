Solubility from Ksp Calculator

Ksp compresses a salt's reluctance to dissolve into one tiny number. This expands it back: the molar solubility — how many moles actually dissolve per litre — for the common salt shapes, plus the grams per litre if you give the molar mass. Silver chloride's famous 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁰ becomes a much more graspable 0.0019 g/L.

AB salt: s = √Ksp · AB₂ or A₂B: s = ∛(Ksp ÷ 4) · AB₃: s = (Ksp ÷ 27)^¼ · A₂B₃: s = (Ksp ÷ 108)^⅕

Worked example

Worked example: AgCl, Ksp = 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁰, a 1:1 salt → s = √(1.8 × 10⁻¹⁰) = 1.34 × 10⁻⁵ mol/L. With a molar mass of 143.32 g/mol, that is 0.00192 grams per litre — about two milligrams in a litre of water.
