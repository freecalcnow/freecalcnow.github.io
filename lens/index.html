Thin Lens Calculator

One equation governs every camera, eye and magnifying glass: the focal length, object distance and image distance locked together. Give two, get the third — plus the magnification and whether the image lands upright or inverted.

1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ · Magnification m = −dᵢ ÷ dₒ (negative = inverted)

Worked example

Worked example: a lens of f = 10 cm with the object 30 cm away forms its image at 15 cm, half size and inverted (m = −0.5).
