Cat Age in Human Years Calculator

Cats compress their youth: year one reaches about 15 human years, year two adds nine, and each year after adds about four. This converts properly, with the life-stage vets use.

Year 1 ≈ 15 human years · Year 2 ≈ +9 (24 total) · Each later year ≈ +4
