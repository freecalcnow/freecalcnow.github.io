Brix ↔ Specific Gravity Converter

Degrees Brix measures dissolved sugar: 25 °Bx means 25 g of sugar per 100 g of liquid. Winemakers, jam-makers and brewers all read it — but half their instruments speak specific gravity instead. This converts both ways and estimates the alcohol if the sugar fermented dry.

SG = 1 + Brix ÷ (258.6 − (Brix ÷ 258.2) × 227.1) · Potential alcohol ≈ (SG − 1) × 131.25

Worked example

Worked example: 25 °Bx → SG 1.106, about 276 g of sugar per litre, and roughly 13.9% potential alcohol if fermented completely dry.
