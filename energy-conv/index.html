Energy Converter

Energy wears more costumes than any other quantity: the physicist's joule, the dieter's calorie, the bill's kilowatt-hour, the heating engineer's BTU. Enter a value in any of them and read it in all of them at once.

Via joules: 1 kcal = 4,184 J · 1 kWh = 3,600,000 J · 1 BTU = 1,055.06 J · 1 ft·lb = 1.35582 J

Worked example

Worked example: 1 kWh = 3,600,000 J = 3,412 BTU = 860 kcal — which is why a 2 kW heater running for half an hour 'eats' about 860 food calories of electricity.
