Water Heater Recovery Calculator

A tank heater is a race between draw and recovery. This computes reheating time from tank size, element or burner power, and the temperature rise — and how many back-to-back showers the tank sustains.

Heating time = litres × ΔT × 1.163 ÷ (watts) hours · Usable hot water ≈ 70% of the tank (mixing and stratification)
