Target Heart Rate (Karvonen) Calculator

The Karvonen method beats the flat 'percentage of max' charts because it starts from your resting heart rate: two people with the same max can have very different reserves. Coaches use it to set zone targets that actually match the athlete in front of them.

Target HR = resting HR + intensity × ((220 − age) − resting HR)

Worked example

Worked example: age 40 with a resting rate of 62 has a max of about 180 and a reserve of 118. At 70% intensity the target is 62 + 0.7 × 118 ≈ 145 bpm.
