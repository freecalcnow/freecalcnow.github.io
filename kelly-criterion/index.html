Kelly Criterion Calculator

The Kelly criterion sizes a bet so the bankroll grows as fast as possible without ruin: bet the edge divided by the odds. Most professionals use half Kelly or less, because a wrong edge estimate at full Kelly is brutal.

Kelly % = (b × p − q) ÷ b, where p = win chance, q = 1 − p, b = net odds (profit per $1 staked)

Worked example

Worked example: a 55% win chance at odds of 1.5 → (1.5 × 0.55 − 0.45) ÷ 1.5 = 25% of the bankroll at full Kelly; half Kelly is 12.5%.
