Firewood Cord Calculator

A cord is a defined stack — 4 × 4 × 8 feet, 128 cubic feet of wood AND air gaps. Enter your stack's size and a species for cords, the heat content, and what the same warmth would take in oil or gas.

Cords = (length × width × height in feet) ÷ 128 · Heat = cords × species MMBTU per cord

Worked example

Worked example: an 8 × 4 × 4 ft stack of red oak = exactly 1 cord ≈ 22.1 million BTU — the heat in about 160 gallons of heating oil.
