Graham's Law (Effusion) Calculator

Gas molecules move at speeds set by their mass: at the same temperature, light ones are faster by the square root of the mass ratio. Graham's law turns that into escape rates through a pinhole (effusion) — the effect once used to enrich uranium, one painstaking stage at a time.

Rate₁ ÷ Rate₂ = √(M₂ ÷ M₁) · Time is inverse: t₁ ÷ t₂ = √(M₁ ÷ M₂)

Worked example

Worked example: helium (M = 4) versus oxygen (M = 32) → helium effuses √8 ≈ 2.83× faster. If the oxygen takes 60 seconds, the helium takes about 21.
