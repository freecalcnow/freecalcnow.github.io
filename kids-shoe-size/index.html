Kids' Shoe Size Calculator

Children's sizes are set by foot length, and kids outgrow them every few months. Measure the foot (heel to longest toe, standing, end of day) and add a little growing room — this converts the length into US, UK and EU kids' sizes.

EU ≈ (foot length in cm + 1 cm growing room) × 1.5 · US/UK kids read from the standard length chart
