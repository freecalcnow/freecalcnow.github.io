Elo Rating Calculator

Elo turns two ratings into a probability: a 200-point gap means the stronger player should score about 76%. After the game, the rating moves by K × (actual − expected) — small when the favourite wins as predicted, large when an upset lands. This shows all three outcomes before you play.

Expected score = 1 ÷ (1 + 10^((opponent − you) ÷ 400)) · New rating = rating + K × (result − expected)

Worked example

Worked example: 1600 against 1800 → expected score 0.240. Win with K = 20 and you gain 15.2 points (to 1615); lose, and you drop only 4.8 — the system already priced the loss in.
