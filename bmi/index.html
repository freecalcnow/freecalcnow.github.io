BMI Calculator

Body mass index is a screening number, not a diagnosis — but it is the standard first check used worldwide. Metric or imperial, with the healthy range for your exact height.

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)² · Imperial: 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)²

Worked example

Worked example: 70 kg at 175 cm → BMI 22.9, inside the WHO healthy band of 18.5–24.9. The healthy weight range for 175 cm is about 56.7–76.3 kg.

Frequently asked questions

Why does BMI misclassify muscular people?

It only knows weight and height — muscle is dense, so a lean athlete can score “overweight”. Waist measurement and body composition give a fuller picture.

Is the healthy band the same for everyone?

The WHO bands above are the global standard for adults. Some guidelines suggest lower thresholds for people of South and East Asian heritage; children use percentile charts instead.
