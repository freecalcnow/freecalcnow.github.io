PPF Calculator

The PPF runs 15 years (extendable in 5-year blocks), takes up to ₹1.5 lakh a year, and its interest is tax-free. This models a deposit made at the start of each year; the real scheme credits interest on the lowest balance between the 5th and month-end, so deposit before the 5th to earn the full year.

Each year: balance = (balance + yearly deposit) × (1 + rate)
