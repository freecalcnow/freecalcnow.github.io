Salinity Converter (ppt ↔ Specific Gravity ↔ Conductivity)

Marine aquarists swear by specific gravity, oceanographers by parts per thousand, and hydroponics growers by conductivity — all measuring the same dissolved salt. This converter translates between them at standard temperature, with the reef-keeping landmarks marked.

At 25 °C, seawater: SG 1.026 ≈ 35 ppt ≈ 53 mS/cm · Conversions use the standard seawater relationships (UNESCO-style approximations) · Conductivity here is mS/cm (EC), not µS/cm

Worked example

Worked example: natural seawater at 35 ppt reads a specific gravity of about 1.026 and a conductivity of about 53 mS/cm. A reef tank target of SG 1.025 is 33.9 ppt — brackish water, by contrast, sits under 15 ppt.
