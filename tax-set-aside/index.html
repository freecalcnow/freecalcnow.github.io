Tax Set-Aside Calculator

Freelancers who spend everything get a tax bill they cannot pay. The fix is mechanical: the day each payment lands, move a fixed percentage into a separate account. This tool sizes that percentage in real money.

Set aside = income × your expected tax rate · Per $1,000 earned = rate × 10

Worked example

Worked example: $85,000 of freelance income at an expected 24% total tax rate → set aside $20,400 a year, $1,700 a month, $240 of every $1,000.
