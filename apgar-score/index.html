APGAR Score Calculator

One minute and five minutes after birth, a newborn is scored on five signs — colour, heart rate, reflex response, muscle tone and breathing — each worth 0, 1 or 2. Virginia Apgar devised it in 1952 to make the delivery room ask, quickly and consistently: does this baby need help right now?

APGAR = Appearance + Pulse + Grimace + Activity + Respiration, each scored 0–2 (total 0–10)

Worked example

Worked example: pink body with blue hands (1), heart rate over 100 (2), a cry on stimulation (2), active movement (2) and a strong cry (2) scores 9 — the most common healthy score, since hands and feet often stay blue a little longer.
