Date Calculator

Days between two dates, or the date a given number of days from another date — deadlines, notice periods, “90 days from signing”, all settled exactly, leap years included.

Worked example

Worked example: 1 January 2026 to 18 November 2026 is 321 days. Add 90 days to 18 November 2026 and you land on 16 February 2027.

Frequently asked questions

Does “days between” include the start and end days?

The headline number is the plain difference (what most contracts mean by “within N days”). The breakdown also shows the inclusive count, both end days included, since forms and policies differ.

Are business days counted?

Not here — this tool counts calendar days. For working-day deadlines, a rough guide is 5 business days per 7 calendar days, minus public holidays in your country.
