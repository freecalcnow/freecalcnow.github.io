Paper Size Calculator

The A-series is beautifully logical: A0 is exactly one square metre, and each size halves the one before, keeping the same √2 shape. US sizes follow no such rule — this tool gives every common size in every unit.

A(n) = A0 halved n times, keeping the 1:√2 ratio · 1 inch = 25.4 mm exactly

Worked example

Worked example: A4 = 210 × 297 mm = 8.27 × 11.69 inches. US Letter = 216 × 279 mm — wider and shorter than A4, which is why documents reflow between continents.
