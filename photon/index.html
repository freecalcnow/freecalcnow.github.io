Photon Energy Calculator

Light comes in packets whose energy is set by wavelength alone: blue photons carry more than red ones, X-rays vastly more. This tool converts wavelength to frequency and energy in joules and electron-volts.

Frequency = c ÷ λ · Energy = h × f = hc ÷ λ · 1 eV = 1.602×10⁻¹⁹ J

Worked example

Worked example: green light at 500 nm → frequency 6.00×10¹⁴ Hz, energy 3.97×10⁻¹⁹ J = 2.48 eV per photon.
