Golden Ratio Calculator

Divide a line so that the whole relates to the longer piece exactly as the longer piece relates to the shorter, and you get φ — 1.618…, the golden ratio. Designers reach for it for layouts and proportions; this splits any length, area target or number into its golden parts.

φ = (1 + √5) ÷ 2 ≈ 1.618034 · Longer part = whole ÷ φ · Shorter = whole − longer

Worked example

Worked example: a length of 100 splits into 61.80 and 38.20. Check: 100 ÷ 61.80 = 1.618 and 61.80 ÷ 38.20 = 1.618 — the same ratio at both scales, which is the whole point.
