SAT ↔ ACT Score Converter

Colleges read SAT and ACT scores on one scale using the College Board / ACT concordance tables. Pick a direction, enter the score, and this interpolates between the published concordance anchor points — the same ranges admissions offices use.

Concordance anchors (SAT total ↔ ACT composite): 1600↔36 · 1530↔35 · 1500↔34 · 1460↔33 · 1430↔32 · 1400↔31 · 1370↔30 · 1340↔29 · 1310↔28 · 1280↔27 · 1250↔26 · 1210↔25 · 1180↔24 · 1140↔23 · 1110↔22 · 1070↔21 · 1040↔20 · 1000↔19 · 960↔18 · 920↔17 · 880↔16 · 840↔15 · 800↔14 · 750↔13 · 700↔12 · 650↔11 · 600↔10 · 550↔9

Worked example

Worked example: an SAT of 1200 sits between the 1180 (ACT 24) and 1210 (ACT 25) anchors → about ACT 25. An ACT 30 converts back to about SAT 1370.

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
