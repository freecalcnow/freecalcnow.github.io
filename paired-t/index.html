Paired t-Test Calculator

When the same subjects are measured twice — blood pressure before and after, scores before and after training — the pairing removes person-to-person noise, making the test far more sensitive. Paste both lists in the same order and this tests the differences.

t = mean of differences ÷ (sd of differences ÷ √n)
