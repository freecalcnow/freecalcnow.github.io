Insulation Calculator

Insulation is sold by coverage: each bag or roll covers a stated area at a stated thickness. Divide your area by the coverage on the label, round up to whole bags, and you have the order — this tool prices it too.

Bags = area ÷ coverage per bag (rounded up)
