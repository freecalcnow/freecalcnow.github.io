Scientific Calculator

A full scientific calculator in your browser — trigonometry, logarithms, powers, roots and constants. Type on your keyboard or tap the keys. Your numbers never leave your browser.

How to use

  1. Tap keys or type on your keyboard — digits, + − × ÷, brackets and functions all work.
  2. Tap = or press Enter for the answer. Degrees/Radians switches the trig mode.
  3. Your last expression is remembered on this device.

Frequently asked questions

What do sin, cos and tan use — degrees or radians?

Degrees by default. Tap the Degrees button to switch to radians — the button shows the current mode.

What does % do?

Percent of the number before it: 50% is 0.5, and 200+10% is 220.

Is my calculation sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser, and the expression is only stored on your own device.
