Leap Year Checker

Leap years exist because a year is 365.2422 days, not 365 — the calendar borrows a day back most four-year cycles, then fine-tunes with the century rules that catch everyone out: 1900 was not a leap year, 2000 was.

Leap if divisible by 4 — except century years, which are leap only if divisible by 400
