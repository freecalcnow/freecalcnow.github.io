Perfect Square Checker

A perfect square is a whole number multiplied by itself: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25… This checker finds the square root, says whether your number is exactly a square, and shows the nearest squares around it.

n is a perfect square when √n is a whole number · Neighbours: ⌊√n⌋² and (⌊√n⌋ + 1)²

Worked example

Worked example: 144 → √144 = 12 exactly, so 144 is a perfect square (12²). The next square up is 169 (13²).
