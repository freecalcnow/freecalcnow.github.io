Poker Outs & Odds Calculator

An 'out' is an unseen card that completes your hand. Pros estimate with the rule of 2 and 4; this tool shows that estimate beside the exact probability, so you can see how honest the shortcut is — and price a call against the pot.

Exact: with one card to come, outs ÷ unseen cards · With two cards to come: 1 − (non-outs ÷ unseen) × ((non-outs − 1) ÷ (unseen − 1)) · Rule of 4/2: outs × 4 (two cards) or × 2 (one card) ≈ %

Worked example

Worked example: a flush draw on the flop has 9 outs. Exact odds: about 35.0% to hit by the river (the rule of 4 says 36% — close), and 19.1% on the turn alone (the rule of 2 says 18%). Against a half-pot bet you need 25% — the river price is fine, the turn price is not.
