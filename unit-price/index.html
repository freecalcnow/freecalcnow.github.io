Unit Price Comparator

Bigger is usually cheaper per unit — but 'usually' is doing heavy lifting, and shelf-edge labels are easy to misread. Enter both options and this normalises them to the same per-100-units price so the winner is arithmetic, not vibes.

Unit price = price ÷ size · compared here per 100 units (grams, mL, sheets — whatever your sizes are in)

Worked example: $3.50 for 500 g ($0.70 per 100 g) against $6.00 for 1,000 g ($0.60 per 100 g) — the big pack wins by 14.29% per unit.
