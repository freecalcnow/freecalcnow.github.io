OEE Calculator

OEE multiplies three honest fractions: of the planned time, how much the machine actually ran (availability); of its rated speed, how fast it ran (performance); and of what it made, how much was good first time (quality). World-class is 85% — and the multiplication is why it's rare.

OEE = availability × performance × quality
