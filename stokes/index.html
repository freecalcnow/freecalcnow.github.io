Stokes' Law Settling Velocity Calculator

Drop a tiny sphere into a fluid and it quickly reaches a steady sinking speed where gravity, buoyancy and drag balance. Stokes' law gives that speed exactly — the maths behind sediment settling, blood-cell sedimentation rates, and why fine silt takes days to clear from water. A Reynolds-number check tells you whether the law even applies.

v = 2 r² g (ρ particle − ρ fluid) ÷ (9 × viscosity)

Worked example

Worked example: a 0.02 mm-radius particle of density 2,500 kg/m³ settling through water → about 1.31 mm/s, reaching the bottom of a 1 m tank in roughly 13 minutes — with a Reynolds number of 0.05, safely inside Stokes territory.
