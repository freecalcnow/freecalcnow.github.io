Bitwise Operations Calculator

Permissions, flags, masks, checksums: low-level computing runs on bitwise operations. Enter two numbers in any base and see every operation's result in binary, decimal and hex side by side — the table you want while debugging a register at midnight.

AND: both bits set · OR: either bit · XOR: exactly one · NAND / NOR / XNOR: the negations · Shifts multiply or divide by powers of two · Results shown in 32-bit unsigned arithmetic

Worked example

Worked example: 12 (1100) AND 10 (1010) = 8 (1000) — only the 8s bit is set in both. 12 OR 10 = 14, 12 XOR 10 = 6. Unix permissions are this in a trench coat: 6 (110, read+write) AND 2 (010) confirms write is set.
