Mole Calculator

The mole is chemistry's counting unit: one mole is 6.022 × 10²³ particles, and its mass in grams is the molar mass. From moles and a molar mass you get both the weighable mass and the particle count — the two directions every stoichiometry problem starts with.

Mass = moles × molar mass · Particles = moles × 6.022×10²³
