Climbing Grade Converter

The same route wears five different numbers around the world: YDS in North America, French sport grades in Europe, UIAA in the German-speaking world, the UK's two-part traditional grades, and Australia's single Ewbank number. Pick a YDS grade and read its equivalents — remembering that every conversion chart is an approximation climbers argue about.
