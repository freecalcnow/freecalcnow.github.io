Superannuation Calculator

Your employer pays the Super Guarantee (12% of ordinary time earnings from 1 July 2025) into your fund, where it is taxed at 15% on the way in and grows until preservation age. This projects the balance with salary growth and your own extra contributions.

Yearly in = salary × (SG % + your extra %) × (1 − 15% contributions tax) · balance grows at the fund return
