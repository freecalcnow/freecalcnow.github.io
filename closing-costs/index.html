Closing Costs Calculator

Buyers are often surprised by the second bill: lender fees, title insurance, escrow prepayments and recording charges typically add 2–5% of the price. This tool itemises a typical closing so nothing lands as a shock.

Total = loan origination + title/escrow percentages of price + flat fees + prepaid escrow (tax & insurance reserves)

Worked example

Worked example: a $320,000 loan on a $400,000 home → about $9,700 of closing costs (1% origination $3,200, title at 0.5% $2,000, $1,500 of flat fees, $3,000 of prepaids).

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
