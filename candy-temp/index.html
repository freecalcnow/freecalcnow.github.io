Candy Stage Temperature Calculator

Candy stages are water content wearing temperature as a label — and altitude moves every stage, because water boils cooler up high. Pick a stage and your altitude: the target temperature drops by about 1 °F for every 500 ft above sea level.

Adjusted target = sea-level stage temperature − (altitude in ft ÷ 500) °F · Water boils at 212 °F − (altitude ÷ 500) at altitude

Worked example

Worked example: soft-ball stage is 235 °F at sea level. At 5,000 ft, aim for 235 − 10 = 225 °F — and your water boils at 202 °F, which is the quick way to calibrate on the day.
