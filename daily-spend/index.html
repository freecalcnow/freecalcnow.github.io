Safe Daily Spend Calculator

Budgets fail in months; they succeed in days. This takes your monthly income, removes the bills that are not negotiable and the savings you promised yourself, and divides what is left by the days in a month — one number you can hold in your head at every checkout.

Daily spend = (monthly income − monthly bills − savings) ÷ 30.44 (the average month)

Worked example

Worked example: $4,000 income, $2,600 of bills, saving 10% ($400) → $1,000 a month of true spending money = $32.85 a day, or about $230 a week.
