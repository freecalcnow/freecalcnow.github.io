Baby's First Year Cost Calculator

A baby's first year costs far more than the pram in the shop window: the monthly drip of nappies, feeding and childcare dwarfs the one-off gear. Enter your real monthly figures (or start from the example) for an honest first-year total.

First-year total = (monthly costs × 12) + one-off gear & setup

Worked example

Worked example: nappies $70, feeding $80, childcare $400, clothes $50 and health $25 a month (= $625 × 12 = $7,500) plus $600 of one-off gear → $8,100 for the year.
