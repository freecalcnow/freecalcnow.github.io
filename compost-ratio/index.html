Compost Ratio Calculator

Compost microbes want a diet near 30 parts carbon to 1 part nitrogen. Greens (grass, scraps) are nitrogen-rich; browns (leaves, straw) are carbon-rich. This tool weighs your actual mix and tells you what to add.

Pile C:N = total carbon ÷ total nitrogen, from each material's typical C and N content by weight

Worked example

Worked example: 10 kg of grass clippings (C:N ≈ 19:1) with 10 kg of dry leaves (≈ 54:1) → a pile ratio of about 28:1 — nearly perfect with no adjustment.
