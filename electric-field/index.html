Electric Field Calculator

Every electric charge warps the space around it. This gives the field strength (the force a unit charge would feel) and the electric potential at any distance from a point charge — the two numbers every electrostatics problem starts with.

E = k·q ÷ r² · V = k·q ÷ r · k = 8.988 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²

Worked example

Worked example: a 5 µC charge at 2 m → E ≈ 11,235 N/C and V ≈ 22,470 V. Halve the distance and the field quadruples — the potential only doubles.
