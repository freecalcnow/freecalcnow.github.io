Creatine Dose Calculator

Creatine dosing is weight-based in the research: a short loading phase of about 0.3 g per kg of body weight per day saturates muscle stores in a week, then a small daily maintenance dose keeps them full.

Loading = 0.3 g × body weight (kg) per day for 5–7 days · Maintenance ≈ 0.03 g × kg per day (min ~3 g)

Worked example

Worked example: at 80 kg → loading is 24 g a day (usually split into 4 × 6 g servings) for 5–7 days, then about 3 g a day. Skipping loading also works — saturation just takes 3–4 weeks.
