Area Converter

Land is measured in acres and hectares, rooms in square feet or metres — and in Pakistan and parts of India, in marla and kanal. This converts between all of them.

1 acre = 4,046.86 m² = 43,560 sq ft · 1 hectare = 10,000 m² · 1 marla = 25.2929 m² (272.25 sq ft) · 1 kanal = 20 marla = 505.857 m²
