Lawn Fertilizer Calculator

Fertilizer rates are quoted per 1,000 square feet, lawns are measured whole, and bags come in fixed weights — three numbers that never line up in your head at the store. Enter the lawn, the rate from the bag's label, and the bag size.

Pounds needed = lawn area ÷ 1000 × rate per 1000 sq ft · Bags = pounds needed ÷ bag weight, rounded up

Worked example

Worked example: a 5,000 sq ft lawn at 3.5 lb per 1,000 sq ft needs 17.5 lb — two 15 lb bags, with 12.5 lb left for next time.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
