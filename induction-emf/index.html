Induced EMF (Faraday's Law) Calculator

Move a magnet near a coil and a voltage appears — proportional to how fast the flux changes and how many turns catch it. Faraday's law is the whole of power generation in one line: every alternator, transformer and wireless charger is this equation wearing a uniform.

EMF = N × ΔΦ ÷ Δt (the minus sign in physics texts is Lenz's law: the induced current opposes the change)

Worked example

Worked example: a 500-turn coil whose flux changes by 0.004 Wb in 0.02 s → 100 V induced. Through a 25 Ω coil that drives 4 amps.
