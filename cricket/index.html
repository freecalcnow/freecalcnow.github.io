Cricket Rates Calculator

Cricket is narrated in rates: how fast the batter scores, how cheaply the bowler concedes, and how steep the chase has become. Pick the rate you need and enter its numbers — each mode shows the rate and the story around it.

Strike rate = runs ÷ balls × 100 · Economy = runs conceded ÷ overs · Required RR = runs needed ÷ overs left

Worked example

Worked example: 75 runs from 50 balls is a strike rate of 150. Chasing 180, at 120 after 12 of 20 overs, the required rate is 60 runs from 8 overs — 7.50 an over against a current rate of 10.00: ahead of the game.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
