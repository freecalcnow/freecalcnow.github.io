VO₂ Max Calculator

VO₂ max is the ceiling of your aerobic engine — the most oxygen your body can use per minute, per kilo. Labs measure it with a mask; these two classic field tests estimate it from a run or a brisk walk.

Cooper: VO₂ max = (12-minute distance in metres − 504.9) ÷ 44.73 · Rockport: 132.853 − 0.0769 × weight(lb) − 0.3877 × age + 6.315 × sex (M=1, F=0) − 3.2649 × walk time(min) − 0.1565 × finishing heart rate

Worked example

Worked example: 2,400 m in the 12-minute Cooper test → (2,400 − 504.9) ÷ 44.73 ≈ 42.4 mL/kg/min — a good amateur level.
