SIP vs FD Calculator — Which Grows Your Money More?

SIP or fixed deposit — which one grows your money more? Put in the same monthly amount for both and compare: the SIP grows at the return you expect, the FD at the bank's fixed rate. You see both final values, both gains, and the difference, year by year.

Future value of a monthly deposit = monthly × (((1+i)^n − 1) ÷ i) × (1+i), with i = yearly rate ÷ 12 and n = months. Same maths for both — only the rate changes.

Worked example

$500 a month for 10 years. At a 12% SIP return it grows to about $116,150 (you put in $60,000, so the gain is about $56,150). The same $500 a month in a 7% fixed deposit grows to about $87,050 (gain about $27,050). The SIP ends about $29,100 ahead — but only if the 12% return really happens.

How to use

  1. Enter the monthly amount and how many years you will invest.
  2. Enter the SIP return you expect and the FD rate the bank offers.
  3. Read the big answer and the year-by-year table.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SIP return guaranteed?

No. A SIP invests in the market, so the return moves up and down. The FD rate is fixed by the bank. Never plan your future on the best-case SIP number alone.

Why compare the same monthly amount?

Because that is the fair test. A lump-sum FD and a monthly SIP are different products — comparing monthly to monthly shows what the rate alone does.

What if the SIP returns only 8%?

Try it — change the SIP rate to 8% and the gap almost disappears. That is why the rate you assume matters more than anything else.
