Speaking Time Calculator

Speeches are timed in words: presenters average 120–150 words a minute, and a '10-minute talk' is roughly 1,300 words. Paste or count your script's words, pick an honest pace, and see the delivery time before the audience does.

Minutes = words ÷ words per minute · Slow deliberate ≈ 110 wpm · Conversational ≈ 130 · Brisk ≈ 160

Worked example

Worked example: an 800-word script at a conversational 130 wpm takes 800 ÷ 130 ≈ 6.2 minutes — about 6 minutes 10 seconds on stage.
