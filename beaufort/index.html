Beaufort Wind Scale Calculator

In 1805 Admiral Beaufort numbered the wind by what it did to a warship's sails; the scale survived because it still describes what wind feels like better than a bare speed does. Enter a speed in any unit and get the force number, its name, and what you would see on land and sea.

Worked example

Worked example: 45 km/h is Force 6, 'Strong breeze' — large tree branches in motion, umbrellas used with difficulty, whistling in wires ashore; at sea, white foam crests and some spray.
