Pace Converter

Paces and speeds are reciprocals wearing different clothes. Enter a pace per kilometre and get the pace per mile plus the actual speeds — the conversion every race plan needs when it crosses the Atlantic.

Pace per mile = pace per km × 1.60934 · Speed (km/h) = 60 ÷ pace per km

Worked example

Worked example: 5:30 per km → 8:51 per mile, which is 10.9 km/h (6.8 mph). A 5:00/km runner is a 8:03/mile runner.
