Meal Calorie Split Calculator

A daily calorie target is easier to follow as four smaller ones. This tool splits your target by the percentages you choose — snacks take whatever is left, so the day always adds up exactly.

Each meal = daily target × its % · Snacks = whatever percentage remains

Worked example

Worked example: 2,000 kcal split 25 / 35 / 30 → breakfast 500, lunch 700, dinner 600, and the remaining 10% gives snacks 200.
