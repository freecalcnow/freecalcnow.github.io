Soap Lye Calculator

Saponification is fixed chemistry: each oil needs a specific amount of lye per gram (its SAP value). Choose the oil, weigh it, set your superfat (the lye discount that leaves some oil unsaponified for a milder bar) and get exact lye and water weights.

Lye = oil weight × SAP value × (1 − superfat %) · Water ≈ 38% of oil weight (standard) or a lye-concentration of your choosing

Worked example

Worked example: 500 g coconut oil (SAP NaOH 0.191) at 5% superfat → 500 × 0.191 × 0.95 ≈ 90.7 g NaOH, with about 190 g of water.
