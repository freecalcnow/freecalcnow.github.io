Molar Mass Calculator

Molar mass is the bridge between the gram scale of the bench and the molecule scale of equations. This parses chemical formulas — capitals, subscripts as plain numbers, and brackets — and totals the standard atomic weights, element by element.

Molar mass = Σ (atoms of each element × its atomic weight)

Worked example: glucose C6H12O6 → 6(12.011) + 12(1.008) + 6(15.999) = 180.16 g/mol.
