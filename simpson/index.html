Simpson's Diversity Index Calculator

Species richness alone lies: two meadows can both hold five species, but one is an even mix and the other is 96% one grass. Simpson's index folds counts into the probability that two random individuals are different species — paste your counts (one per species) to get D, 1−D and the effective number of species.

D = Σ n(n − 1) ÷ N(N − 1) · Simpson's diversity = 1 − D · Effective species = 1 ÷ D

Worked example

Worked example: counts of 12, 8, 5, 3, 2 → D = 0.248, so 1 − D = 0.752 and 1/D ≈ 4.03 effective species out of 5 present — fairly even, mildly dominated by the commonest.
