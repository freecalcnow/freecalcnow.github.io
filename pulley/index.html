Pulley Mechanical Advantage Calculator

A block and tackle shares the load across every rope segment holding the moving pulley. Count the supporting ropes and that is your mechanical advantage: the effort divides by it — and the rope you must pull multiplies by the same number. There is no free energy, only a fair trade.

Mechanical advantage = number of supporting rope segments · Effort = load ÷ MA · Rope pulled = lift height × MA

Worked example: a 240 lb load on 4 supporting ropes needs 60 lb of effort — but to lift it 6 ft you pull 24 ft of rope.
