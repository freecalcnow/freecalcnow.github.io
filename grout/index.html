Grout Calculator

Grout use depends on joint geometry, not just area: small tiles mean far more joint length per square metre. This tool works out the joint volume honestly and converts it to kilograms of dry grout.

Joint length per m² = (1/tile length + 1/tile width) in metres · Volume = joint length × width × depth × area · Mass = volume × ~1,600 kg/m³

Worked example

Worked example: 20 m² of 300 × 300 mm tiles with 3 mm joints, 8 mm deep → about 5.1 kg of grout. Switch to 100 mm tiles and the same room needs roughly 13 kg.
