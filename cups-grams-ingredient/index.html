Cups to Grams Converter (by Ingredient)

A cup is a volume; a recipe's success is weight. Every ingredient packs differently — a cup of flour is 120–125 g, of sugar 200 g, of honey 340 g. Pick the ingredient for the honest conversion both ways.

Grams = cups × grams-per-cup for that ingredient · Cups = grams ÷ grams-per-cup
