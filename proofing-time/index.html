Dough Proofing Time Estimator

Yeast roughly doubles its speed for every 10 °C warmer (the Q10 rule) — and halves it going colder, which is the whole basis of retarding dough in the fridge. Enter a known time at a known temperature for the adjusted time at your kitchen's temperature.

Adjusted time = known time × 2^((known temp − new temp) ÷ 10) — temperatures in °C

Worked example

Worked example: a dough that proofs in 120 minutes at 24 °C needs about 120 × 2^((24−6)/10) ≈ 417 minutes (about 7 hours) in a 6 °C fridge — an overnight retard, exactly as intended.
