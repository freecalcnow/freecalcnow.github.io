UAE Gratuity (End of Service) Calculator

In the UAE, end-of-service gratuity is based on your basic salary (not allowances): 21 days of basic pay for each of the first five years of service and 30 days for every year after, capped at two years' total pay. One year of service is the minimum to qualify.

Daily basic = monthly basic salary ÷ 30 · Gratuity = (first 5 years × 21 days + later years × 30 days) × daily basic

Worked example

Worked example: basic salary AED 10,000 a month for 7 years → daily basic 333.33; days = 5×21 + 2×30 = 165; gratuity = 165 × 333.33 = AED 55,000.
