CTC to In-Hand Salary Calculator

CTC is what you cost the company; in-hand is what reaches your bank — the gap is employer PF, gratuity and your own deductions. This applies the standard Indian salary structure (basic at 40% of CTC, HRA at 50% of basic) so you can compare offers honestly.

Basic = 40% of CTC · HRA = 50% of basic · Employer PF = 12% of basic · Gratuity ≈ 4.81% of basic · In-hand ≈ (CTC − employer PF − gratuity) ÷ 12 − employee PF − professional tax

Worked example

Worked example: CTC 1,200,000 → basic 480,000, HRA 240,000, employer PF 57,600, gratuity about 23,088. Monthly gross is about 93,276 and estimated in-hand about 88,276 before income tax.
