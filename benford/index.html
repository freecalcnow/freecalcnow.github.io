Benford's Law Checker

Real measurements — invoices, populations, river lengths — start with 1 about 30% of the time and with 9 only 4.6%. Made-up numbers spread their first digits far too evenly. Paste a column of figures and compare their first digits against Benford's expected curve, with a chi-square verdict.

Benford: P(first digit d) = log₁₀(1 + 1÷d) → 1: 30.1%, 2: 17.6%, 3: 12.5% … 9: 4.6% · Chi-square below 15.5 (8 df, 5%) = consistent with Benford

Worked example

Worked example: a set of 500 genuine expense amounts usually lands a chi-square under 15 — consistent. A fabricated set with first digits spread evenly scores above 60, because its 1s appear only ~11% of the time instead of 30%.
