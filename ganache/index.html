Chocolate Ganache Ratio Calculator

Ganache is two ingredients and one decision: the ratio. More cream makes a sauce that pours; equal parts make a spreadable filling; double chocolate makes truffles you can roll. Enter how much you want and its job, and get both ingredients weighed.

Dark chocolate — glaze 1:1 · filling 2:1 · truffles 2.5–3:1 (chocolate : cream, by weight) · Milk chocolate needs ~0.5 more chocolate per part · White needs ~1 more — cocoa butter sets softer

Worked example

Worked example: 400 g of dark-chocolate truffle ganache at 2.5:1 splits into 286 g of chocolate and 114 g of cream. The same 400 g as a pourable glaze at 1:1 is 200 g of each — one recipe, two desserts, decided entirely by the ratio.
