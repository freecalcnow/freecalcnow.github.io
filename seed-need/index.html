Grass & Garden Seed Calculator

Seed is sold by weight and sown by rate: a new lawn wants about 35 g per square metre, overseeding an existing one about half that, and wildflower mixes far less. Enter the area and the job, and get the kilograms — plus a sensible margin for edges and birds.

Seed needed = area (m²) × rate (g/m²) ÷ 1,000, plus about 10% for edges, spillage and birds

Worked example

Worked example: a new 80 m² lawn at 35 g/m² needs 2.8 kg of seed — call it 3.1 kg with the margin, so one 5 kg bag covers it with seed to spare for patching.
