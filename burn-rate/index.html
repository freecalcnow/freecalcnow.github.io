Startup Burn Rate & Runway Calculator

Burn rate is how fast a startup spends its cash; runway is how many months are left at that speed. Investors ask for both in the first five minutes — this works them out from three numbers.

Net burn = monthly spending − monthly revenue · Runway = cash in bank ÷ net burn

Worked example

Worked example: spending $85,000 a month, earning $55,000 → net burn $30,000. With $600,000 in the bank, the runway is 20 months.
