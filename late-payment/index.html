Cost of Paying Late Calculator

Pay a card or bill late and two charges land: the flat late fee, and interest on the whole balance for every day it sat unpaid — often at a penalty rate. On a big balance the 'small' fee is the least of it. This prices the mistake exactly.

Cost = late fee + balance × (APR ÷ 100) × days late ÷ 365

Worked example

Worked example: a $1,200 balance paid 30 days late with a $30 fee and 24% APR → $23.67 of interest plus the $30 fee = $53.67 for one month of delay — 4.5% of the balance.
