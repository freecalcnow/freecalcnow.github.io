Unused Vacation Payout Calculator

Leaving a job with unused leave? In many places those days are paid out at your daily rate. This values your balance — before tax — and shows what each day is worth.

Daily rate = annual salary ÷ working days per year (usually 260) · Payout = daily rate × unused days · Hourly version: rate × hours per day × days
