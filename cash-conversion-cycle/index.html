Cash Conversion Cycle Calculator

Buy stock today, sell it in 45 days, get paid 38 days after that, while your own supplier waits 30 days — your money is gone for 53 days before it returns. That gap, the cash conversion cycle, is what overdrafts and working-capital loans exist to bridge.

CCC = days inventory outstanding (DIO) + days sales outstanding (DSO) − days payables outstanding (DPO)

Worked example

Worked example: stock sits 45 days, customers pay in 38, you pay suppliers in 30 → a 53-day cycle. At $5,000 a day of costs, about $265,000 of cash is permanently trapped inside it.
