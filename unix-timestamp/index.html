Unix Timestamp Converter

Computers count time as seconds since 1 January 1970 (UTC) — the Unix epoch. Paste a timestamp from a log or database and get the exact human date and time it encodes, in UTC and ISO form.

Date = epoch (1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC) + timestamp seconds
