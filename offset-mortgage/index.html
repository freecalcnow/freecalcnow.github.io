Offset Mortgage Savings Calculator

An offset mortgage links your savings to your loan: the savings earn nothing, but you pay interest only on the difference. Kept at a steady level, an offset balance both cuts the interest and — if you keep the same payment — shortens the loan itself.

Each month: interest = (loan balance − offset balance) × rate ÷ 12 · Same payment, balance simulated month by month

Worked example

Worked example: a $400,000 mortgage at 6% over 30 years with a steady $50,000 offset pays off about 6 years early and saves roughly $184,000 of interest — the tax-free, risk-free 'return' on savings that would otherwise earn a fraction of 6%.
