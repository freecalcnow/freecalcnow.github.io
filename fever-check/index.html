Fever Temperature Checker

Body temperature bands are usually quoted in one unit while thermometers show the other. Enter a reading in either unit: you get the conversion and the standard band — normal, raised, fever, or high fever.

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32 · Bands (°C): under 36.1 low · 36.1–37.2 normal · 37.3–37.9 raised · 38.0–39.4 fever · 39.5+ high fever

Worked example

Worked example: 38.5 °C = 101.3 °F — a fever. Normal is around 36.5–37.2 °C (97.7–99.0 °F), and it varies through the day.
