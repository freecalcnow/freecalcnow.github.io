Weight Loss Timeline Calculator

Fat loss is arithmetic before it is anything else: a kilogram of body fat stores roughly 7,700 kcal, so a steady daily deficit maps to a predictable timeline. This turns your target into a date — and shows what gentler and harder deficits do to it.

Days = (kg to lose × 7,700) ÷ daily deficit · Weekly rate = deficit × 7 ÷ 7,700

Worked example

Worked example: from 90 kg to 80 kg at a 500 kcal daily deficit → 10 kg × 7,700 ÷ 500 = 154 days — about 22 weeks, losing roughly 0.45 kg a week.
