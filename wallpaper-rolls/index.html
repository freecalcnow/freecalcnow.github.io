Wallpaper Rolls Calculator

Wallpaper is bought by the roll but used in drops, and pattern repeat decides how much of each drop is waste. This counts the drops around the room, the usable drops per roll at your repeat, and rounds up the way the shop will.

Drops needed = room perimeter ÷ roll width · Usable length per drop = round down(roll length ÷ (height + repeat)) drops
