Half-Life Calculator

Every half-life that passes halves what remains — after 10 half-lives, less than a thousandth is left. Works for radioactive decay, drug elimination and anything else that decays exponentially.

Remaining = N₀ × (½)^(t ÷ t½)

Worked example

Worked example: 100 g with a 5-day half-life → after 15 days (3 half-lives) exactly 12.5 g remains. Carbon-14’s half-life of 5,730 years is what makes radiocarbon dating possible.
