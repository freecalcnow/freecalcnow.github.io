Right Triangle Calculator

Enter the two sides around the right angle and everything else follows: the hypotenuse by Pythagoras, both remaining angles, the area and the perimeter. The 5-12-13 defaults are one of the classic whole-number triples.

Hypotenuse c = √(a² + b²) · Angle at A = atan(a ÷ b) · Area = a × b ÷ 2
