Mobile Data Usage Estimator

Plans are sold in gigabytes a month; life is lived in hours a day. This bridges the two with typical per-hour rates for the big four data-eaters — video, music, video calls and online gaming — so you can see which habit is actually eating the plan.

Monthly data = Σ (hours per day × GB per hour) × 30

Worked example

Worked example: 3 hours of HD video, 2 of music, 1 of video calls and 1 of gaming a day → about 10.9 GB a day, roughly 327 GB a month. The video is 83% of it.
