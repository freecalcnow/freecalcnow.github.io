Self-Employment Tax Calculator

Employees split Social Security and Medicare tax with their employer and barely see it. The self-employed pay both halves — 15.3% — on 92.35% of their net earnings, which is why freelance income feels smaller than it looks. This works out the tax and the half you can deduct.

Taxable earnings = net income × 92.35% · Social Security = 12.4% up to the wage base · Medicare = 2.9% on all

Worked example

Worked example: $80,000 of net self-employment income → $73,880 taxable. Social Security $9,161.12 + Medicare $2,142.52 = $11,303.64 of SE tax, of which $5,651.82 is deductible against income tax.
