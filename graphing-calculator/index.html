Graphing Calculator

Plot functions on a graph — type f(x) and see it drawn instantly. Up to three functions at once, with zoom, pan and a click-to-trace readout. Your numbers never leave your browser.

Tap the graph to read values.

Try: x² · sin(x) · x³−3x · 1/x · 2ˣ — use x, +, −, *, /, ^, brackets, sin, cos, tan, log, ln, sqrt, abs, pi, e.

How to use

  1. Type a function of x, like x^2 or sin(x), and tap Plot.
  2. Drag the graph to pan; use Zoom in and out, or tap the graph to read exact values.
  3. Add g(x) and h(x) to compare functions on the same axes.

Frequently asked questions

What can I type?

x, numbers, + − * / ^, brackets, and sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, log (base 10), ln, sqrt, abs, exp, pi and e. Example: 2*sin(x)+x^2.

Why is part of the graph missing?

Some functions have gaps — 1/x is undefined at x = 0, and log(x) only exists for x over 0. The graph skips those points honestly instead of drawing a false line.

Is my function sent anywhere?

No. The plotting runs entirely in your browser.
