Cell Potential Calculator

A galvanic cell's voltage is the gap between its two half-cells: the cathode's reduction potential minus the anode's. Positive gap, working battery; negative, and the reaction needs pushing — electrolysis.

E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode · ΔG° = −n × F × E°cell (F = 96,485 C/mol)

Worked example

Worked example: cathode +0.34 V, anode −0.76 V → E°cell = +1.10 V — the Daniell cell, zinc and copper, the battery that powered the telegraph age. With n = 2, ΔG° ≈ −212 kJ/mol.
