Trapezoid Area Calculator

A trapezoid's area is its height times the average of its two parallel sides — that average is the median, the segment joining the midpoints of the legs, and the reason the formula works is that the shape is secretly a parallelogram in disguise.

Area = (a + b) ÷ 2 × h, where a and b are the parallel sides and h is the distance between them

Worked example

Worked example: parallel sides 8 and 5 with a height of 4 → (8 + 5) ÷ 2 × 4 = 26 square units, and the median measures 6.5.
