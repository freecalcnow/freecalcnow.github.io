Thread Pitch ↔ TPI Converter

Metric threads are named by the space between threads; imperial threads by how many fit in an inch. They are reciprocals wearing different clothes: pitch in mm = 25.4 ÷ TPI. Enter either and get the other, plus the standard thread you are probably holding.

Pitch (mm) = 25.4 ÷ TPI · TPI = 25.4 ÷ pitch (mm) · Common pairs: M6×1.0 ≈ 25.4 TPI · 1/4-20 = 1.27 mm · M10×1.5 ≈ 16.9 TPI · 3/8-16 = 1.59 mm

Worked example

Worked example: a bolt measuring 20 threads per inch has a pitch of 25.4 ÷ 20 = 1.27 mm — that is 1/4-20 UNC if the diameter is 6.35 mm, or an M6×1.25 mis-measured: always confirm with diameter and a pitch gauge before forcing a nut.
