Field of View Calculator

Angle of view turns a focal length into what the frame covers: wide, normal or telephoto in degrees, and — at a given distance — the actual width and height of the scene captured. Photographers, camera installers and sim racers all live on these numbers.

Angle = 2 × atan(sensor dimension ÷ (2 × focal length))

Worked example

Worked example: a 24 mm lens on full frame sees 74° horizontally — at 5 m from the subject the frame covers about 7.5 m across.
