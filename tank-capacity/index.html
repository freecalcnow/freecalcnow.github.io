Water Tank Capacity Calculator

Tank sizes are sold in litres but spaces are measured in metres. This converts dimensions to capacity for the two shapes that cover almost every tank, and adds the figure that decides where it can stand: a full tank weighs a kilogram per litre, plus the tank.

Cylinder: π × radius² × height · Rectangular: length × width × height · 1 m³ = 1,000 litres

Worked example

Worked example: a cylindrical tank 1 m across and 1.5 m tall holds π × 0.25 × 1.5 ≈ 1.178 m³ — about 1,178 litres, weighing about 1,178 kg full. That is why big tanks live on the ground.
