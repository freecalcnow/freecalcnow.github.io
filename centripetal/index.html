Centripetal Force Calculator

Anything moving in a circle is constantly accelerating toward the centre, and something must supply that force: friction for a cornering car, gravity for a planet, the hammer wire for an athlete. Stop supplying it and the object flies off on a straight line.

Acceleration a = v² ÷ r · Force F = m × v² ÷ r

Worked example

Worked example: 2 kg moving at 10 m/s around a 5 m circle needs a steady 40 N toward the centre — and experiences 20 m/s², about 2 g.
