Insulation R-Value Calculator

Insulation is sold by resistance: R-value adds up layer by layer, and each material resists heat flow at its own rate per inch. Pick the material, enter the thickness you have (or can fit), and see the total — plus what a target R would demand.

Total R = R-per-inch of the material × thickness in inches

Worked example

Worked example: 2 inches of XPS foam board at R-5 per inch gives R-10. Reaching R-30 with it would take 6 inches — which is why attics use cheap, deep fibreglass instead.
