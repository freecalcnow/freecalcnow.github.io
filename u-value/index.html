U-Value Calculator

R-values add; the U-value — how fast heat leaks through each square metre per degree — is their reciprocal, with the inside and outside surface films included. Enter each layer's R (in m²K/W), then an area and temperature difference for the actual watts escaping.

U = 1 ÷ (R_inside film 0.13 + Σ layer R + R_outside film 0.04) · Heat loss = U × area × ΔT

Worked example

Worked example: layers of 0.5 + 2.5 + 0.8 = R 3.8, plus films → R 3.97 → U = 0.25 W/m²K. Over 15 m² at ΔT 20 K that leaks 76 W.
