Roof Snow Load Calculator

Building codes start from the ground snow load and adjust it for the roof: exposure to wind, the building's warmth melting the underside, importance, and slope shedding its load. This tool runs the standard ASCE 7 flat-roof and sloped-roof reductions so you can see the design number engineers use.

Flat roof: Pf = 0.7 × Ce × Ct × I × Pg · Sloped: Ps = Cs × Pf · Cs falls as slope rises (warm, slippery roofs shed sooner) · Minimums apply in low-load regions

Worked example

Worked example: a ground snow load of 30 psf, with standard exposure and thermal factors of 1.0, gives a flat-roof load of 0.7 × 30 = 21 psf. On a warm 30° roof (Cs ≈ 0.62) the design load falls to about 12.9 psf — slope is structural relief.
