Body Fat % Calculator

Body fat percentage from a tape measure, using the US Navy method — the standard field estimate that pairs with your BMI and calorie numbers to give a fuller picture than weight alone. This is an estimate, not a medical measurement.

Men: 86.010 × log₁₀(waist − neck) − 70.041 × log₁₀(height) + 36.76 · Women: 163.205 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log₁₀(height) − 78.387 (measurements in inches)

Worked example

Worked example: a man, 175 cm tall, 38 cm neck, 86 cm waist → about 17.8% body fat — at the top of the “fitness” range for men on the standard ACE bands (athletes 6–13%, fitness 14–17%, average 18–24%, obese 25%+). Those exact measurements are pre-filled below.

Frequently asked questions

Why use body fat % as well as BMI?

BMI cannot tell muscle from fat, so it mislabels lean athletes and misses people at a “healthy” weight with high fat. Body fat % answers the question BMI cannot — which is why the two tools sit side by side here.

Where exactly do I measure?

Neck: just below the Adam’s apple, tape sloping slightly down. Waist: horizontal at the navel, at the end of a normal breath out. Hips (women): the widest point. Keep the tape level and snug.
