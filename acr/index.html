Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (ACR) Calculator

ACR corrects urine albumin for how concentrated the urine is, by dividing by creatinine — so a single spot sample does the job of a 24-hour collection. It stages albuminuria into A1 (normal to mildly increased), A2 (moderately increased — the old 'microalbuminuria') and A3 (severely increased).

ACR = urine albumin (mg) ÷ urine creatinine (g) — reported as mg/g (or mg/mmol in SI)

Worked example

Worked example: albumin 45 mg/L with creatinine 1.0 g/L → ACR 45 mg/g — category A2, moderately increased.
