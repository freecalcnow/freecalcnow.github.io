Time Zone Offset Difference Calculator

Every time zone is an offset from UTC. Enter two offsets (for example +5:30 and −4) to get the exact gap, plus a converter: pick a time in the first zone and see it in the second. Daylight saving is why offsets, not zone names, are the honest input.

Difference = offset A − offset B · Converted time = time − offset A + offset B
