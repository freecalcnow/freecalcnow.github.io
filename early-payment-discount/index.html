Early Payment Discount Calculator

Terms like '2/10 net 30' offer 2% off for paying 20 days early. Sounds tiny — until you annualise it: skipping that discount is like borrowing at about 37% a year. This does that maths for any terms, on any invoice.

Annualised cost = [discount % ÷ (100 − discount %)] × [365 ÷ (net days − discount days)] × 100

Worked example

Worked example: 2/10 net 30 on a $10,000 invoice — pay $9,800 on day 10 or $10,000 on day 30. The $200 you save by paying early equals borrowing at 37.24% a year. Almost any business loan is cheaper than skipping it.
