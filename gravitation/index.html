Newton's Gravitation Calculator

Every two masses in the universe pull on each other with a force that weakens with the square of the distance. It is the weakest force in nature — two people standing a metre apart attract each other by less than a ten-millionth of a newton — yet it runs the solar system, because planets are so heavy.

F = G × m₁ × m₂ ÷ r² · G = 6.674×10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²
