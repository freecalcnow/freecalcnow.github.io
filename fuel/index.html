Fuel Cost Calculator

What a journey actually costs in fuel — for road trips, commutes and delivery rounds. Miles and gallons, or kilometres and litres, with the cost per 100 miles/km so trips can be compared fairly.

Fuel used = distance ÷ mpg (US) or distance × litres-per-100 km ÷ 100 (metric) · Cost = fuel used × price per unit

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my car’s real efficiency?

Most cars show it on the trip computer; otherwise fill the tank, reset the trip meter, and divide the distance by the fuel needed to refill. That measured figure beats the brochure number every time.

How do I split a trip’s fuel cost?

Divide the total by the number of people sharing — the tip & split calculator handles the general case, including food stops.
