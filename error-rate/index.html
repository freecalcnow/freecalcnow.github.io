Error Rate Calculator

Raw error counts mean nothing without the denominator. This tool turns errors and total requests into the rate every dashboard, SLA and post-mortem actually argues about.

Error rate = errors ÷ total × 100 · Success rate = 100 − error rate

Worked example: 45 errors in 12,000 requests → an error rate of 0.375%, a success rate of 99.625% — about 1 error in every 267 requests.
