Empirical Rule Calculator (68-95-99.7)

For bell-shaped data, the empirical rule fixes three landmarks: about 68% of values within one standard deviation of the mean, 95% within two, 99.7% within three. Enter a mean and sd to see the actual ranges on your scale — and the exact percentages, not just the rounded rule.

Within k sd: Φ(k) − Φ(−k) — 68.27%, 95.45%, 99.73% for k = 1, 2, 3
