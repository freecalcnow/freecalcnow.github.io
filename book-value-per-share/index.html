Book Value Per Share Calculator

Book value per share is what each share would claim if the company sold its assets at their balance-sheet values and paid every debt. Value investors compare it with the market price (the price-to-book ratio) to spot shares trading below their accounting worth.

BVPS = (total equity − preferred equity) ÷ shares outstanding

Worked example

Worked example: equity of $5,000,000 with $1,000,000 preferred and 2,000,000 shares → (5,000,000 − 1,000,000) ÷ 2,000,000 = $2.00 per share.
