True Shooting % Calculator

Field-goal percentage lies: it ignores free throws entirely and counts a three the same as a two. True shooting fixes both, pricing every scoring possession — the league-average TS% hovers around 57–58%, and 65% is elite.

TS% = points ÷ (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)) · eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) ÷ FGA

Worked example

Worked example: 28 points on 18 shots and 8 free-throw attempts → TS = 28 ÷ (2 × 21.52) = 65.1%. With 10 makes including 3 threes, eFG = 63.9%.
