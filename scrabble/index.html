Scrabble Score Calculator

Every Scrabble tile carries its value on its face — common letters cheap, Q and Z the 10-point treasures. Enter a word for its raw tile total and the per-letter breakdown. Board bonuses are yours to add: they belong to the squares, not the word.

Score = Σ tile values · 1: E A I O N R T L S U · 2: D G · 3: B C M P · 4: F H V W Y · 5: K · 8: J X · 10: Q Z
