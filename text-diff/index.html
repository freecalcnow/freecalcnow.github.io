Text Difference Checker

Paste the old version and the new version and see the edit at a glance: lines only in the first, lines only in the second, and the word-count change. It is the quick pre-flight check before sending a revised document, contract or post.

Lines are compared exactly (after trimming) · Word counts and similarity use unique and total word sets · Similarity = shared lines ÷ all distinct lines (Jaccard)

Worked example

Worked example: a 40-line draft revised to 44 lines, with 6 lines removed and 10 added, shows 34 unchanged lines and a similarity of about 74% — a real revision. A similarity of 99% means someone changed a comma and called it a new draft.
