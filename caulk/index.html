Caulk Tube Calculator

A standard 10.1 fl oz tube holds about 18.2 cubic inches of sealant. Divide by the bead's cross-section and you get the honest coverage per tube — which bead size changes by a factor of sixteen.

Coverage per tube = tube volume (18.2 in³) ÷ bead cross-section (bead width²) · Tubes = total length ÷ coverage

Worked example

Worked example: a ¼ in bead → about 24.3 ft per tube, so 100 ft of joints needs 5 tubes (4.1 exactly). A ⅛ in bead would stretch one tube to about 97 ft.
