Free-Space Path Loss Calculator

Even in empty space, a radio signal thins as its wavefront expands — the loss grows 6 dB every time the distance doubles, and 6 dB every time the frequency doubles. Every Wi-Fi, satellite and radio link budget starts with this number.

FSPL (dB) = 20 log₁₀(distance km) + 20 log₁₀(frequency MHz) + 32.44
