Factorial Calculator

A factorial is a number multiplied by every whole number below it: 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120. Factorials count arrangements — 10! is the number of ways to order just 10 books, which is why the numbers explode so fast.

n! = n × (n − 1) × (n − 2) × … × 1, with 0! = 1 by definition

Worked example: 10! = 3,628,800. That is also the number of seconds in exactly 6 weeks — a coincidence worth remembering.
