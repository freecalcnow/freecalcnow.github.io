Heart Rate Recovery Calculator

Stop exercising and a fit heart brakes fast. The drop in the first minute — heart rate recovery — is used in exercise testing worldwide: bigger drops track with better fitness and better long-term heart health.

Recovery = heart rate at the end of exercise − heart rate exactly 1 minute later (keep moving slowly, don't stop dead)

Worked example

Worked example: finishing at 152 bpm and reading 128 a minute later → a 24 bpm drop — a good result. A drop under 12 bpm is the level exercise labs flag as abnormal.
