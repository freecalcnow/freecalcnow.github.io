True APR Calculator (with fees)

A loan’s headline rate ignores the fees taken out before you see the money. Enter the loan, the upfront fees and the actual monthly payment, and this solves for the true APR — the rate that makes the payment match the money you really received.

You receive: loan − fees · APR solves: amount received = payment × [1 − (1 + i)^−n] ÷ i, APR = i × 12 (found numerically)

Worked example

Worked example: borrow $10,000, pay $879.16 a month for 12 months and the APR is exactly 10.00% with no fees. Add $300 of upfront fees — you only receive $9,700 — and the same payments mean a true APR of about 15.9%.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
