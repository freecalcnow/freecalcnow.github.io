Fertile Window Calculator

Pregnancy is possible on about six days in each cycle: the five days before ovulation (sperm can wait) and the day of ovulation itself. This finds those days from your last period and your usual cycle length.

Ovulation ≈ first day of last period + cycle length − 14 · Fertile window = 5 days before ovulation to 1 day after
