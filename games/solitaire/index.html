How to play Solitaire

Clear the deck: build the four foundation piles from Ace to King.

  1. Tap a card, then tap where it should go — or drag it.
  2. Build tableau piles down in alternating colours; only Kings can fill an empty column.
  3. Tap the deck to deal a new card when you are stuck.
