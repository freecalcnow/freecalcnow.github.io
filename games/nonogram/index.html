How to play Nonogram

Paint cells by the number clues to reveal the hidden picture!

  1. The numbers tell you how many painted cells are in each row and column.
  2. Tap a cell to paint it, tap again to mark it empty.
  3. Start with the biggest numbers — they are the easiest clues.
