How to play Space Invaders

Blast the descending invaders before they land.

  1. Move with the arrow keys and shoot with Space (or the on-screen buttons).
  2. Invaders speed up as their numbers fall — stay calm and keep firing.
  3. Hide behind the shields, but remember they wear away.
