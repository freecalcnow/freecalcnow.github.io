How to play 2048

Slide the tiles and merge matching numbers to reach 2048.

  1. Use the arrow keys, swipe, or the on-screen arrows.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one when they slide together.
  3. Keep your biggest tile in one corner and build around it.
