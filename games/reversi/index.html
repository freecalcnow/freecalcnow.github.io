How to play Reversi (Othello)

Flip discs so that most of the board is your colour at the end.

  1. Tap an empty square — you must flip at least one enemy disc.
  2. Corners are gold: a disc in a corner can never be flipped.
  3. Take your time — a big early lead often flips away.
