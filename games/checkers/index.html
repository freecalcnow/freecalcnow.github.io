How to play Checkers

Capture all of the computer's pieces — or block it completely.

  1. Tap one of your pieces, then tap a highlighted square to move.
  2. Jumps are forced: if you can capture, you must. Chain multi-jumps!
  3. Reach the far side to crown a king — kings move both ways.
