How to play Snake

Steer the snake, eat strawberries, and grow as long as you can.

  1. Move: tap the big arrow buttons, swipe on the board, or use your keyboard arrow keys. The snake starts slow and speeds up a little every time you eat.
  2. Eat: every strawberry is +1 point and the snake grows one segment longer.
  3. Survive: hitting a wall or your own body ends the game. Longer snake, tighter turns!
  4. Score: your best score is saved on this device. Beat it — and beat the bot players below.
