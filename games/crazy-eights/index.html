How to play Crazy Eights

Match the suit or rank — eights are wild! First to shed all cards wins.

  1. Tap a card that matches the suit or the rank on the pile.
  2. Play an 8 to change the suit to whatever helps you most.
  3. Keep an 8 for the endgame.
