How to play Match 3 Gems

Swap gems to match 3 or more in a row and clear them.

  1. Tap a gem, then tap a neighbour to swap them.
  2. Matches of 4 or 5 make special gems that clear whole rows.
  3. Plan cascades — one swap can set off a chain.
