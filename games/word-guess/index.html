How to play Word Guess

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries.

  1. Type letters on your keyboard or tap the on-screen keys.
  2. Green means the letter is right and in the right spot, amber means it is in the word but in the wrong spot, grey means it is not in the word.
  3. Start with a word full of common vowels and consonants.
