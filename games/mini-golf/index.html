How to play Mini Golf

Putt through 9 or 18 holes. Beat par, hole by hole!

  1. Drag to aim, release to putt. The longer the drag, the harder the hit.
  2. Watch out for walls and water — they cost you strokes.
  3. Gentle putts beat wild ones near the hole.
