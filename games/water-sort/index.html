How to play Water Sort

Pour coloured water between tubes until each tube holds one colour!

  1. Tap a tube, then tap another tube to pour into it.
  2. You can only pour onto the same colour or an empty tube.
  3. Plan ahead — a wrong pour can block the whole puzzle.
