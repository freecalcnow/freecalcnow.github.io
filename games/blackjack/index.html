How to play Blackjack

Beat the dealer without going over 21.

  1. Tap Hit to take a card, Stand to keep your hand, or Double to double down.
  2. Face cards are worth 10, Aces are 1 or 11 — whichever helps you.
  3. The dealer must hit on 16 and stand on 17.
