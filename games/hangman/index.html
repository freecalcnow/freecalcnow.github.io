How to play Hangman

Guess the hidden word letter by letter before the drawing is finished.

  1. Tap letters to guess them. Right letters appear in the word.
  2. Every wrong guess adds a piece to the drawing — too many and you lose.
  3. Vowels first is usually a safe start.
