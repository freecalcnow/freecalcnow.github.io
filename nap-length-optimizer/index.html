Nap Length Calculator

Nap badly and you wake in deep sleep, groggier than before. Nap 20 minutes (light sleep only) or 90 minutes (one full cycle) and you wake clear. Enter when the nap starts — or when you must wake — for both options.

Power nap: wake after ~20 minutes, before deep sleep · Full-cycle nap: ~90 minutes = one complete sleep cycle · Avoid waking at 40–70 minutes (deep-sleep zone)
