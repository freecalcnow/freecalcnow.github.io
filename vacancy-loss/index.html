Rental Vacancy Loss Calculator

A rental is not let 52 weeks a year. Even a short gap between tenants, plus re-letting costs, quietly takes a slice of the rent roll. This prices the gap honestly.

Annual vacancy loss = monthly rent × 12 × vacancy % · Effective rent = gross rent − vacancy loss − re-letting costs
