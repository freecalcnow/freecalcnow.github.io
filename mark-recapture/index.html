Mark–Recapture Population Calculator

You cannot count every fish in a lake — but you can catch 50, mark them, release them, then catch 40 more and see how many carry the mark. If 10 do, marked animals are a quarter of the second catch, so the first 50 were probably a quarter of the lake: about 200 fish. This runs that estimate with its working shown.

Population N = (marked & released × second catch) ÷ recaptured with marks

Worked example

Worked example: 50 marked and released, a second catch of 40 containing 10 marked → N = 50 × 40 ÷ 10 = 200 animals.
