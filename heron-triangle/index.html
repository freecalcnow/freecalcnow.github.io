Triangle Area by Heron's Formula Calculator

Know all three sides but no height? Heron's formula (nearly 2,000 years old) gives the area from the sides alone. This also checks the triangle inequality — not every three lengths can form a triangle — and finds the inradius and circumradius.

s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2 · Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]
