Heart Rate Reserve Calculator

Two people the same age can have very different hearts. The Karvonen method respects that: it works from your heart rate reserve — the gap between resting and maximum — so a fit heart's zones sit higher than a beginner's at the same age.

Max HR ≈ 208 − 0.7 × age (Tanaka) · Target = resting + intensity × (max − resting)
