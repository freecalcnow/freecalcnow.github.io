Solar Panels Needed Calculator

Solar sizing starts from your annual consumption and local sun, not from roof area. This estimates the system size and panel count, then checks what your roof can actually hold.

System kW = yearly kWh ÷ (sun hours × 365 × 0.8 system factor) · Panels = system watts ÷ panel watts
