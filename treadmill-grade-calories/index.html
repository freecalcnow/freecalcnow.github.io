Treadmill Incline Calories Calculator

Incline transforms walking: at a 12% grade the same speed costs more than double. This uses the ACSM walking equation — the one behind the 12-3-30 workout's fame — for any speed and grade.

VO₂ = 0.1×speed(m/min) + 1.8×speed×grade + 3.5 · Calories = VO₂ × weight × minutes ÷ 200
