Business Ratios Calculator

Lenders and buyers read a business the way doctors read bloodwork: a handful of ratios, each answering one question. Can it pay next month's bills (liquidity)? How much is borrowed (leverage)? How hard does each dollar work (profitability)? Enter the balance-sheet numbers and read all of them at once.

Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities · Quick ratio excludes inventory · Debt/equity = total debt ÷ equity · ROE = net income ÷ equity

Worked example

Worked example: $150,000 of current assets (including $40,000 inventory) against $75,000 of current liabilities → current ratio 2.00, quick ratio 1.47. Debt of $200,000 on $300,000 equity is 0.67; $60,000 profit on $500,000 revenue is a 12% margin and a 20% ROE.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
