True Cost Per Mile Calculator

Drivers feel the fuel cost and forget the rest. Depreciation alone is usually the biggest cost of a car, silently ticking away in the driveway. This divides the whole honest total — fuel, insurance, upkeep, depreciation, fees — by your real mileage.

Cost per mile = (fuel + insurance + maintenance + depreciation + fees for the year) ÷ miles per year

Worked example

Worked example: 12,000 miles a year at 35 mpg and $3.60 a gallon, with $1,400 insurance, $800 upkeep, $2,500 depreciation and $200 of fees → about $0.51 a mile, $511 a month.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
