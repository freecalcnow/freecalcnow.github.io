Power Factor Calculator

Motors and other inductive loads draw current that sloshes back unused — billed as apparent power, wasted as heat in cables. The power factor is the fraction doing real work. This computes it from kW and kVA, and sizes the capacitor bank that corrects it to your target.

PF = kW ÷ kVA · correction kVAr = kW × (tan φ₁ − tan φ₂)

Worked example

Worked example: 100 kW at PF 0.8 (125 kVA) corrected to 0.95 needs about 42 kVAr of capacitance, and frees the supply down to 105 kVA.
