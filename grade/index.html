Test Grade Calculator

Enter the points you earned and the points possible, and get the percentage, the letter grade on the standard scale, and the grade points it is worth — plus exactly how many more points the next letter up would have needed.

Percentage = points earned ÷ points possible × 100 · Letters: A ≥ 93, A− ≥ 90, B+ ≥ 87, B ≥ 83, B− ≥ 80, C+ ≥ 77, C ≥ 73, C− ≥ 70, D+ ≥ 67, D ≥ 63, D− ≥ 60, F below

Worked example

Worked example: 86 out of 100 is 86% — a B, worth 3.0 grade points. One more point (87) would have made it a B+.

How to use

  1. Type your numbers in the boxes above.
  2. Tap Calculate — or just type, the answer updates as you go.
  3. Read the big answer and the breakdown table below it.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
