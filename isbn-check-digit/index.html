ISBN Check Digit Calculator

Every ISBN ends in a check digit calculated from the rest, so a single mistyped digit breaks it. Paste a full ISBN to validate it, or the first 9 (ISBN-10) or 12 (ISBN-13) digits to compute the check digit that completes it.

ISBN-13: check = (10 − (alternating 1,3 weighted sum mod 10)) mod 10 · ISBN-10: weighted sum mod 11, X = 10
