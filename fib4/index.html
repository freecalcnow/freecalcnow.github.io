FIB-4 Calculator (Liver Fibrosis)

FIB-4 estimates liver fibrosis from bloods already in the chart, no scan needed. Below 1.3, advanced fibrosis is unlikely; above 2.67 it is likely enough to refer; between them is the grey zone where a scan (elastography) settles it. Cut-offs shift a little over age 65.

FIB-4 = (age × AST) ÷ (platelets × √ALT)

Worked example

Worked example: age 55, AST 45, ALT 30, platelets 200 → 55 × 45 ÷ (200 × 5.48) = 2.26 — the indeterminate band.
