Modulo Calculator

Modulo is the remainder after division — the arithmetic of clocks (14:00 is 2 PM because 14 mod 12 = 2), calendars and every 'every Nth' pattern. This tool uses floored division, the mathematical convention, so negative numbers behave the way mathematicians expect.

a mod b = a − b × floor(a ÷ b) — the remainder always takes the sign of the divisor

Worked example

Worked example: 17 mod 5 = 2, because 17 = 5 × 3 + 2. On a clock, 17 mod 12 = 5 — five hours past noon.
