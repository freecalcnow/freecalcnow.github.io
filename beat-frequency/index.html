Beat Frequency Calculator

Play two tones of slightly different pitch together and the sound swells and fades at the difference between them. That pulsing — the beat frequency — is how tuners set temperaments by ear and how heterodyne radios shift signals: simple subtraction, endlessly useful.

Beat frequency = |f₁ − f₂| · The combined tone is heard at the average pitch (f₁ + f₂) ÷ 2, pulsing at the beat rate

Worked example

Worked example: a 440 Hz tuning fork against a piano string at 443 Hz beats 3 times a second — the tuner loosens the string until the beats stretch out and vanish. Two guitar strings at 82.4 and 84 Hz beat 1.6 times a second: clearly audible, clearly out.
