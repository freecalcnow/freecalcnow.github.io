Frequently asked questions

Where do the rates come from?

Live rates from free public exchange-rate services (ExchangeRate-API, with Frankfurter/ECB as a backup). No rate is ever typed in by hand, and the date of the rate is always shown.

Why does my bank give a different number?

Banks and exchange services add their own margin and fees on top of the mid-market rate shown here. The rate here is the honest middle — what you actually get will be a little less.

What if the rates will not load?

If you are offline or the rate service is down, the page says so plainly instead of guessing a rate. Check your connection and try again.
