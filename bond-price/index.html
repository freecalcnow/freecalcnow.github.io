Bond Price Calculator

A bond's price is just its future payments discounted at today's market yield. When yields rise above the coupon, the price falls below face value — this tool prices it exactly, payment by payment.

Price = coupon payment × [1 − (1 + y/m)^−n] ÷ (y/m) + face ÷ (1 + y/m)^n, with m payments a year

Worked example

Worked example: a $1,000 bond paying 5% twice a year for 10 years, at a 6% market yield → $925.61. The $74.39 discount is the market charging you for the below-market coupon.
