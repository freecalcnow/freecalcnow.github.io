Paper Size Converter

A4 is not Letter, and printing one on the other crops or shrinks your page. Pick a paper size and see its exact dimensions in mm and inches, plus the pixel size needed at print quality.

A-series: each size halves the previous (A0 = 841×1189 mm). Pixels = inches × DPI (300 DPI is print quality).
