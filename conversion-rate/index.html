Conversion Rate Calculator

Conversion rate is the multiplier on everything else: double it and every ad, post and search ranking earns twice as much. This tool works out the rate, the value of each visitor, and the prize for lifting the rate by a point.

Conversion rate = conversions ÷ visitors × 100 · Revenue per visitor = total revenue ÷ visitors

Worked example

Worked example: 40,000 visitors producing 1,100 orders is a 2.75% conversion rate. At a $65 average order that is $1.79 of revenue per visitor — and lifting the rate to 3.75% would add about $26,000 a month on the same traffic.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
