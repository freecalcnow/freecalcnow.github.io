3×3 Matrix Calculator

A 3×3 matrix packages nine numbers into one machine that stretches and rotates space. Its determinant says whether the machine flattens everything (zero — no inverse exists) and its inverse is the undo button. Enter the nine entries and get both, plus the trace.

det = a(ei − fh) − b(di − fg) + c(dh − eg) for rows [a b c / d e f / g h i] · Inverse = adjugate ÷ determinant
