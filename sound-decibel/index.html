Sound Level (Decibel) Calculator

Decibels are a logarithm wearing a friendly face: every +10 dB is ten times the intensity, which your ears hear as roughly twice as loud. A whisper is near 30 dB, conversation 60, and 120 is where pain starts — this converts the raw physics numbers into the scale everyone quotes.

Intensity level = 10 × log₁₀(I ÷ 10⁻¹²) · Pressure level = 20 × log₁₀(p ÷ 20 µPa)
