Half-Life & Decay Calculator

Every half-life that passes halves what remains: after 3 half-lives, ⅛ is left. Enter the half-life and elapsed time for the surviving fraction — the same maths runs carbon dating, nuclear waste and how long a medicine stays in your system.

Remaining = start × (½)^(time ÷ half-life) · Time to reach a level = half-life × log₂(start ÷ level)
