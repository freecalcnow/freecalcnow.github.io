Roulette Odds & Payout Calculator

Roulette pays as if the wheel had fewer pockets than it does — that gap is the house edge. Pick a bet and wheel type to see the true odds, the payout, and the edge on your stake.

Win % = winning pockets ÷ total pockets · Fair payout = (total − winning) ÷ winning · House edge = 1 − (payout+1) × win %
