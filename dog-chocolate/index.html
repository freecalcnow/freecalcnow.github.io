Dog Chocolate Toxicity Calculator

Chocolate's danger to dogs is dose maths: theobromine per gram of chocolate, times grams eaten, divided by the dog's weight. Dark chocolate carries ten times the theobromine of milk. Enter what was eaten and the dog's size for the exposure band vets use.

Theobromine dose (mg/kg) = grams eaten × theobromine per gram ÷ dog's weight in kg · Milk ≈ 2.3 mg/g · Dark (50–60%) ≈ 5.3 · Baking/cocoa ≈ 14–16 · Under 20 mg/kg mild · 40–50 seizures possible · 60+ life-threatening

Worked example

Worked example: a 10 kg dog that eats 50 g of dark chocolate takes in about 265 mg of theobromine — 26.5 mg/kg, inside the band where vomiting, restlessness and a racing heart are expected. The same 50 g of milk chocolate is about 11.5 mg/kg — usually a stomach upset only. Either way, call the vet.
