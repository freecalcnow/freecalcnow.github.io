DNA GC Content Calculator

Paste a DNA sequence (A, T, G, C — spaces and line breaks fine) for its GC content: the share of bases that are guanine or cytosine. GC content predicts melting temperature and varies systematically between species and genome regions.

GC% = (G + C) ÷ (A + T + G + C) × 100 · Rough Tm for short primers ≈ 2×(A+T) + 4×(G+C) °C
