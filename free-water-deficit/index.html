Free Water Deficit Calculator

A high sodium means the body's water is diluted too little, not that it holds too much salt. The free water deficit estimates the litres of pure water needed to bring sodium back to 140 — using total body water (about 60% of weight in men, 50% in women, less in older patients). Correction must be slow; the number guides, the protocol rules.

Deficit = total body water × (measured Na ÷ 140 − 1)

Worked example

Worked example: a 70 kg man with sodium 155 → TBW 42 L, deficit = 42 × (155/140 − 1) = 4.5 litres.
