Critical Angle Calculator

Light leaving a dense material bends away from the normal — until, past the critical angle, it cannot leave at all and reflects perfectly inside. That total internal reflection carries every fibre-optic signal on Earth. This finds the angle for any pair of materials.

Critical angle θc = arcsin(n₂ ÷ n₁) — only exists when n₁ > n₂

Worked example

Worked example: glass (n = 1.50) to air (n = 1.00) → θc = arcsin(1 ÷ 1.5) ≈ 41.8°. Water (1.33) to air gives about 48.8°.
