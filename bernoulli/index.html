Bernoulli Equation Calculator

Bernoulli's principle is energy bookkeeping for a moving fluid: pressure, speed and height trade against each other along a streamline, and the total never changes. It is why shower curtains drift inward and how aeroplane wings and carburettors work. Enter conditions at two points and solve for the unknown pressure.

P₁ + ½ρv₁² + ρgh₁ = P₂ + ½ρv₂² + ρgh₂ → P₂ = P₁ + ½ρ(v₁² − v₂²) + ρg(h₁ − h₂)

Worked example

Worked example: water (ρ = 1,000) at 200,000 Pa moving at 2 m/s, 10 m up, speeds up to 5 m/s at 8 m height → the speed costs 10,500 Pa but the 2 m drop gains 19,620 Pa, so P₂ = 209,120 Pa.
