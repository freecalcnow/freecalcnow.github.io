Equilibrium Constant (Kc) Calculator

At equilibrium the ratio of products to reactants (each raised to its equation coefficient) settles to a constant, Kc. Big Kc: the reaction essentially finishes. Small Kc: it barely starts. Enter the balanced equation's coefficients and the equilibrium concentrations to get Kc and its verdict.

For aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD: Kc = ([C]^c × [D]^d) ÷ ([A]^a × [B]^b)

Worked example

Worked example: all coefficients 1, with [A] = [B] = 0.50 mol/L and [C] = 0.80, [D] = 0.20 → Kc = (0.80 × 0.20) ÷ (0.50 × 0.50) = 0.64 — a mixture with reactants somewhat favoured.
