Roof Area Calculator

A roof is bigger than the house under it — the slope stretches it, and overhangs stretch it further. Builders order material by the true sloped area (and by 'squares' of 100 ft²). This converts footprint and pitch into both, with an allowance for overhangs and waste.

Plan area with overhangs = (length + 2 × overhang) × (width + 2 × overhang) · Roof area = plan area ÷ cos(pitch)

Worked example

Worked example: a 12 × 9 m house at 30° pitch with 0.5 m overhangs → plan area 13 × 10 = 130 m², roof area about 150.1 m² — about 16.2 roofing squares.
