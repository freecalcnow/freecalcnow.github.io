Circular Segment Calculator

A segment is what's left when a chord cuts a circle — the shape of a part-filled pipe, an arched window, a lens blank. Give the radius and the central angle and this finds the segment's area, the chord, the arc and the sagitta (the segment's height).

Area = ½ r² (θ − sin θ), θ in radians · Chord = 2r sin(θ/2) · Sagitta = r (1 − cos(θ/2))
