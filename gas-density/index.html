Gas Density Calculator

Rearrange the ideal gas law and density falls out: ρ = PM ÷ RT. It explains why helium balloons rise (0.17 g/L against air's ~1.19), why CO₂ pools in cellars (1.84 g/L), and how hot-air balloons float — heating drops the density inside the envelope.

ρ = P × M ÷ (R × T), R = 0.082057 L·atm/(mol·K)
