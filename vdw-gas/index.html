Van der Waals Gas Calculator

PV = nRT pretends molecules are massless points that ignore each other. Real gases deviate — molecules take up room (the b constant) and attract each other (the a constant). Van der Waals' equation prices both corrections in, and this shows the real pressure beside the ideal one so the size of the lie is visible.

P = nRT ÷ (V − nb) − a n² ÷ V² · R = 0.082057 L·atm/mol·K

Worked example

Worked example: 1 mol of CO₂ in 0.5 L at 300 K → van der Waals pressure about 39.3 atm against an ideal 49.2 atm — attractions win at this density, and the compressibility factor Z is about 0.80.
