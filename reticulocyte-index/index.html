Reticulocyte Production Index Calculator

A raw reticulocyte percentage flatters a failing marrow twice: anaemia inflates the percentage (fewer red cells to divide by) and stress pushes reticulocytes out early, so they linger longer in the blood. The production index corrects both distortions — above 2 means the marrow is responding; below 2 in an anaemic patient means it is not.

Corrected retic = retic % × (patient haematocrit ÷ 45) · RPI = corrected retic ÷ maturation factor (Hct 40–45: 1 · 35–39: 1.5 · 25–34: 2 · 15–24: 2.5 · under 15: 3)

Worked example

Worked example: a reticulocyte count of 4% with a haematocrit of 25% corrects to 4 × (25 ÷ 45) = 2.2%, and dividing by the maturation factor of 2 gives an RPI of 1.1 — an inadequate marrow response despite the 'high' raw number. The anaemia is a production problem, not blood loss.
