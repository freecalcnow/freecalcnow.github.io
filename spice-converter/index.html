Spice Converter

A teaspoon of salt weighs about 6 grams; a teaspoon of turmeric about 3. Volume-to-weight conversion depends entirely on the spice — this uses the standard reference density for each one and converts between spoons and grams both ways.

Grams = teaspoons × grams-per-teaspoon for that spice · 1 tablespoon = 3 teaspoons · 1 oz = 28.35 g

Worked example

Worked example: 1 tablespoon of ground cinnamon = 3 tsp × 2.6 g ≈ 7.8 g. A tablespoon of table salt is much heavier — about 18 g.
