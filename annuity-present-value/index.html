Present Value of an Annuity Calculator

A promise of 1,000 a month for 20 years is not worth 240,000 today — money arriving later is worth less. This discounts each payment back to today and adds them up: the fair lump-sum value of the stream.

PV = payment × [1 − (1 + r) ^ −n] ÷ r · r and n in the payment period
