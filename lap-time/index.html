Lap Time Calculator

Coaches deal in laps; watches deal in totals. Enter the total time and lap count (and the lap distance, for pace and speed) to get the average lap — the number to chase on the next repetition.

Average lap = total time ÷ laps · Pace = lap time ÷ lap distance

Worked example

Worked example: 45:30 for 10 laps of a 400 m track → 4:33 per lap, an 11:23-per-km pace — an easy jog. A 60-second lap, by contrast, is 2:30/km: championship territory.
