Light Bulb Savings Calculator

An old 60 W bulb and the 9 W LED that replaces it give the same light; the difference is pure waste heat. Over a year of evenings that gap is real money — this tool prices both bulbs at your electricity rate and shows the saving per bulb, per year.

Yearly kWh = watts × hours/day × 365 ÷ 1000 · Cost = kWh × rate

Worked example

Worked example: 60 W vs 9 W, 5 hours a day at $0.17/kWh → $18.62 vs $2.79 a year: $15.82 saved per bulb.
