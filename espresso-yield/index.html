Espresso Extraction Yield Calculator

Extraction yield is the percentage of the coffee's mass dissolved into the cup — the number behind 'under-extracted' (sour, under ~18%) and 'over-extracted' (bitter, over ~22%). It needs a refractometer reading (TDS) plus two weights.

Extraction yield % = (TDS % × beverage weight) ÷ dose × 100 · Brew ratio = beverage weight ÷ dose

Worked example

Worked example: an 18 g dose producing 36 g of beverage at 9% TDS → (9 × 36) ÷ 18 = 18% extraction yield — the low edge of the classic sweet spot. (Filter coffee reads far lower TDS, around 1.35%, but extracts to a similar yield because the beverage is so much larger.)
