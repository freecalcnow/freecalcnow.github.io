Antoine Equation Calculator

Where Clausius–Clapeyron approximates, the Antoine equation fits: each liquid carries three empirical constants that reproduce measured vapour pressures closely across a stated range. Constants for water, ethanol and benzene are built in — the form chemical engineers reach for first.

log₁₀ P = A − B ÷ (C + T), P in mmHg, T in °C

Worked example

Worked example: water at 90°C with A = 8.14019, B = 1810.94, C = 244.485 → log₁₀P = 2.72, P ≈ 525 mmHg — matching the measured 526 mmHg.
