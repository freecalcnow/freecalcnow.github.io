Mortgage Recast Calculator

A recast (re-amortisation) is the quiet alternative to refinancing: you hand the lender a lump sum, they re-spread the smaller balance over the months you have left, and the payment falls — same rate, same end date, usually a small fee instead of full closing costs.

New payment = the standard amortising payment on (balance − lump sum) over the months remaining, at the same rate
