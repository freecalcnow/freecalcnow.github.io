Floor Screed Calculator

Screed is ordered by area but mixed by volume, and the cement hides inside a ratio. This converts area and thickness into wet volume, applies the standard dry-volume factor, and splits the mix into cement bags and sand at your chosen ratio.

Wet volume = area × thickness · Dry volume = wet × 1.54 · Cement = dry × (1 ÷ (1 + ratio)) × 1440 kg/m³

Worked example

Worked example: 20 m² at 50 mm in a 1:4 mix → 1.00 m³ wet, about 444 kg of cement (18 × 25 kg bags) and about 1.23 m³ of sand.
