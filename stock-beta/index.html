Beta Calculator (Stocks)

Beta is the slope of a stock's returns against the market's: 1.0 moves with the market, 1.5 swings half as much again, 0.5 half as much. Paste matching returns for the stock and its index (same periods, same order) and this finds beta, the correlation, and Jensen's alpha against the risk-free rate you enter.

Beta = covariance(stock, market) ÷ variance(market) · Alpha = mean stock − [risk-free + beta × (mean market − risk-free)]
