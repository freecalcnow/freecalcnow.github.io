Pomodoro Study Planner

The Pomodoro method alternates focused blocks (classically 25 minutes) with short breaks, and a long break every four blocks. Tell it your block lengths and how many blocks you need — it returns total study time, total elapsed time and the clock time you finish.

Total = (blocks × focus) + (short breaks × short) + (long breaks × long) · A long break replaces the short break after every 4th block

Worked example

Worked example: 8 blocks of 25 minutes with 5-minute breaks and a 15-minute long break → 200 minutes of focus, 45 minutes of breaks, 245 minutes (4 h 05) elapsed.
