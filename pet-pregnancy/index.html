Pet Pregnancy Calculator

Gestation lengths are remarkably consistent within a species: about 63 days for dogs, 65 for cats, 283 for cows, 340 for horses. Enter the mating (or breeding) date and species for the expected due date and the normal window around it.

Due date = mating date + the species' typical gestation
