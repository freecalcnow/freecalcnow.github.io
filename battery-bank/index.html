Solar Battery Sizing Calculator

Battery sizing starts from two honest numbers: how much energy you use in a day, and how many sunless days you want to survive. Divide by how deeply your battery type may be discharged, and you get the nameplate capacity to buy — plus the amp-hours at your system voltage, which is how batteries are actually sold.

Nameplate capacity = daily use × days of autonomy ÷ depth of discharge · Amp-hours = capacity (Wh) ÷ system voltage

Worked example

Worked example: 5 kWh a day, 2 days of autonomy, lithium batteries at 80% usable depth → 5 × 2 ÷ 0.8 = 12.5 kWh of nameplate storage — 260 Ah at 48 V, or a sobering 1,042 Ah at 12 V, which is why bigger systems use higher voltages.
