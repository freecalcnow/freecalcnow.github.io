Warm-Up Set Calculator (Barbell Ramp)

Warm-up sets have two jobs: rehearse the movement and arrive at the work weight with the nervous system awake and the muscles unfatigued. The classic ramp — empty bar, then roughly 40 / 60 / 80 / 90% — does both. Enter your top set for the full ladder with plates per side.

Ramp: bar only × 8–10 · 40% × 5 · 60% × 3–4 · 80% × 2 · 90% × 1 · then work sets · Round each step to loadable plates; keep the last jump small

Worked example

Worked example: working up to 100 kg: bar (20) × 10, 40 × 5, 60 × 3, 80 × 2, 90 × 1 — then the work sets. Total warm-up tonnage is small by design: if the ramp tires you, the jumps were too many or too heavy.
