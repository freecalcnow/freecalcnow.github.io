VAT / Sales Tax Calculator

Add VAT, GST or sales tax to a price — or strip it out of a price that already includes it. Works for any rate: UK VAT at 20%, EU rates, US state sales tax, GST in India, Canada, Australia and beyond.

Add: tax = amount × rate ÷ 100 · Remove: net = amount ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100), tax = amount − net

Worked example

Worked examples: add 20% VAT to $100 → tax $20, total $120. Remove 20% VAT from $120 → net $100, tax $20. The two directions use different maths — dividing by 1.2, not subtracting 20% — which is exactly the mistake this tool prevents.

Frequently asked questions

Which rate should I use?

Your local one: UK standard VAT 20%, Germany 19%, France 20%, India GST 18% on many goods, Australia GST 10%, US sales tax varies by state and city (0%–10%+). Reduced rates often apply to food, books and children’s items.

Is VAT the same as sales tax?

The maths here is identical; the systems differ. VAT/GST is charged at every stage of production with credits, sales tax only at the final sale — but adding or removing a percentage from a price works the same way.
