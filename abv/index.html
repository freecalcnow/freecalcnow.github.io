ABV Calculator (Homebrew)

Brewers measure sugar before and after fermentation with a hydrometer; the drop is the sugar the yeast turned into alcohol. The standard short formula converts that drop straight into alcohol by volume, and the same readings give attenuation — how much of the available sugar the yeast actually ate.

ABV % = (OG − FG) × 131.25 · Apparent attenuation = (OG − FG) ÷ (OG − 1) × 100

Worked example

Worked example: an original gravity of 1.050 finishing at 1.010 gives (0.040 × 131.25) = 5.25% ABV and 80% attenuation — a healthy, typical fermentation.
