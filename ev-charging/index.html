EV Charging Cost Calculator

An EV's fuel bill is set at home, on your electricity tariff, long before any public charger. This prices a charge between any two battery percentages, converts it to the cost per 100 miles that actually compares with petrol, and shows the petrol car's bill for the same distance.

Energy added = battery size × (charge to − charge from) % · Cost = energy × price · Per 100 miles = cost ÷ miles added × 100

Worked example

Worked example: a 60 kWh battery charged from 20% to 80% takes 36 kWh — $6.12 at $0.17/kWh. At 3.5 miles per kWh that is 126 miles of range, or $4.86 per 100 miles, against $10.29 for a 35 mpg petrol car at $3.60 a gallon.
