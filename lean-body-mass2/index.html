Lean Body Mass Calculator

Lean body mass is everything that is not fat: muscle, bone, organs, water. Drug dosing, protein targets and physique tracking all run off it. Three validated formulas are shown — they should agree within a couple of kilos.

Boer: men 0.407×W + 0.267×H − 19.2 · women 0.252×W + 0.473×H − 48.3 (W kg, H cm) · James and Hume formulas shown for cross-check
