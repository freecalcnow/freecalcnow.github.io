Paint Coverage Calculator

Paint maths goes wrong in two places: forgetting the ceiling-or-not decision, and forgetting that two coats means twice the paint. Measure the walls, subtract the openings, pick the coverage printed on your tin (10–12 m²/L is typical) and the litres — plus the tin sizes to buy — come out.

Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height − openings · Litres = area × coats ÷ coverage per litre
