Marathon Fuelling Calculator

Endurance performance runs on carbohydrate intake: the gut can absorb about 60 g an hour from one sugar source, up to ~90 g with mixed sugars. Enter your event length for a fuelling schedule that avoids the wall.

Target ≈ 45–60 g carbs/hr for events under 2.5 h · 60–90 g/hr beyond that (glucose+fructose mixes) · One gel ≈ 22–25 g carbs
