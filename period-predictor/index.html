Period & Cycle Predictor

Cycle maths runs from the first day of your last period: the next period is one cycle length away, and ovulation usually lands about 14 days before the period that follows it. This lays out the next two cycles, the ovulation estimate and the fertile window around it.

Next period = last period + cycle length · Ovulation ≈ next period − 14 days · Fertile window ≈ ovulation − 5 days to ovulation + 1 day
