CAGR Calculator

CAGR smooths a bumpy ride into one steady yearly rate: the rate that, applied every year, takes you exactly from the start value to the end value. It is how funds and businesses compare growth over different time spans fairly.

CAGR = (End ÷ Start)^(1 ÷ years) − 1

Worked example

Worked example: $10,000 growing to $25,000 in 8 years is a CAGR of 12.14% — even if some individual years were far better or worse than that.
