Bond Equivalent Yield Calculator

Treasury bills are quoted on a discount basis that understates the true return — it divides by the face value you never paid and pretends the year has 360 days. Bond equivalent yield restates the same bill the way every other investment is quoted.

BEY = (face − price) ÷ price × 365 ÷ days to maturity · Discount yield = (face − price) ÷ face × 360 ÷ days

Worked example

Worked example: a $10,000 bill bought for $9,800 with 182 days to run is quoted at a 3.96% discount yield — but its bond equivalent yield is 4.09%, the number to compare against a savings account.
