Time Dilation Calculator

At 90% of light speed, a traveller's clock ticks once for every 2.29 ticks back home. Special relativity's gamma factor turns 'fast' into an exact exchange rate between time experienced and time observed — and stretches lengths the same way. No speed you will ever travel at makes it noticeable; every GPS satellite must correct for it anyway.

γ = 1 ÷ √(1 − v²/c²) · Moving time observed = γ × proper time · Moving length observed = length ÷ γ

Worked example

Worked example: at 90% of light speed γ = 2.294 — one year aboard is 2.29 years at home, and a 100 m ship measures 43.6 m long to a stationary watcher.
