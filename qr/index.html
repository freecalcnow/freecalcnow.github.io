QR Code Generator

Turn any link or text into a QR code, right here — the code is drawn on your device, and you can download it as a PNG for printing, menus, posters or packaging.

QR codes encode text in a grid with built-in error correction — they still scan with up to ~30% of the code damaged, depending on the correction level.

Frequently asked questions

What size should I print?

At least 2 × 2 cm for close-up scanning, and bigger the further away the scanner will be — a rough rule is one tenth of the scanning distance. Always test the printed code with your own phone before a big run.

Do QR codes expire?

Codes made here are static — the text is inside the pattern itself, so they never expire and never route through a third-party server. That also means the content cannot be changed later; for changeable links, point the code at a page you control.

Is anything uploaded?

No — the pattern is generated in your browser from the text you type.
