Mead Maker's Calculator

Honey contributes about 35 gravity points per pound per gallon. Enter your honey and batch size for the original gravity and the maximum potential alcohol if it ferments dry — then plan nutrients and yeast around real numbers instead of folklore.

Gravity points = (lb honey ÷ gallons) × 35 · OG = 1 + points/1000 · Potential ABV ≈ (OG − 1.000) × 131.25 (to a dry 1.000 finish)

Worked example

Worked example: 12 lb of honey in a 5-gallon batch → 12 ÷ 5 × 35 = 84 points → OG 1.084 → potential ABV about 11.0% fermented dry.
