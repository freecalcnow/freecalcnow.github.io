Quilting Fabric Calculator

A square-block quilt top is arithmetic: count the blocks, count how many cut squares fit across a strip of fabric, and the strips give the yardage. Binding is costed from the quilt's perimeter. Seam allowances are included (¼ inch each side).

Cut square = finished square + 0.5 in · Squares across = quilt width ÷ finished size (rounded up) · Strips = squares ÷ squares per strip · Yardage = strips × cut size ÷ 36

Worked example

Worked example: a 60 × 80 in quilt from 5-inch finished squares → 12 × 16 = 192 squares; cut at 5.5 in, 8 squares fit across 44-inch fabric → 24 strips → 24 × 5.5 ÷ 36 ≈ 3.67 yards for the top, plus about half a yard of binding.
