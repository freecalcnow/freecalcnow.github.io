Diaper Usage Calculator

Newborns can need 10–12 changes a day; toddlers about half that. Pick the age stage, choose your period, and see the count and the cost — the numbers explain why nappies are a first-year budget line of their own.

Diapers needed = changes per day × days · Cost = diapers × price per diaper

Worked example: a newborn at 10 changes a day for 30 days → 300 nappies; at $0.25 each that is $75 a month.
