3×3 Matrix Determinant Calculator

The determinant is one number that summarises a matrix: zero means the transformation flattens space (and the matrix has no inverse); its size is the volume scaling factor. Enter the nine entries row by row.

det = a(ei − fh) − b(di − fg) + c(dh − eg) for rows [a b c / d e f / g h i]
