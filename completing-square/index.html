Completing the Square Calculator

Completing the square turns a quadratic into a shifted square — instantly revealing the vertex of the parabola, its axis of symmetry, and (by taking roots) the solutions. It is also where the quadratic formula itself comes from.

ax² + bx + c = a(x + b/2a)² + (c − b²/4a)

Worked example

Worked example: x² − 6x + 5 = (x − 3)² − 4. Vertex (3, −4), axis x = 3, roots x = 1 and x = 5.
