Average Atomic Mass Calculator

Elements arrive as mixtures of isotopes, and the periodic table prints the abundance-weighted average. Chlorine's famous 35.45 is not an atom that exists — it is 75.78% of ³⁵Cl and 24.22% of ³⁷Cl, blended.

Average = (mass₁ × abundance₁ + mass₂ × abundance₂) ÷ (abundance₁ + abundance₂)

Worked example: chlorine — 34.9689 u at 75.78% and 36.9659 u at 24.22% → 35.45 u, the number on the periodic table.
