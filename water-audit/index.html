Home Water Use Audit

Water bills arrive quarterly; water use happens daily, fixture by fixture. Enter your household's habits and this builds the daily picture — showers, flushing, taps, laundry, dishes, garden — for one person, so the biggest saver identifies itself instead of hiding in a total.

Each use = events × volume (or minutes × flow) · Garden and laundry are weekly figures ÷ 7

Worked example

Worked example: an 8-minute shower, 5 flushes, 5 tap-minutes, 3 laundry loads and 4 dishwasher loads a week, plus 30 garden minutes → about 212 litres a day for one person.
