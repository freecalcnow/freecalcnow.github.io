GPA Calculator

Your grade point average on the standard 4.0 scale, course by course — with weighted GPA for Honours and AP classes, total credits, and a live answer as you type. Add or remove courses to match your transcript.

GPA = Σ (grade points × credits) ÷ Σ credits · Weighted: Honours +0.5 and AP +1.0 per course (capped at 5.0), the common US convention

Grade points: A = 4.0 · A− = 3.7 · B+ = 3.3 · B = 3.0 · B− = 2.7 · C+ = 2.3 · C = 2.0 · C− = 1.7 · D+ = 1.3 · D = 1.0 · F = 0. Weighted adds +0.5 (Honours) or +1.0 (AP), capped at 5.0; an F earns no bonus.

Worked example

Worked example: an A in a 3-credit course, a B in a 3-credit course, an A in a 4-credit course and a B in a 3-credit course → (12 + 9 + 16 + 9) ÷ 13 credits = 3.54. Those exact courses are pre-filled below.

Frequently asked questions

What do the letter grades convert to?

The standard 4.0 scale: A+ and A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C− = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, F = 0. Some schools differ slightly — check your handbook for official figures.

How does weighting work?

Many US high schools add +0.5 for Honours and +1.0 for AP/IB courses, capped at 5.0, to reward harder classes. An F earns no bonus. Colleges often recalculate unweighted, so this tool shows both.

What GPA do I need next term?

Add next term’s courses with the grades you are aiming for and watch the combined figure move — the fastest way to see what a target GPA actually requires.
