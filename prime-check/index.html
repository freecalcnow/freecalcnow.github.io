Prime Number Checker

A prime has exactly two divisors: 1 and itself. Primes are the atoms of arithmetic — every whole number is built from them — and checking one is a matter of testing divisors up to its square root, which this tool does instantly.

Test every possible divisor from 2 up to √n. If none divides n exactly, n is prime.

Worked example

Worked example: 97 is prime — nothing between 2 and √97 ≈ 9.8 divides it. Its neighbours are 89 below and 101 above.
