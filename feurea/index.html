FeUrea Calculator (AKI Workup)

When the kidneys fail, the first question is whether they are under-perfused (pre-renal) or damaged (acute tubular necrosis). FeNa answers it — unless the patient takes diuretics, which fake a high FeNa. FeUrea survives diuretics: under 35% points pre-renal, over 50% points intrinsic.

FeUrea % = (urine urea ÷ blood urea) × (blood creatinine ÷ urine creatinine) × 100 · Use the same units top and bottom — they cancel

Worked example

Worked example: blood urea 84 mg/dL, urine urea 840 mg/dL, blood creatinine 2.0 and urine creatinine 80 gives (840 ÷ 84) × (2.0 ÷ 80) × 100 = 25% — a pre-renal picture: the tubules are still avidly reabsorbing urea.
