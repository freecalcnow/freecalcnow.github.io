Effective Annual Rate Calculator

A 12% rate compounded monthly is not 12% — each month's interest itself earns interest, so the true annual rate is higher. The effective annual rate (EAR, also quoted as APY on savings) is the honest number for comparing accounts and loans.

EAR = (1 + r ÷ n)^n − 1 · r = nominal annual rate · n = compounding periods per year

Worked example

Worked example: 12% compounded monthly → (1.01)^12 − 1 = 12.68% effective. On $10,000 that is $68.25 a year more than the headline rate suggests.
