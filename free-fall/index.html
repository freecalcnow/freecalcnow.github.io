Free Fall Calculator

Drop something and gravity accelerates it at 9.81 m/s² — after 1 second it is already moving at 9.8 m/s, after 3 seconds at 29.4. Work from a fall time or from a height. Air resistance is ignored, as in every textbook.

Distance = ½gt² · Speed = gt · From height: t = √(2h ÷ g), v = √(2gh) · g = 9.81 m/s²

Worked example

Worked example: 3 seconds of falling → 44.1 metres and 29.4 m/s (106 km/h) at impact. A 20 m drop takes 2.02 seconds and lands at 19.8 m/s.
