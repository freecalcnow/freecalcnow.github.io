CTC to In-Hand Salary Calculator (India)

CTC is what you cost the company, not what you receive: employer's PF and gratuity sit inside it, and your own PF and income tax come out before the bank transfer. This breaks a yearly CTC into the monthly in-hand under the new regime (FY 2025-26 slabs, ₹75,000 standard deduction, rebate up to ₹12 lakh taxable).

Gross = CTC − employer PF − gratuity · Taxable = gross − standard deduction · In-hand = gross − employee PF − tax − professional tax
