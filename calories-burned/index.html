Calories Burned Calculator

How much energy an activity burns depends mostly on your weight and how long you do it. Pick the activity, enter your weight and minutes — the MET (metabolic equivalent) values are the standard research figures used by exercise science.

Calories = MET × weight (kg) × hours · 1 MET = resting energy use; running at 10 km/h ≈ 9.8 METs

Worked example

Worked example: a 70 kg person running for 30 minutes at 9.8 METs burns about 343 kcal. The same 30 minutes walking briskly (3.5 METs) burns about 123 kcal.
