Body Roundness Index (BRI) Calculator

BRI models the body as an ellipse: height gives the long axis, waist the width. The rounder the ellipse, the higher the estimated visceral fat — and in large studies BRI tracks diabetes and heart risk better than BMI.

BRI = 364.2 − 365.5 × √(1 − (waist ÷ (2π))² ÷ (0.5 × height)²) — Thomas et al., 2013
