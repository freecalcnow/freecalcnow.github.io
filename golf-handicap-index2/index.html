Golf Handicap Index Calculator (WHS)

Under the World Handicap System, your index is the average of your best 8 score differentials from your last 20 rounds. Enter your differentials (comma-separated) and get the index.

Differential = (adjusted gross score − course rating) × 113 ÷ slope rating · Index = average of the lowest 8 of the last 20 differentials (fewer rounds use fewer, per the WHS table)
