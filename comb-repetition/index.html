Combinations with Repetition Calculator

When you can pick the same item more than once — three scoops from five flavours, repeats allowed — ordinary combinations undercount. The stars-and-bars formula counts every multiset correctly.

Combinations with repetition = C(n + r − 1, r), where n = types available and r = picks
