Coulomb's Law Calculator

Charges push and pull with a force that follows the same inverse-square shape as gravity but is enormously stronger: two microcoulombs a hand's breadth apart already push with the weight of a small apple.

F = k × q₁ × q₂ ÷ r² · k = 8.988 × 10⁹ N·m²/C² · charges entered in microcoulombs (µC)
