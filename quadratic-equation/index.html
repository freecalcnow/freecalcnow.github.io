Quadratic Equation Solver

Every quadratic is a parabola; its roots are where it crosses zero. The discriminant (b² − 4ac) decides before you solve: positive means two real roots, zero means one touching root, negative means none (the roots are complex).

x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] ÷ 2a · Vertex x = −b ÷ 2a
