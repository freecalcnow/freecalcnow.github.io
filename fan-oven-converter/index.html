Oven Temperature Converter (Fan · Conventional · Gas Mark)

Recipes speak four temperature languages and fan ovens run hotter in effect than conventional ones. Enter a temperature in any system for all the others — including the fan adjustment (typically 20°C lower for the same result).

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32 · Fan ≈ conventional − 20°C · Gas mark ≈ (°C − 121) ÷ 14 (above 140°C)
