CCTV Storage Calculator

Camera storage is just bitrate × time × cameras, but the retention requirement (7, 14, 30 days) is what turns it into terabytes. Enter your setup for the drive size to buy — with the motion-recording discount shown honestly as an estimate.

Storage per camera per day = bitrate (Mbps) × 0.45 GB/hr × recording hours · Total = per-camera × cameras × retention days

Worked example

Worked example: 8 cameras at 4 Mbps recording 12 hours a day for 30 days → per camera 4 × 0.45 × 12 = 21.6 GB/day → × 8 × 30 = 5,184 GB ≈ 5.2 TB of drives.
