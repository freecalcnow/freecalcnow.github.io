Effective Population Size Calculator

A herd of 1,000 with five breeding males behaves, genetically, like a population of about twenty. Effective size Ne converts unequal sex ratios and family sizes into the headcount that drift and inbreeding actually experience — the number conservation genetics is built on.

Unequal sexes: Ne = 4 × Nm × Nf ÷ (Nm + Nf) · Inbreeding rate per generation ΔF = 1 ÷ (2Ne) · Conservation guide: Ne ≥ 50 avoids short-term inbreeding harm, ≥ 500 (often now argued 1,000) preserves long-term potential

Worked example

Worked example: 5 breeding males and 500 females gives Ne = 4 × 5 × 500 ÷ 505 ≈ 19.8 — despite 505 animals, inbreeding accumulates at 2.5% a generation, deep in the danger zone. Balancing to 50 males lifts Ne to about 182.
