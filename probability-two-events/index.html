Probability of Two Events Calculator

Most real probability questions involve two events. Enter each probability and the relationship between them — independent (unrelated) or mutually exclusive (cannot both happen) — and get all four combinations at once.

Independent: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B) · P(either) = P(A) + P(B) − P(both) · Exclusive: P(both) = 0
