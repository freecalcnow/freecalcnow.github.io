Patio Paver Calculator

Pavers are a division problem with sharp edges: patio area over paver area, plus a waste allowance for the cuts every edge demands. The sand bed is the other forgotten line item — about a cubic yard per 100 sq ft at a 1-inch bed, which this tool counts too.

Pavers = ceil( patio area ÷ paver area × (1 + waste %) ) · Jointing/base sand ≈ area × depth

Worked example

Worked example: a 100 sq ft patio with 8 × 4 inch pavers (0.222 sq ft each) needs 450 pavers before waste, 473 with 5% added.
