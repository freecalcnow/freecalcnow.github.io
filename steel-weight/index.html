Steel Weight Calculator

Steel weighs 7,850 kg per cubic metre — everything else is geometry. Enter a plate's dimensions or a round bar's diameter and length, and get the weight before the crane (or the invoice) arrives.

Plate: weight = length × width × thickness × 7,850 kg/m³ · Bar: π/4 × d² × length × 7,850

Worked example

Worked example: a 2 m × 1 m plate, 10 mm thick → 0.02 m³ → 157 kg. A 20 mm round bar, 6 m long → 14.8 kg.
