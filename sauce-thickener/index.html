Sauce Thickener Calculator

Thickeners dose by the cup and the target: a light coating sauce needs a whisper, a pie filling needs structure. Pick your thickener and thickness for exact amounts — remembering cornstarch and arrowroot have about twice the power of flour, and go in as slurries, not roux.

Per cup — flour (as roux): thin 1 tbsp, medium 2, thick 3 · Cornstarch / arrowroot: half the flour amount · Roux is flour + equal fat by weight

Worked example

Worked example: 4 cups of gravy at medium thickness with roux → 8 tbsp flour (about 64 g) cooked with 64 g of butter or drippings. As cornstarch instead: 4 tbsp in a cold-water slurry.
