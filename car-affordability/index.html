Car Affordability Calculator (20/4/10 Rule)

The 20/4/10 rule is the classic guardrail against car-poverty: at least 20% down, a loan of no more than 4 years, and ALL monthly car costs — payment, fuel, insurance — under 10% of gross monthly income. This works backwards from your income to a maximum sensible price.

Monthly car budget = 10% of gross monthly income · Payment budget = 65% of that (the rest is fuel + insurance) · Max loan = payment × annuity factor (48 months) · Max price = loan ÷ 0.8

Worked example

Worked example: gross income 8,000 a month → total car budget 800 a month, payment budget 520. At 7% over 48 months that supports a loan of about 21,700, so a car of about 27,100 with about 5,400 down.
