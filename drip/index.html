Dividend Reinvestment (DRIP) Calculator

Reinvested dividends compound twice: the share count grows, and most payers also raise the dividend itself. This tool simulates both, year by year, with your yearly additions included.

Each year: dividends = portfolio value × yield → reinvested into more shares; dividend per share grows at the dividend-growth rate; prices grow at the price-growth rate

Worked example

Worked example: $10,000 invested, $1,200 added yearly, a 3.5% yield growing 6% a year and prices rising 4% → after 15 years roughly $58,000, paying about $2,600 a year of dividends — versus $350 in year one.
