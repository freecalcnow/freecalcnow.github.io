Beer Colour (SRM) Calculator

Beer colour is estimated from Malt Colour Units: grain weight times its Lovibond rating, divided by batch volume. The Morey equation then converts MCU to SRM, the scale from pale straw (2) to opaque black (40+).

MCU = Σ (grain weight lb × °Lovibond) ÷ batch gallons · SRM = 1.4922 × MCU^0.6859 (Morey)

Worked example

Worked example: 10 lb of 3°L pale malt in 5 gallons → MCU = 10 × 3 ÷ 5 = 6 → SRM = 1.4922 × 6^0.6859 ≈ 5.1 — a deep gold beer.
