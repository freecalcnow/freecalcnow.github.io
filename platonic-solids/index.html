Platonic Solids Calculator

Exactly five solids can be built from identical regular faces: tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron and icosahedron. Give an edge length and get the volume, surface area, inradius, circumradius and dihedral angle for any of them.

Each solid has fixed constants: V = kᵥ·a³, A = kₐ·a² · The icosahedron packs the most volume per surface — which is why viruses wear it

Worked example

Worked example: an icosahedron with 2 cm edges has a volume of about 17.45 cm³ and a surface area of about 34.64 cm² — nearly double the volume of a 2 cm cube (8 cm³) from a similar amount of material.
