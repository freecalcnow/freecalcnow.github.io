Gravel & Aggregate Calculator

Gravel is bought by the tonne or the bulk bag but spread by volume. Enter the area and depth for the cubic metres you need and the tonnes to order — with the standard 10% settling allowance included in the headline figure.

Volume = area × depth · Tonnes = volume × density (~1.5 t/m³ for gravel) · Order = tonnes × 1.1
