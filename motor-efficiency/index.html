Motor Efficiency Calculator

Efficiency is the share of input power that survives as useful output. The rest is heat, noise and vibration — which is why an inefficient motor is also a heater you did not ask for.

Efficiency = output ÷ input × 100 · Losses = input − output

Worked example

Worked example: 2,000 W in, 1,700 W out → 85% efficient, 300 W lost as heat. Run for an hour, that is 0.3 kWh warming the room instead of turning the shaft.
