Carpool Cost Split Calculator

A solo commute is the most expensive seat in transport. Splitting fuel and running costs across riders cuts everyone's bill to a fraction — this tool divides the monthly cost and shows each person's saving.

Each pays = total monthly cost ÷ people · Saving = solo cost − each pays

Worked example

Worked example: $240 a month of commuting cost shared by 3 people → $80 each, saving every person $160 a month — $1,920 a year.
