Aquarium Water Change Calculator

A water change dilutes everything by the fraction replaced: change 30% and nitrates fall 30%. One change often cannot reach the target without stressing the fish — this tool works out the single change needed, or splits it into gentle daily changes.

One change: fraction needed = (current − target) ÷ (current − tap level) · Repeated changes: level after n changes = tap + (current − tap) × (1 − fraction)ⁿ

Worked example

Worked example: a 200-litre tank at 40 ppm nitrate, tap water at 5 ppm and a target of 15 ppm needs (40 − 15) ÷ (40 − 5) = 71% changed at once — too much for one day. Three daily changes of about 34% each (68 litres) get there gently.
