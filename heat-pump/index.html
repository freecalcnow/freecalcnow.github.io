Heat Pump Cost & CO₂ Calculator

A heat pump does not make heat, it moves it — which is why one unit of electricity can deliver three units of warmth (a COP of 3). Whether that beats a gas boiler on cost and carbon depends on your prices and your grid. Enter your home's yearly heat demand and see both bills and both footprints.

Electricity used = heat demand ÷ COP · Gas used = heat demand ÷ boiler efficiency · Running cost = energy used × price

Worked example

Worked example: a home needing 12,000 kWh of heat. A heat pump at COP 3 uses 4,000 kWh of electricity — $800 at $0.20/kWh. A 90%-efficient gas boiler burns 13,333 kWh of gas — $1,200 at $0.09/kWh. The heat pump saves $400 a year and about 840 kg of CO₂.
