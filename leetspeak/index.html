Leetspeak Translator

Leetspeak swaps letters for lookalike digits and symbols — a from the BBS era that never quite died in gamer tags and handles. Pick a level: light keeps it readable, full 1337 goes all in. The decoder reverses the standard substitutions.

Classic swaps: A→4 · B→8 · E→3 · G→6 · I→1 · L→1 · O→0 · S→5 · T→7 · Z→2 · Full leet adds multi-character forms: A→/\ · H→|-| · M→|\/| · N→|\| · W→\/\/ · K→|<

Worked example

Worked example: 'leet' in classic leet is '1337' — the word the dialect is named after. 'hello world' becomes 'h3110 w0r1d' at the classic level, and something considerably less legible at full.
