Photoelectric Effect Calculator

Light below a threshold frequency ejects NO electrons from a metal, however bright it is — the fact that forced physics to accept photons. Each photon spends its energy hf freeing one electron (the work function) and gives the change as kinetic energy. This runs the full account.

Photon energy E = hc ÷ λ · KE(max) = E − work function · Threshold λ = hc ÷ work function · hc ≈ 1240 eV·nm

Worked example

Worked example: 450 nm light on a metal with a 2.3 eV work function → photons of 2.76 eV, electrons ejected with up to 0.46 eV, stopped by 0.46 V. The threshold wavelength is 539 nm — redder light than that frees nothing.
