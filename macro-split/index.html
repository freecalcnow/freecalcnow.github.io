Macro Split Calculator

Calories set the budget; macros decide what the budget buys. Enter your daily calories and the split you follow, and get grams — the numbers food labels and trackers actually speak.

Protein & carbs = 4 kcal/g · Fat = 9 kcal/g · Grams = (calories × macro %) ÷ kcal per gram
