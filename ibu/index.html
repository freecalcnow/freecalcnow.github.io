IBU Calculator (Tinseth)

The Tinseth model is homebrewing's standard bitterness estimate: alpha acids isomerise during the boil at a rate set by wort gravity and time. Enter the hop addition and get its IBU contribution — add multiple additions by running each and summing.

IBU = utilisation × (hop grams × alpha % × 1000) ÷ wort litres · Utilisation = 1.65 × 0.000125^(OG−1) × (1 − e^(−0.04 × minutes)) ÷ 4.15

Worked example

Worked example: 30 g of 5% alpha hops boiled 60 minutes in 20 L of 1.050 wort → utilisation 0.231 → about 17 IBU from this addition.
