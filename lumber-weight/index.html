Lumber Weight Calculator

Wood weight is species plus water: the same board in white pine or red oak differs by half again. Enter board feet and a species for the seasoned (kiln-dried) weight — green lumber can run 50% heavier, which matters for trailers and floor loads.

Weight = board feet ÷ 12 × density (lb per cubic foot)

Worked example

Worked example: 50 board feet of red oak at 44 lb/ft³ → 50 ÷ 12 × 44 = 183 lb (83 kg) of wood.
