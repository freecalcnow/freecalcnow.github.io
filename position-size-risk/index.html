Position Size Calculator (Risk %)

Professionals size positions backwards from the loss they can accept: risk a fixed slice of the account if the stop is hit, and the share count follows. This is that calculation.

Risk per share = entry − stop · Shares = (account × risk %) ÷ risk per share · Position value = shares × entry
