Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) Calculator

The ANC is what actually matters for infection risk in neutropenia — not the headline white cell count. It combines segmented neutrophils and band forms, and its bands (mild, moderate, severe) drive decisions from chemotherapy timing to isolation precautions.

ANC = WBC × (segmented % + band %) ÷ 100

Worked example

Worked example: WBC 3.5 ×10⁹/L with 40% segmented and 5% bands → ANC = 3.5 × 0.45 = 1.58 ×10⁹/L — mild neutropenia.
