Area Under a Curve (Trapezoidal Rule)

When you have measurements instead of a formula — speed every minute, flow every hour, drug levels every sample — the trapezoidal rule is how the area (and therefore the total) is honestly estimated: join the dots with straight lines and add up the trapezoids.

AUC ≈ Σ (xᵢ₊₁ − xᵢ) × (yᵢ + yᵢ₊₁) ÷ 2 · For evenly spaced points: spacing × (first÷2 + middle points + last÷2)

Worked example

Worked example: a pump's flow readings of 10, 14, 18, 15 and 11 litres a minute, one minute apart, give an area of 0.5×10 + 14 + 18 + 15 + 0.5×11 = 57.5 litres delivered over the four minutes.
