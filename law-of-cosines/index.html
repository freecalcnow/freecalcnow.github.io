Law of Cosines Calculator

Pythagoras only works on right angles. The cosine rule is its general form: give it two sides and the angle between them and it finds the third side; give it all three sides and it finds any angle. Surveyors and navigators have used it for centuries.

c² = a² + b² − 2ab·cos(C) · cos(C) = (a² + b² − c²) ÷ 2ab

Worked example

Worked example: sides 5 and 7 with a 60° angle between them → the third side is √39 ≈ 6.24. With sides 5, 7 and 8, the angle opposite the side of 8 is about 81.8°.
