Distance to the Horizon Calculator

The Earth curves away at about 8 inches per mile squared, so your horizon depends only on your height above the surface: roughly 3.57 × √height (metres) kilometres away. Add a target's height too — two heights, two horizons, added together — for lighthouses, ships and mountains.

Distance ≈ 3.57 × √h km (h in metres), from √(2Rh) with R = 6,371 km

Worked example

Worked example: standing at the shore, eyes 1.7 m up → the horizon is 4.65 km away. From a 100 m cliff it stretches to 35.7 km.
