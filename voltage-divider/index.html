Voltage Divider Calculator

Two resistors in series split a voltage in proportion to their values. It is how sensors are read, how big voltages are shrunk for measurement, and the first circuit every electronics student builds.

Vout = Vin × R₂ ÷ (R₁ + R₂) · the output is taken across R₂

Worked example

Worked example: 12 V across two equal 1 kΩ resistors gives exactly 6 V out — half the input, because the split is equal.
