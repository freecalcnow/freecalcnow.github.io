Age in Days, Hours & Minutes Calculator

Years flatten a life; days make it vivid. Enter a birth date for your age counted in days, weeks, hours and minutes — plus two playful estimates: breaths and heartbeats, at typical resting rates.

Days = today − birth date · Hours = days × 24 · Heartbeats ≈ minutes × ~72
