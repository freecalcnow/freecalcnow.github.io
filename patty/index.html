Burger Patty Calculator

The barbecue maths that always goes wrong at the shop: how many patties does this pack of mince actually make, at a sensible patty weight, and is that enough for everyone coming? Enter the mince and the patty size — get the count, the awkward leftover, and the headcount it covers.

Patties = total mince ÷ patty weight (rounded down) · Serves = patties ÷ patties per person

Worked example

Worked example: 2,000 g of mince at 150 g a patty → 13 patties with 50 g left over (a taster for the cook) — at 2 patties each, it feeds 6 people.
