Echo & Sound Delay Distance Calculator

Sound is slow enough to measure with a stopwatch: shout at a cliff, time the echo, and the distance falls out — remembering the sound made a round trip. The same maths sets speaker delays in venues, where the rule is about 2.9 milliseconds per metre.

Speed of sound ≈ 331.3 + 0.606 × temperature (°C) m/s · One-way distance = speed × echo time ÷ 2

Worked example

Worked example: an echo returning after 2 seconds at 20 °C → sound speed about 343.4 m/s, round trip 686.9 m, so the cliff is about 343 metres away.
