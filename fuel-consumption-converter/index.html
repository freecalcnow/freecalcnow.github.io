Fuel Consumption Converter

Fuel economy is quoted upside-down in different countries: bigger-is-better mpg and km/L versus smaller-is-better L/100 km — and US and UK mpg differ by 20%. This converts all four honestly.

L/100 km = 235.215 ÷ mpg (US) · L/100 km = 282.481 ÷ mpg (UK) · km/L = 100 ÷ L/100 km
