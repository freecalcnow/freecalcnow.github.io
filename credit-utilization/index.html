Credit Utilization Calculator

Utilization — your card balances as a share of your limits — is one of the biggest levers in a credit score. Issuers report it monthly, and lower is better: under 30% is the common guideline, under 10% is where the strongest scores sit.

Utilization = total balances ÷ total limits × 100

Worked example

Worked example: $3,000 of balances on $10,000 of limits is 30.0% utilization. Paying $2,000 off takes it to 10%.
