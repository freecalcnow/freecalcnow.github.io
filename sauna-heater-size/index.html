Sauna Heater Size Calculator

Sauna heaters are sized by room volume: roughly 1 kW per cubic metre. But glass doors and stone walls leak heat — each square metre of them counts as extra volume. This does the corrected sizing.

Effective volume = room volume + 1.2 m³ per m² of glass or uninsulated stone/tile · Heater ≈ effective volume × 1 kW (round up)
