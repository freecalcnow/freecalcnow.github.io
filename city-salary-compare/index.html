City vs City Salary Compare — What Is Your Salary Worth Elsewhere?

$3,000 a month in New York is not the same as $3,000 a month in Lahore. Pick two cities, enter your salary, and this compares the cost of living and what you could save in each — using a simple cost index (New York = 100), labelled as an estimate.

Cost index: New York = 100. Equivalent salary in city B = your salary × index B ÷ index A. Living costs are taken as 60% of salary in city A and scaled by the index.

Worked example

$3,000 a month in New York (index 100) vs Lahore (index 20). The same lifestyle costs about $360 a month in Lahore instead of $1,800 in New York. Your $3,000 feels like $15,000 in Lahore — and you could save about $2,640 a month there instead of $1,200 in New York.

How to use

  1. Enter your monthly salary in dollars.
  2. Pick the city you are in now and the city you are comparing with.
  3. Read what your salary is really worth in the other city — and what you could save.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cost index?

A simple number for how expensive a city is. New York is set to 100. A city with index 50 costs about half as much for the same life; index 20 costs about a fifth.

Are these live prices?

No — they are estimates from a built-in cost table (2026). Rents change fast. Use this to compare, then check real rents and grocery prices before you move.

Why is 60% of salary used for living costs?

It is a rough average for rent, food, transport and bills. Your real share may be higher or lower — the comparison still shows the direction and the size of the gap.
