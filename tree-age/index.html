Tree Age Calculator

Forestors estimate a standing tree's age from its girth: measure the trunk at chest height, turn circumference into diameter, and multiply by a species growth factor — roughly how many years the tree takes to add an inch of diameter. It is an estimate with wide error bars, but it is the best one that leaves the tree standing.

Diameter = circumference ÷ π · Age ≈ diameter (inches) × species growth factor

Worked example

Worked example: a trunk measuring 150 cm around → diameter 47.7 cm = 18.8 inches. At the average hardwood factor of 4.5, the tree is about 85 years old — planted, if the estimate is right, some time in the late 1930s.
