Wells Score Calculator (Pulmonary Embolism)

Wells' criteria turn clinical findings into a PE probability before any test: points for leg signs, tachycardia, immobility, prior clots, haemoptysis, cancer — and the big one, whether PE is the most likely diagnosis. The two-tier reading (likely / unlikely) decides the next step.

≤ 4 = PE unlikely (D-dimer first) · > 4 = PE likely (imaging) — the two-tier model
