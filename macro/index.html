Macro Calculator

Calories tell you how much; macros tell you what. Set your daily calorie target and the percentage split you follow, and get exact grams — protein and carbs at 4 kcal per gram, fat at 9.

Protein g = kcal × P% ÷ 4 · Carbs g = kcal × C% ÷ 4 · Fat g = kcal × F% ÷ 9 · Percentages should sum to 100

Worked example

Worked example: 2,500 kcal at 30/40/30 → 188 g protein, 250 g carbs, 83 g fat. A higher-protein 40/30/30 split on the same calories: 250 g protein, 188 g carbs, 83 g fat.

How is this calculated?

How exact is the result?
