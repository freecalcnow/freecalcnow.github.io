Gutter & Downspout Calculator

Gutters fail by undersizing, not by leaking: the drainage area of the roof section and your local rainfall intensity decide the gutter size and how many downspouts the run needs. Enter the roof section that drains to this gutter.

Adjusted drainage area = roof plan area × slope factor (low 1.0, medium 1.1, steep 1.2) · One 3×4 in downspout drains ≈ 1,200 sq ft at 4 in/hr, scaling inversely with intensity

Worked example

Worked example: a 1,200 sq ft roof section, medium pitch, at 4 in/hr design rainfall → adjusted 1,320 sq ft → 2 downspouts, and a 6-inch gutter is the safer size (over ~750 sq ft per run, step up from 5-inch).
