Moles to Gas Volume (STP) Calculator

At 0 °C and 1 atmosphere, one mole of any ideal gas fills 22.4 litres — oxygen, hydrogen or steam, the count of molecules is what matters, not their identity. At room conditions (25 °C) the figure is 24.45 L.

Volume = moles × molar volume · 22.4 L/mol at STP (0 °C, 1 atm) · 24.45 L/mol at 25 °C, 1 atm

Worked example

Worked example: 2 mol at STP → 44.8 litres. The same 2 mol at 25 °C fills 48.9 litres — gases expand with warmth.
