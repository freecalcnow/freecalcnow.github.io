Generator Size Calculator

Generators fail households in one predictable way: the fridge, pump and air conditioner all try to start in the same second, and starting a motor takes two to three times its running watts. This totals your running load, applies the surge to the biggest motor, and lands on the generator class to shop for.

Running total = Σ appliance watts · Surge total = running total − largest motor + largest motor × 3 · Shop size ≈ surge total, with running headroom

Worked example

Worked example: fridge 150 W, central AC 3,500 W, furnace fan 600 W, well pump 1,000 W, lights 300 W and 500 W of other loads → 6,050 W running. With the AC as the largest motor, surge is 6,050 − 3,500 + 10,500 = 13,050 W: shop for roughly a 7,500 W running / 13,500 W peak class machine.
