Week Number Calculator

Business and much of Europe run on ISO week numbers: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week containing the year's first Thursday. This tool gives any date's ISO week and week-year (which can differ from the calendar year near New Year).

ISO 8601: the week of the Thursday of the current week; week 1 contains 4 January.
