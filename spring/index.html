Spring (Hooke's Law) Calculator

Springs push back in exact proportion to how far you stretch or squeeze them — double the stretch, double the force. That linearity (Hooke's law) runs everything from bathroom scales to car suspensions, and the stored energy grows with the square of the stretch.

Force F = k × x · Stored energy = ½ × k × x², where k is the spring constant (N/m) and x the stretch or compression
