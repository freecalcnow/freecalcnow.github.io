Dog Calorie Calculator

Dog food bags print feeding charts by weight, but the honest number starts from RER — resting energy requirement, 70 × (weight in kg)^0.75 — multiplied by a life-stage factor. This gives the daily kcal and what it means in cups of a typical food.

RER = 70 × weight^0.75 · Daily kcal = RER × life-stage factor
