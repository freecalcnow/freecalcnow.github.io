Standby Power (Vampire Load) Calculator

TVs on standby, chargers with nothing attached, the router, the microwave clock — a modern home leaks watts around the clock. Add up your always-on draw and this turns it into the yearly bill and carbon it quietly produces, so you can see whether smart plugs and switched sockets pay for themselves.

Yearly kWh = standby watts × hours per day × 365 ÷ 1000 · Cost = kWh × price per kWh

Worked example

Worked example: 40 W of standby draw for 20 hours a day → 292 kWh a year — $87.60 at $0.30/kWh, and about 117 kg of CO₂ on an average grid.
