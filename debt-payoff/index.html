Debt Payoff Calculator

Enter a balance, its rate and what you pay each month. See the payoff date, the total interest — and exactly what one extra payment a month saves you. That last number is why this tool exists.

Months to clear = −ln(1 − balance × r ÷ payment) ÷ ln(1 + r), where r = APR ÷ 12

Worked example

Worked example: a $5,000 card balance at 22% APR, paying $150 a month, takes about 52 months and costs roughly $2,800 in interest. Pay $200 instead and it clears in about 34 months with roughly $1,750 interest — about $1,050 saved and 18 months of your life back.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my balance barely move at first?

Interest is charged on the whole remaining balance. Early payments are mostly interest; as the balance falls, the same payment eats more and more principal — which is why extra payments early on are so powerful.

Snowball or avalanche?

Avalanche (highest rate first) is mathematically cheapest. Snowball (smallest balance first) is behaviourally stickier for many people. The best method is the one you will still be using in month six.
