Naismith's Rule Hiking Time Calculator

William Naismith's rule has survived 130 years of GPS because it works: an hour for every 5 km, plus an hour for every 600 m climbed. This tool adds the standard descent adjustments (gentle descents save time, steep ones cost it) and the breaks nobody admits to needing.

Time = distance ÷ 5 km/h + ascent ÷ 600 m/h · Langmuir's descent correction: gentle (−5° to −12°) subtract ~10 min per 300 m descended · steep (over −12°) add ~10 min per 300 m

Worked example

Worked example: a 16 km round with 900 m of climb takes 16 ÷ 5 + 900 ÷ 600 = 3.2 + 1.5 = 4.7 hours of moving time — plan five and a half with stops, and be off the summit by the time the forecast turns, not when you feel like it.
