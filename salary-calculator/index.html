Take-Home Pay Calculator (Salary Calculator)

What actually lands in your bank account? Enter your salary and filing status — this works out your 2026 US federal tax, Social Security and Medicare, and shows your take-home pay per paycheck. Federal estimate only; state tax is a flat rate you enter yourself.

Take-home = gross − federal tax − Social Security − Medicare − state tax − deductions. Federal tax uses the 2026 marginal brackets (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32).

Worked example

Single filer, $80,000 a year, paid every two weeks, no deductions. Standard deduction $16,100 leaves $63,900 taxable: 10% on the first $12,400 ($1,240), 12% on the next $38,000 ($4,560), 22% on the last $13,500 ($2,970) — federal tax $8,770. Social Security 6.2% is $4,960 and Medicare 1.45% is $1,160. Take-home: $65,110 a year, $2,504.23 per paycheck.

How to use

  1. Type your annual gross salary and pick your filing status and pay frequency.
  2. Add any pre-tax deductions (401(k), HSA) and your state tax rate if you want it included.
  3. Read the big answer — your take-home pay per paycheck — and the breakdown table.

Frequently asked questions

Is this my exact paycheck?

No — it is a federal estimate. Your employer's withholding also depends on your W-4, and state tax is usually progressive, not flat. Use it to plan, not to predict the cent.

Why is Social Security capped?

Social Security tax (6.2%) only applies to the first $184,500 of 2026 wages. Earn more than that and the Social Security part stops for the rest of the year. Medicare (1.45%) has no cap.

What is the additional 0.9% Medicare tax?

High earners pay an extra 0.9% Medicare tax on wages over $200,000 (single) or $250,000 (married filing jointly). It is included here automatically.
