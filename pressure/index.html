Pressure Calculator

Pressure is force spread over area — the reason a stiletto heel dents a floor a boot does not, and why snowshoes work. Results in pascals, bar, psi and atmospheres.

Pressure = force ÷ area · 1 atm = 101,325 Pa = 1.013 bar = 14.7 psi

Worked example

Worked example: 1,000 N on 0.5 m² → 2,000 Pa. The same 1,000 N on a 1 cm² heel tip (0.0001 m²) → 10,000,000 Pa — a hundred atmospheres.
