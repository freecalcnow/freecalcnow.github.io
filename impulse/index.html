Impulse Calculator

Impulse is force applied over time, and the impulse-momentum theorem says it exactly equals the change in momentum. It is why airbags and crumple zones work: same impulse, spread over more time, means less force.

J = F × t = Δp = m × Δv

Worked example: 50 N for 0.2 s → an impulse of 10 N·s, which speeds a 2 kg object up by 5 m/s.
