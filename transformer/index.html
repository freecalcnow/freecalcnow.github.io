Transformer Calculator

A transformer trades voltage for current through the turns ratio of its two coils. Double the turns, double the voltage, halve the current — (ideally) no power created or lost.

V₂ = V₁ × (N₂ ÷ N₁) · I₂ = I₁ × (N₁ ÷ N₂) · Power in = power out (ideal)

Worked example

Worked example: 120 V into a 1000-turn primary with a 100-turn secondary → 12 V out; a 0.5 A draw on the secondary pulls only 0.05 A from the primary side.
