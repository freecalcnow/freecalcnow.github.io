Mass–Energy (E = mc²) Calculator

E = mc² says mass IS energy, frozen. Because c² is enormous, a trivial amount of matter holds staggering energy: one kilogram fully converted would release about 21 megatons of TNT equivalent. Nothing in daily life converts mass completely — but nuclear reactions convert a fraction of a percent, and that fraction powers stars and bombs alike.

E = m × c², c = 299,792,458 m/s · 1 kiloton of TNT = 4.184 × 10¹² J

Worked example

Worked example: 1 kg → 8.99 × 10¹⁶ joules — about 2.50 × 10¹⁰ kWh, or roughly 21.5 megatons of TNT equivalent. A single gram is about 21.5 kilotons.
