Odds Ratio Calculator

Odds ratios run medicine, marketing tests and social science: how much more likely is the outcome in the exposed group than in the group without the exposure? Enter the four cells of a 2×2 table.

Odds ratio = (a ÷ b) ÷ (c ÷ d) = (a × d) ÷ (b × c)

Worked example

Worked example: 45 of 75 exposed people have the outcome; 20 of 80 unexposed do. Odds ratio = (45 × 60) ÷ (30 × 20) = 4.50; relative risk = 60% ÷ 25% = 2.40.
