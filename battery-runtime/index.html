Battery Runtime Calculator

Battery capacity in milliamp-hours only becomes time when you know the voltage and the load. This converts capacity to watt-hours, takes off a realistic efficiency loss, and divides by your device's draw — the honest runtime, not the box's promise.

Energy = mAh × volts ÷ 1000 (Wh) · Runtime = energy × efficiency ÷ load (W)

Worked example

Worked example: a 20,000 mAh power bank at 3.7 V holds 74 Wh. At 85% efficiency powering a 10 W load, it runs 6.29 hours — about 6 hours 17 minutes.
