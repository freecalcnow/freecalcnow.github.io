Roast Cooking Time Calculator

Roasting charts are minutes-per-kilo plus a fixed start, at a stated oven temperature, for a joint at room temperature. This calculator carries the classic timings for beef, lamb, pork and chicken, and adds the resting time that decides whether the juices stay in the meat or flood the board.

Beef: rare 20 min/kg + 20 · medium 25 + 25 · well 30 + 30 (at 180 °C) · Lamb: 25–30/kg + 25 · Pork: 35/kg + 35 (cooked through) · Chicken: 20/kg + 20 (always through) · Rest: at least 15–30 min under foil

Worked example

Worked example: a 1.8 kg beef joint for medium: 1.8 × 25 + 25 = 70 minutes at 180 °C, then 20 minutes resting. Total plan: take it out of the fridge an hour before, roast 70, rest 20 — carve at the 2½-hour mark from fridge door to table.
