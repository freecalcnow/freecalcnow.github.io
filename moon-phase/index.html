Moon Phase Calculator

The moon cycles through its phases every 29.53 days. Counting from a known new moon (6 January 2000), this tool gives the moon's age on any date, how much of its face is lit, and the phase name — from new, through the quarters, to full and back.

Age = days since 6 Jan 2000 new moon, mod 29.53059 · Illumination = (1 − cos(2π × age ÷ 29.53059)) ÷ 2
