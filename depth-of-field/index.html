Depth of Field Calculator

Depth of field is the zone that looks acceptably sharp, set by three choices: aperture (the biggest lever), focal length and focus distance. This uses the standard thin-lens formulas with the circle of confusion for your sensor size.

Hyperfocal H = f² ÷ (N × c) + f · Near = s(H − f) ÷ (H + s − 2f) · Far = s(H − f) ÷ (H − s)

Worked example

Worked example: 50 mm at f/2.8 on full frame, focused at 3 m → sharp from about 2.78 m to 3.26 m — a 0.48 m zone. Stop down to f/8 and it stretches past 1.4 m.
