Cash-Out Refinance Calculator

A cash-out refinance replaces your mortgage with a bigger one and pays you the difference in cash. The questions that decide whether it is wise: the new payment, how much cash actually lands after closing costs, and how many months of payment savings (if any) repay those costs.

New loan = current balance + cash out (+ closing costs if rolled in) · New payment = standard amortisation on the new loan

Worked example

Worked example: balance $200,000 at 7% with 25 years left (payment $1,414), taking $50,000 cash out at 6% over a fresh 30 years with $6,000 of costs rolled in → new loan $256,000 and a payment of about $1,535 — roughly $121 MORE a month despite the lower rate, because the balance grew and the clock restarted. That is the honest comparison this tool makes.
