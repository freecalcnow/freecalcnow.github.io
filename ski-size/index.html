Ski Length Calculator

Ski length is anchored to height — beginners ski between chin and nose, experts up to full height and beyond — then nudged by weight and terrain. This returns the range for your level, with the adjustments that matter.

Beginner: height − 15 to − 10 cm · Intermediate: − 10 to − 5 · Advanced: − 5 to 0 (and beyond for powder / race)

Worked example: 175 cm tall, intermediate → skis of 165–170 cm. Heavier than average for your height? Take the longer end.
