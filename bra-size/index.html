Bra Size Calculator

Bra sizing starts from two measurements: underbust (snug, around the ribcage just under the bust) and bust (around the fullest point). The underbust sets the band; the difference between the two sets the cup. Enter inches for the standard US/UK calculation.

Band = underbust rounded to the nearest even number · Cup = bust − band: 0 = AA, 1 = A, 2 = B, 3 = C, 4 = D, 5 = DD/E, 6 = DDD/F, 7 = G…
