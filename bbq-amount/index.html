BBQ Meat Calculator

Barbecue shrinks: a brisket can lose 45% of its weight between the butcher and the plate, which is why cooks who buy 'half a pound per person' of raw meat serve quarter-pounds. Start from the cooked portion you want each guest to eat and divide by the meat's yield — that is the raw weight to buy.

Raw meat to buy = guests × cooked portion per person ÷ cooking yield

Worked example

Worked example: 20 guests, a generous half-pound cooked portion each, brisket at a 55% yield → 20 × 0.5 ÷ 0.55 = 18.2 lb of raw brisket (about 8.3 kg). The same crowd on pulled pork (60% yield) needs 16.7 lb of pork butt.
