eGFR (Kidney Function) Calculator

eGFR estimates how much blood your kidneys filter each minute, adjusted to a standard body surface. It is the number kidney health is staged by, and since 2021 it is calculated with the CKD-EPI equation that needs only creatinine, age and sex — no race term. Bring a recent creatinine result and get your estimate and stage.

2021 CKD-EPI: eGFR = 142 × min(Scr ÷ κ, 1)^α × max(Scr ÷ κ, 1)^(−1.200) × 0.9938^age (× 1.012 if female); κ = 0.7 female / 0.9 male, α = −0.241 / −0.302

Worked example

Worked example: a 50-year-old man with creatinine 1.0 mg/dL → eGFR about 92 mL/min/1.73 m², in the G1 range (90 or above). The same creatinine in a smaller or older person gives a lower estimate — which is the point of adjusting.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
