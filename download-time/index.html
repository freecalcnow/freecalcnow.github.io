Download Time Calculator

Files are measured in bytes and speeds in bits, and real connections lose about 15% to overhead — the two reasons downloads always take longer than the napkin maths. This shows the theoretical time and the realistic one.

Seconds = file size (GB) × 8000 ÷ speed (Mbps) · realistic time adds 15% overhead
