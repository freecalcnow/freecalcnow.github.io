Antifreeze / Coolant Mix Calculator

Coolant concentrate protects in proportion to its strength — but stronger is not better: past about 70% the freeze protection actually gets worse. Enter your system's capacity and the cold you must survive for the exact concentrate and water split.

Ethylene-glycol protection: 25% ≈ −12 °C · 33% ≈ −20 °C · 40% ≈ −25 °C · 50% ≈ −37 °C · 60% ≈ −52 °C · Never exceed ~70% — protection falls and heat transfer suffers

Worked example

Worked example: a 6-litre system facing −25 °C nights needs a 40% mix — 2.4 litres of concentrate and 3.6 litres of deionised water. The popular 50/50 mix (3 L + 3 L) protects to about −37 °C and is the default for most climates.
