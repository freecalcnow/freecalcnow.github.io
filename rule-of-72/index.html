Rule of 72 Calculator

The Rule of 72 is the fastest mental maths in finance: divide 72 by your yearly growth rate and you get roughly how many years money takes to double. This tool shows the rule’s answer next to the exact one, so you can see how good the shortcut is.

Years to double ≈ 72 ÷ rate % · Exact: ln(2) ÷ ln(1 + rate) · Rate to double in N years ≈ 72 ÷ N

Worked example

Worked example: at 8% a year the rule says 9 years to double — and the exact answer is 9.01 years. At 3% the rule says 24 years; the exact answer is 23.4.
