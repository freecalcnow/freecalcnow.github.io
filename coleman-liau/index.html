Coleman–Liau Index Calculator

Coleman and Liau designed their 1975 index for machines: it counts characters instead of syllables, so it can be computed exactly. Enter a text's letters, words and sentences — the US school-grade estimate follows.

Index = 0.0588 × L − 0.296 × S − 15.8 · L = letters per 100 words, S = sentences per 100 words
