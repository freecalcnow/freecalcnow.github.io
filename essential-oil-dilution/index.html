Essential Oil Dilution Calculator

Essential oils go on skin diluted or not at all. The maths is drops and millilitres: roughly 20 drops make a millilitre, so a 2% dilution in a 30 ml bottle is 12 drops. This tool works it out for any bottle and any strength, with the standard safety bands built in.

Oil needed (ml) = bottle size × dilution % · Drops = oil ml × 20 (standard dropper) · Face 0.5–1% · Body 2–3% · Short-term targeted use up to 5%

Worked example

Worked example: a 30 ml roller bottle at a 2% body dilution needs 0.6 ml of essential oil — 12 drops — topped up with 29.4 ml of carrier oil. A face blend at 1% in the same bottle is just 6 drops.
