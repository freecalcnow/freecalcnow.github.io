Marks Percentage Calculator — Percentage & Grade from Marks

How many percent did you get — and which grade? Enter the marks you obtained and the total marks. This works out your percentage, your grade, how many more marks you need for the next grade, and how many you need to pass.

Percentage = (marks obtained ÷ total marks) × 100. Grade: 90+ A+, 80+ A, 70+ B, 60+ C, 50+ D, below 50 F.

Worked example

450 marks out of 500 is 90% — Grade A+. You already have the top grade. If you had scored 430 out of 500 (86%), the answer would be Grade A, and you would need 20 more marks for an A+.

How to use

  1. Enter the marks you obtained.
  2. Enter the total marks for the exam.
  3. Read your percentage, your grade, and what you need for the next grade.

Frequently asked questions

What grade bands do you use?

90% and above is A+, 80% A, 70% B, 60% C, 50% D, below 50% F. It is the most common scale — but your school or board may use different bands.

Can I use this for one subject or the whole exam?

Both. Put in the marks for one subject to see that subject's percentage, or the total of all subjects to see your overall result.

What if my total is not 500?

Any total works — 100, 850, 1,100. The percentage is always marks obtained divided by total marks, times 100.
