Wine Acid Adjustment Calculator

Raising acidity is simple arithmetic once you know the gap: each gram per litre of tartaric acid added raises titratable acidity by about 1 g/L (expressed as tartaric). Enter current and target TA and your volume for the exact addition.

Tartaric acid (g) = (target TA − current TA) in g/L × volume in litres

Worked example

Worked example: 100 litres at 5.5 g/L TA targeting 7.0 → gap 1.5 g/L × 100 = 150 g of tartaric acid.
