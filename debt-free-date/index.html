Debt-Free Date Calculator

Enter a balance, its interest rate and what you actually pay each month, and this counts forward month by month to the exact payoff date — and shows the total interest the debt will have cost you by then.

Each month: interest = balance × APR ÷ 12, then balance falls by (payment − interest), until it reaches zero

Worked example

Worked example: $8,500 at 19.99% APR paid at $300 a month clears in about 39 months, with roughly $3,100 of interest paid along the way.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
