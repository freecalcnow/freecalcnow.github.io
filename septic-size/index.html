Septic Tank Size Calculator

Septic tanks are sized by bedrooms (a proxy for occupants) and the daily wastewater flow they must settle. Most codes start at 750–1,000 gallons for a small home and step up with bedrooms — this applies the standard table and shows the flow behind it.

Design flow ≈ 75 gallons per person per day · Standard minimums: 1–2 bedrooms 750 gal · 3 BR 1,000 · 4 BR 1,250 · 5 BR 1,500 · 6 BR 1,750 (typical US code table)

Worked example

Worked example: a 3-bedroom home → 1,000-gallon minimum tank; at 4 occupants × 75 gallons that is 300 gallons a day of design flow, giving over 3 days of settling retention.
