Catalan Number Calculator

Catalan numbers count an astonishing family of structures: the ways to bracket a product, triangulate a polygon, build a full binary tree, or walk a grid path that never dips below the diagonal. If a counting problem's answer starts 1, 1, 2, 5, 14, 42 — it is Catalan.

C(n) = (2n)! ÷ ((n + 1)! × n!) = C(2n, n) ÷ (n + 1)

Worked example

Worked example: C(6) = 132 — there are 132 ways to fully bracket a product of 7 factors, and 132 binary trees with 7 leaves' worth of structure.
