CSS Swim Pace Calculator

Critical Swim Speed is the swimming twin of the cyclist's FTP: the fastest pace you can sustain aerobically, found from two maximal time trials — a 400 and a 200. Swim both with a short rest, enter the times, and get your CSS pace per 100 m, the number CSS training sets are built around.

CSS pace per 100 m = (T400 − T200) ÷ 2, in seconds · CSS speed = 200 ÷ (T400 − T200) m/s

Worked example

Worked example: a 400 m trial in 6:00 and a 200 m trial in 2:40 → (360 − 160) ÷ 2 = 100 seconds per 100 m — a CSS pace of 1:40/100 m, or about 1:31 per 100 yards.
