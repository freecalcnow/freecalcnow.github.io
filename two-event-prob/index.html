Two-Event Probability Calculator

For two events, three questions cover most of life: does at least one happen (union), do both happen (intersection), does neither happen? This tool answers all three, for independent events or when you already know the overlap.

Independent: P(A∩B) = P(A) × P(B) · Always: P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B) · P(neither) = 1 − P(A∪B)
