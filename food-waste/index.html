Food Waste Cost Calculator

Food waste is a weekly habit with a yearly bill. Enter what your household throws away in a typical week — money and weight — and see the honest annual totals, including the emissions embedded in food that was grown, shipped and never eaten.

Yearly = weekly × 52 · CO₂ ≈ food waste (kg) × 2.5 kg CO₂e per kg (a widely used average factor)

Worked example

Worked example: $25 and 2 kg wasted a week → $1,300 and 104 kg a year, carrying roughly 260 kg of CO₂e — before landfill methane makes it worse.
