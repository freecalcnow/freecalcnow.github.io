Specific Heat Calculator

Warming things takes a predictable amount of energy: mass × the material's specific heat capacity × the temperature rise. Water's value is famously huge — which is why the sea moderates climates and kettles take minutes, not seconds.

Q = m × c × ΔT · c(water) = 4,186 J/(kg·°C)

Worked example

Worked example: 1 kg of water warmed by 10 °C needs 1 × 4,186 × 10 = 41,860 J — about 10 kcal.
