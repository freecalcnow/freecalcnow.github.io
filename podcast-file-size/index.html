Podcast File Size Calculator

Podcast hosts cap storage and bandwidth, and both are just bitrate × time. Enter the episode length and export bitrate for the exact MP3 size, plus a weekly show's monthly and yearly footprint.

Size (MB) = bitrate (kbps) × seconds ÷ 8 ÷ 1000

Worked example

Worked example: a 60-minute episode at 96 kbps → 96 × 3600 ÷ 8 ÷ 1000 = 43.2 MB. A weekly show at that size uses about 2.25 GB a year.
