Spearman Rank Correlation Calculator

Pearson measures straight lines; Spearman measures 'when one goes up, does the other tend to go up?' — by correlating the ranks. It shrugs off outliers and bent relationships that fool Pearson. Paste two lists in matching order.

ρ = Pearson correlation of the ranks (average ranks for ties)
