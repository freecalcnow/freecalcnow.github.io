Bishop Score Calculator

Before inducing labour, the Bishop score grades the cervix on five findings — dilation, effacement, station, consistency and position. A score of 8 or more behaves like spontaneous labour; 6 or less says the cervix needs ripening first.

Dilation 0–3 · Effacement 0–3 · Station 0–3 · Consistency 0–2 · Position 0–2 · Total ≥ 8 favourable · ≤ 6 unfavourable (some pathways use ≤ 5)

Worked example

Worked example: a cervix 3 cm dilated (2), 60% effaced (2), at station −1 (2), medium consistency (1) and mid-position (1) scores 8 — favourable: induction is likely to succeed without ripening.
