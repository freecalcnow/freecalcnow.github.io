Primer Melting Temperature (Tm) Calculator

Before a PCR run, the lab checks that primers will grip their target at the chosen temperature. For short primers the Wallace rule is the classic back-of-envelope estimate: each A or T contributes 2°C, each G or C contributes 4°C, because GC pairs hold on with three hydrogen bonds instead of two.

Tm ≈ 2°C × (A + T count) + 4°C × (G + C count) — the Wallace rule, for primers under about 20 bases

Worked example

Worked example: the 12-base primer ATGCGTACGTAG has six A/T and six G/C bases: Tm = 2 × 6 + 4 × 6 = 36°C.
