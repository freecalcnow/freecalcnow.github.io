Stair Calculator

Stairs are a division problem with comfort rules: split the total rise into equal risers, and the steps build themselves. This tool also checks the old 2×riser + tread ≈ 63 cm comfort rule.

Risers = round(total rise ÷ target riser) · Actual riser = total rise ÷ risers · Treads = risers − 1 · Run = treads × tread depth

Worked example

Worked example: a 260 cm floor-to-floor rise with a 17.5 cm target riser → 15 risers of 17.3 cm, 14 treads of 28 cm, a total run of 392 cm, and a comfort check of 62.7 cm — right in the comfortable band.
