Normal Distribution Between Calculator

Most 'what are the chances it lands between X and Y' questions about measurements — heights, errors, test scores — are areas under the normal curve. Enter the mean, the spread and the two bounds.

z = (value − mean) ÷ standard deviation · P(between) = Φ(z high) − Φ(z low)

Worked example

Worked example: mean 100, SD 15, between 85 and 115 → z-scores of −1 and +1 → 68.27% — the famous 'within one standard deviation' share.
