Cost of Smoking Calculator

Cigarettes are priced to feel small — a pack here, a pack there. This adds the habit up the way your bank account experiences it: the yearly bill, the decade total, the sheer count of cigarettes, and what the same money would have become if it had been invested instead.

Yearly cost = cigarettes per day ÷ pack size × pack price × 365 · Invested value = the yearly cost grown as a yearly investment

Worked example

Worked example: 10 cigarettes a day at $8 a pack of 20 → $4 a day, $1,460 a year, $14,600 over 10 years — 36,500 cigarettes. Invested at 7% instead, the same habit money becomes about $20,200.
