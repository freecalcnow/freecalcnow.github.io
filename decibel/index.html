Decibel Calculator

Decibels are logarithmic: +10 dB means ten times the intensity, +3 dB roughly double. This tool converts between sound intensity and dB, and between two levels — the maths behind every noise chart.

Level (dB) = 10 × log₁₀(intensity ÷ 10⁻¹² W/m²) · A difference of ΔdB = intensity ratio 10^(Δ/10)

Worked example

Worked example: 10⁻⁶ W/m² = 60 dB — normal conversation. A jet at 130 dB is not 'twice as loud' as 65 dB; it carries about 3 million times the intensity.
