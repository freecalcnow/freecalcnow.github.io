3×3 System of Equations Solver

Three planes meet at one point — or they don't, and the determinant tells you which before you waste an hour. Enter the coefficients of three equations in x, y and z and get the exact meeting point, or a straight answer that there isn't one.

a₁x + b₁y + c₁z = d₁ (and two more) · Solved by Cramer's rule: x = det X ÷ det, y = det Y ÷ det, z = det Z ÷ det · det = 0 means no unique solution

Worked example

Worked example: x + y + z = 6, 2x − y + z = 3 and x + 2y − z = 2 meet at exactly (1, 2, 3) — check: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6, 2 − 2 + 3 = 3, 1 + 4 − 3 = 2.
