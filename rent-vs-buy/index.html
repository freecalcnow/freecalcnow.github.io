Rent vs Buy Calculator

The honest comparison is not rent against the mortgage payment — it is everything: rent rises, taxes, insurance, maintenance and selling costs on one side; equity and appreciation on the other. This tool totals both over your stay.

Renting cost = sum of monthly rent growing yearly · Buying net cost = all payments + tax + insurance + maintenance + selling cost (6%) − sale proceeds after repaying the loan

Worked example

Worked example: $1,800 rent rising 3% a year vs a $400,000 home, 20% down, 6.5% for 30 years, sold after 7 years at 3% yearly appreciation — buying usually pulls ahead around year 5–7 in this setup; change the stay to 3 years and renting wins clearly.
