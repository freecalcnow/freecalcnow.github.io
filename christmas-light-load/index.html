Christmas Lights Load Calculator

Holiday lighting trips breakers by accumulation: each string is small, the chain is not. This totals watts and amps for your display and prices a season of evenings.

Amps = total watts ÷ volts · Keep a circuit under 80% of its rating · Season cost = kW × hours × price
