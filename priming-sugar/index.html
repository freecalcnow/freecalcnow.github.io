Priming Sugar Calculator

Beer leaves the fermenter already holding dissolved CO₂ — how much depends on its warmest temperature since fermentation. Priming sugar only needs to supply the gap to your target volumes. This uses the standard residual-CO₂ curve and per-sugar yields.

Sugar (g) = (target − residual) volumes × grams per litre per volume × beer litres

Worked example

Worked example: 20 L of beer at 20°C (residual 0.86 vol) primed to 2.4 vol with sucrose → (2.4 − 0.86) × 4.0 × 20 = 123 g.
