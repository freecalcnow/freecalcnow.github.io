Moving Average Calculator

A moving average smooths a jagged series by averaging each point with its neighbours: the simple version (SMA) weights the window equally, the exponential version (EMA) lets recent points count more, which is why traders watch EMAs cross. Paste a series and a window and see both, plus the full SMA series.

SMA = mean of the last w values · EMA: next = price × k + previous EMA × (1 − k), with k = 2 ÷ (w + 1)

Worked example

Worked example: the series 10, 12, 14, 13, 15, 18 with a window of 3 gives SMA readings 12, 13, 14, 15.33 — the latest is 15.33. The EMA, seeded at 10, ends at 15.94, sitting higher because it weights the recent climb more.
