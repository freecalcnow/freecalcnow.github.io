Concentration Converter (ppm / mg/L / %)

For dilute water-based solutions, ppm and mg/L are the same number wearing two hats, and percent is that number divided by 10,000. This converter moves between all three without the decimal-place accidents that ruin batches.

1% = 10,000 ppm = 10,000 mg/L (for water-density solutions) · mg/L = ppm · % = ppm ÷ 10,000

Worked example

Worked example: 250 ppm = 250 mg/L = 0.025%. And pool chlorine at 3 ppm is 3 mg/L — a 0.0003% solution, which is why ppm exists.
