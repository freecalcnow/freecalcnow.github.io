Hypergeometric Calculator

When you draw without putting things back, the odds change with every draw — that is the hypergeometric distribution. It answers the classic questions: two aces in a five-card hand, three defectives in a sample of ten, your numbers among the drawn balls.

P(X = k) = C(K, k) × C(N − K, n − k) ÷ C(N, n) · Mean = n × K ÷ N

Worked example

Worked example: a 52-card deck (N) holds 4 aces (K). In a 5-card hand (n), the chance of exactly 2 aces (k) is C(4,2) × C(48,3) ÷ C(52,5) = 3.99% — and the chance of at least 2 is 4.17%.
