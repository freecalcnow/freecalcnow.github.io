Home Loan Eligibility Calculator

Lenders cap the share of your income that can go to EMIs — the Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio, usually 40–60% once existing EMIs are counted. Whatever EMI room is left converts into a loan amount at the rate and tenure you choose.

EMI room = income × FOIR% − existing EMIs · Loan = EMI room × [(1 + i)^n − 1] ÷ [i × (1 + i)^n]
