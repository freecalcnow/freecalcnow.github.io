Words to Pages Calculator

'Write 2,000 words' — but how long is that on paper? It depends on spacing and font, which is why this tool asks. Standard academic settings (Times 12, double-spaced) fit about 250 words a page.

Pages = words ÷ words-per-page · 250 (double-spaced), 375 (1.5 spacing), 500 (single-spaced) at 12pt

Worked example

Worked example: 60,000 words — a short novel — is 240 pages double-spaced, 120 pages single-spaced. A 1,000-word essay is 4 pages double-spaced.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
