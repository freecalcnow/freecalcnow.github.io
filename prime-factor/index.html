Prime Factor Calculator

Every whole number is built from primes multiplied together. Enter a number and get its prime factorisation, whether it is prime itself, and how many divisors it has — the facts behind fractions, LCM and half of number theory.

n = p₁^a₁ × p₂^a₂ × … · Number of divisors = (a₁ + 1)(a₂ + 1)…

Worked example

Worked example: 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5, so it has (3+1)(2+1)(1+1) = 24 divisors. And 97 is prime — its only factorisation is itself.
