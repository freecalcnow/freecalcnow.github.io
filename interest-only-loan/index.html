Interest-Only Loan Calculator

An interest-only loan keeps payments low by never touching the principal — until it must be repaid at once or amortised over the remaining term. This shows both phases honestly.

Interest-only payment = principal × rate ÷ 12 · Follow-on payment = amortisation over the remaining years (or the full principal as a balloon)
