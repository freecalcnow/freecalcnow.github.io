Concrete Steps Calculator

A solid concrete step is a stack of boxes — each step adds its own riser-height box across the full width. Summing the stack gives the pour volume; the bags and the 10% waste allowance follow.

Volume = width × tread × riser × (1 + 2 + … + steps) ÷ 1,000,000 (cm inputs)
