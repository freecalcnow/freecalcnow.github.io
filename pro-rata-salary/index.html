Pro-Rata Salary Calculator

Job ads quote the full-time salary; your contract pays a fraction of it. This scales a full-time salary to your hours exactly as payroll does — by the FTE (full-time equivalent) fraction.

FTE = your hours ÷ full-time hours · Pro-rata salary = full-time salary × FTE

Worked example

Worked example: a 52,000 full-time role at 40 hours, worked at 24 hours → FTE 0.6, so the pro-rata salary is 31,200 a year — 2,600 a month, at the same 25 an hour.

How is this calculated?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
