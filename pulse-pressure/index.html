Pulse Pressure Calculator

Blood pressure is quoted as two numbers, but the difference between them is a measurement in its own right. A pulse pressure around 40 mmHg is typical; a persistently wide gap (over 60) is linked with stiffer arteries, and a narrow one (under 30) can signal the heart is struggling to fill or pump.

Pulse pressure = systolic − diastolic · MAP = diastolic + pulse pressure ÷ 3
