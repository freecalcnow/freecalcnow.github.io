Tax-Equivalent Yield Calculator

A municipal bond paying 4% tax-free can beat a taxable bond paying 5% — it depends entirely on your tax bracket. Tax-equivalent yield converts the tax-free yield into the taxable yield you would need to match it, so the comparison is honest.

Tax-equivalent yield = tax-free yield ÷ (1 − your combined tax rate)

Worked example

Worked example: a 4% muni for someone in a combined 29% bracket (24% federal + 5% state) has a tax-equivalent yield of 5.63% — a taxable bond must pay more than that to win.
