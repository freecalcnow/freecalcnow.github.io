Bike Frame Size Calculator

Frame sizing starts at the inseam: road frames run about 0.685 × inseam in centimetres. Mountain frames size smaller for clearance. This gives both, in centimetres and inches, with the usual letter sizes.

Road frame (cm) ≈ inseam (cm) × 0.685 · MTB ≈ road − 10 cm

Worked example

Worked example: an 84 cm inseam → road frame 57.5 cm (a 57 or 58), mountain frame about 47.5 cm ≈ 19 inches (large).
