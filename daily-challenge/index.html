How the challenge works

  1. Play all three daily puzzles — Daily Number, Daily Word Guess and Daily Sudoku.
  2. Each puzzle you finish counts toward the same streak. Miss a day and the streak starts again at 1.
  3. Copy your score link and send it to a friend. When they open it, this page shows both scores side by side — fewest tries wins.
  4. Come back tomorrow: new puzzles, same streak.
