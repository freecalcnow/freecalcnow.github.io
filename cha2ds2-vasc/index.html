CHA₂DS₂-VASc Score Calculator

In atrial fibrillation the heart's irregular beat lets clots form. CHA₂DS₂-VASc adds up eight risk factors — heart failure, blood pressure, age, diabetes, past stroke, vascular disease and sex — to estimate the yearly stroke risk and guide prevention.

Points: heart failure 1 · hypertension 1 · age 75+ 2 · diabetes 1 · stroke/TIA before 2 · vascular disease 1 · age 65–74 1 · female sex 1 (maximum 9)
