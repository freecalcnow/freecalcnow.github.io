Paycheck Calculator

The full paycheck, line by line: gross pay per period, pre-tax retirement, federal tax, state tax, Social Security and Medicare (FICA) — then what actually lands in your account. You set your own federal and state rates from a recent payslip, so the estimate matches your situation instead of a generic table.

Net = Gross per period − retirement − federal − state − Social Security (6.2% up to the wage base) − Medicare (1.45%) − other deductions

Frequently asked questions

Why enter my own federal and state rates?

Real withholding depends on your W-4, state, allowances and year — a fixed table would be wrong for most people. Your effective rate from a real payslip (tax divided by gross) is the honest shortcut.

What is FICA?

The US payroll tax funding Social Security and Medicare: 6.2% + 1.45% = 7.65% for most employees, matched by the employer. Self-employed workers pay both halves (15.3%) — if that is you, add the two halves into the rates above.

Why is my first paycheck of the year different?

Social Security stops once your wages pass the yearly wage base, and some deductions are per-period flat amounts. This tool spreads the year evenly, which is why it is labelled an estimator.
