HEART Score Calculator (Chest Pain)

HEART scores five things — History, ECG, Age, Risk factors and Troponin — at 0, 1 or 2 points each. The total separates chest-pain patients into a low-risk group with about a 2% chance of a major cardiac event in six weeks, and a high-risk group where roughly half will have one.

Each of History, ECG, Age, Risk factors, Troponin scores 0–2 · 0–3 low risk (≈2% MACE) · 4–6 moderate (≈17%) · 7–10 high (≈50%)

Worked example

Worked example: a 58-year-old (1) with a moderately suspicious history (1), a normal ECG (0), three risk factors (2) and a normal troponin (0) scores 4 — moderate risk: admission and observation rather than discharge, despite the reassuring blood test.
