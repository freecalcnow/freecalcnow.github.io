Sweat Rate Calculator

Your sweat rate is the missing number in every hydration plan: weigh yourself before and after training, add back what you drank, and the difference is what you sweated. Replacing about 80% of it during long sessions keeps performance from sliding.

Sweat (L) = (weight before − after, kg) + fluid drunk (L) · Rate = sweat ÷ hours

Worked example

Worked example: 80 kg before, 79 kg after, 0.5 L drunk in 1 hour → 1.5 L of sweat, a rate of 1.5 L/h.
