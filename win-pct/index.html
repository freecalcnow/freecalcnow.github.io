Winning Percentage Calculator

Standings percentages count a tie as half a win — which is why a 40–20–0 team and a 35–15–10 team can sit level. Enter the record and get the number the table prints.

Win % = (wins + 0.5 × ties) ÷ (wins + losses + ties) × 100
