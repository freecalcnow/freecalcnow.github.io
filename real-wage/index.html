Real Wage Calculator

A 5% raise in a 7% inflation year is a pay cut wearing a costume. This converts an old salary into today's money at the inflation you enter, then shows whether the new salary actually kept up, and by how much.

Old salary in today's money = old salary × (1 + inflation)^years · Real change = new ÷ adjusted old − 1

Worked example

Worked example: 50,000 five years ago, 56,000 now, inflation averaging 3.5% → the old pay is worth 59,384 today, so the 'rise' is really a 5.7% cut in buying power.
