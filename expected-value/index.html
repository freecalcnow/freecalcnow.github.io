Expected Value Calculator

The expected value is the long-run average of a random outcome: each value weighted by its probability. This tool also gives E(X²), the variance and the standard deviation — the full shape of the gamble, not just its centre.

E(X) = Σ x·p(x) · Var(X) = E(X²) − E(X)²
