Pan Size Converter

Batter does not care about a pan's name, only its area: swap an 8-inch round for a 9-inch and the same batter sits shallower and bakes faster. This tool gives the area ratio between common pans and scales your batter amount by it.

Scale = area of new pan ÷ area of recipe pan (round: πr² · square/rectangle: l × w)
