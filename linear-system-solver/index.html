System of Equations Solver (2×2)

Two lines on a graph meet at exactly one point — unless they are parallel (no solution) or the same line twice (infinitely many). This solves a₁x + b₁y = c₁ and a₂x + b₂y = c₂ and tells you which case you are in.

Determinant D = a₁b₂ − a₂b₁ · x = (c₁b₂ − c₂b₁) ÷ D · y = (a₁c₂ − a₂c₁) ÷ D
