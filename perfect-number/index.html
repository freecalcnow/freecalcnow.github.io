Perfect Number Checker

Add up a number's proper divisors — every factor except itself. If the sum equals the number, it is perfect (6 = 1 + 2 + 3). If the sum is bigger, the number is abundant; smaller, deficient. Perfect numbers are vanishingly rare and linked to Mersenne primes; only 51 are known.

Compare n with σ(n) − n, the sum of its proper divisors
