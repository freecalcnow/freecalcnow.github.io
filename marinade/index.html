Marinade Amount Calculator

Too little marinade and half the meat stays dry; too much and you have soup. The working rule is about 120 ml per kg of meat — enough to coat every surface in a snug bag or dish. This tool totals it and splits the classic 3:1 oil-to-acid base.

Marinade = meat (kg) × ml per kg · Oil = ¾ of the base · Acid = ¼ · Salt ≈ 1.5% of the marinade weight

Worked example

Worked example: 2 kg of meat at 120 ml/kg → 240 ml of marinade: 180 ml oil, 60 ml acid (vinegar, citrus or wine), about 3.6 g of salt, plus your aromatics.
