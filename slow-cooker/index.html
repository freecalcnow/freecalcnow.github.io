Slow Cooker Conversion Calculator

Slow cookers trade in two gears and the conversion is a rule of thumb every manufacturer prints: low takes roughly twice high, and both map to a modest oven. Enter the time on one setting for the equivalents — with the honest range, because pot size and fullness move it.

LOW time ≈ 2 × HIGH time · Oven (325°F) time ≈ half the HIGH time for braises

Worked example: a recipe saying 4 hours on HIGH → about 8 hours on LOW (7–9 in practice), or roughly 2 hours in a 325°F oven.
