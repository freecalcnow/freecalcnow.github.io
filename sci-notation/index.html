Scientific Notation Converter

Very large and very small numbers are written as a mantissa times a power of ten: 3.0 × 10⁸ for the speed of light. Convert an ordinary number into that form, or build one from a mantissa and exponent.

Scientific notation = m × 10^e, with 1 ≤ |m| < 10 · E notation writes it as mEe

Worked example

Worked example: 299,792,458 → 2.99792458 × 10⁸. And 4.2 × 10⁻³ → 0.0042. The exponent counts how far the decimal point moves.
