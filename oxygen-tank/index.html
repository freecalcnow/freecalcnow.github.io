Oxygen Cylinder Duration Calculator

Oxygen cylinders empty silently. The maths is simple — cylinder factor times the pressure on the gauge, divided by the flow — but it must be done before the ambulance doors close, with a safety reserve, not discovered on the motorway.

Minutes = (gauge pressure − safe residual) × cylinder factor ÷ flow · Factors (L per psi): D ≈ 0.16 · E ≈ 0.28 · M ≈ 1.56 · Keep a 200 psi residual

Worked example

Worked example: an E cylinder reading 1,800 psi at 4 litres a minute: (1,800 − 200) × 0.28 ÷ 4 = 112 minutes of usable oxygen. The same maths at 15 L/min on a non-rebreather gives under 30 minutes — plan the journey accordingly.
