Five-Number Summary Calculator

Five numbers that sketch a whole data set: the extremes, the middle, and the two quartiles that bound the middle half. They are everything a box plot draws — and they resist outliers far better than a mean does.

Quartiles by the median-of-halves (Tukey) method · IQR = Q3 − Q1 · Range = max − min
