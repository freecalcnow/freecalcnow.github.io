Circle Equation Calculator

A circle is one fact — every point is the same distance from the centre — wearing two algebraic costumes: the standard form that shows the centre and radius at a glance, and the general form your textbook questions arrive in. Enter the centre and radius and get both, with area and circumference alongside.

Standard form: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r² · General form: x² + y² + Dx + Ey + F = 0 with D = −2h, E = −2k, F = h² + k² − r²

Worked example

Worked example: centre (2, −3), radius 5 → standard form (x − 2)² + (y + 3)² = 25, general form x² + y² − 4x + 6y − 12 = 0. The point (2, 2) sits exactly 5 from the centre — on the circle.
