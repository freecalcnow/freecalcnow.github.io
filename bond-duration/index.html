Bond Duration (Macaulay) Calculator

Duration answers two questions at once: the weighted average time until a bond pays you back, and — more usefully — roughly how much its price moves when interest rates move. A duration of 7 means about a 7% price swing for a 1% rate change.

Macaulay duration = Σ (t × PV of cash flow at t) ÷ bond price · Modified duration = Macaulay ÷ (1 + yield ÷ payments per year)

Worked example

Worked example: a $1,000 bond paying 5% twice a year, 10 years to run, yielding 6% → priced at about $925.61, Macaulay duration about 7.89 years, modified duration about 7.67 — so a 1% rise in yields knocks roughly 7.7% off the price.
