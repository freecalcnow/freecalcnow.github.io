Staking Rewards Calculator

Staking pays a percentage on coins you lock with a network or platform. This compounds the APY you enter at the frequency you pick and shows the rewards in coins and in value — at today's price, which is the catch explained below.

Final = principal × (1 + APY ÷ n)^(n × years)

Worked example: 5,000 staked at 8% APY for 3 years, compounded yearly → 6,298.56 — rewards of 1,298.56 before any price movement.
