Money tools made for Pakistan — in rupees, with the things Pakistanis actually look up: Zakat, freelancer pay in PKR, what your salary is worth abroad, and what your money bought years ago.

Money in Pakistan — the short version

  • Currency: Pakistani Rupee (PKR) — $1 is about Rs 277 (live rate in the currency converter).
  • Zakat is 2.5% of savings held for a full lunar year above the nisab — work it out in the Zakat calculator.
  • Freelancers here are usually paid in dollars and convert to PKR — the Freelancer Rate Calculator takes off the platform fee first.
  • Petrol is Rs 322 a litre (19 Nov 2026, OGRA) — see the daily money lines for today's dollar, gold and petrol.
  • No Pakistan income-tax calculator on the site yet — request it here and vote for it.
