PPI Calculator (Pixel Density)

PPI — pixels per inch — is the honest sharpness number behind marketing words like 'Retina'. It combines the resolution and the physical size: the same 4K panel is razor sharp at 27 inches and merely good at 55.

PPI = √(width² + height²) ÷ diagonal inches · physical width = diagonal × (w ÷ √(w² + h²))
