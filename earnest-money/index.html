Earnest Money Calculator

Earnest money is the deposit a buyer hands over with an accepted offer to show they are serious — typically 1–3% of the price, more in hot markets. It is not an extra cost: at closing it becomes part of your down payment.

Earnest money = purchase price × earnest % · Still needed for the down payment = down payment − earnest money

Worked example

Worked example: a $400,000 home with 3% earnest money → $12,000 deposit. Against a 20% down payment of $80,000, that leaves $68,000 more due at closing (plus closing costs).
