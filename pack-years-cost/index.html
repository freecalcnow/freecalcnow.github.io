Pack Years & Smoking Cost Calculator

Doctors measure smoking exposure in pack-years: packs per day multiplied by years smoked. This tool computes yours, the common screening threshold, and the money side — yearly cost and the decade figure invested instead.

Pack-years = packs per day × years smoked · 1 pack = 20 cigarettes
