Bonus Tax Calculator

In the US, bonuses are usually withheld at a flat 22% federal rate, plus 7.65% Social Security and Medicare, plus state tax where it applies. The withholding is not the final tax — but this is the number that lands in your account.

Take-home = bonus × (1 − federal % − Social Security/Medicare % − state %)

Worked example

Worked example: a $10,000 bonus at 22% federal, 7.65% FICA and 5% state → $3,465 withheld, $6,535 in your account.
