Scale Converter (Maps & Models)

A scale like 1:50,000 means one unit on the paper is 50,000 of the same unit in the real world. Hikers, architects and model-makers all live by it — this converts in both directions and can also work out the scale itself from a known pair.

Real distance = measured distance × scale denominator · Scale = real ÷ measured (same units)

Worked example

Worked example: 6 cm on a 1:50,000 map = 6 × 50,000 cm = 3 km on the ground — about 1.86 miles.
