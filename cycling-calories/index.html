Cycling Power to Calories Calculator

A power meter measures the mechanical work you actually did, in kilojoules. Because human pedalling efficiency is roughly 20–25%, the food calories burned come out close to the kilojoule count — this tool does the exact conversion at your chosen efficiency.

Mechanical kJ = watts × seconds ÷ 1000 · kcal = kJ ÷ (efficiency × 4.184)
