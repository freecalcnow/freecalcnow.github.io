Investment Fee Impact Calculator

Fund fees look tiny — 0.15% versus 1% — but they are charged every year on the whole pot, so they compound against you exactly the way returns compound for you. This runs your savings at three fee levels and shows the gap.

Growth at (return − fee), compounded monthly, for both the starting pot and the monthly deposits

Worked example

Worked example: $50,000 plus $300 a month at 8% for 30 years grows to roughly $994,000 with no fee — and roughly $772,000 after a 1% yearly fee. The 'small' fee eats about $222,000.
