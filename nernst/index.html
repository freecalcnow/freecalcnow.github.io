Nernst Equation Calculator

The textbook cell voltage assumes everything is 1 mol/L. Real cells drift as they discharge — the Nernst equation tracks the actual voltage from the reaction quotient, which is why a dying battery sags gradually instead of stopping dead.

E = E° − (0.05916 ÷ n) × log₁₀(Q) at 25 °C, where n = electrons transferred and Q = reaction quotient

Worked example

Worked example: the Daniell cell (E° = 1.10 V, n = 2) with Q = 0.1 → E = 1.10 + 0.0296 = 1.13 V. With Q = 10 (products built up) → 1.07 V.
