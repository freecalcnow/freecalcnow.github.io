Coordinate Converter (DMS ↔ Decimal)

Maps and GPS speak two dialects: decimal degrees (51.5074) and degrees, minutes and seconds (51° 30′ 26.6″). Charts, older gazetteers and surveying use DMS; apps and databases use decimal. Pick a direction and convert — each minute is 1/60 of a degree, each second 1/3600.

Decimal = degrees + minutes ÷ 60 + seconds ÷ 3600 · And back: split the fraction by 60 twice

Worked example

Worked example: 51.5074° → 51° + 0.5074 × 60 = 51° 30.444′ → 51° 30′ 26.6″ (Greenwich-side London). Converting 51° 30′ 26.6″ back: 51 + 30/60 + 26.6/3600 = 51.5074°.
