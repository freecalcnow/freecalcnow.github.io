Hemocytometer Cell Counter

The hemocytometer's ruled chamber holds a known volume: each large square is exactly 10⁻⁴ mL. So the count in a square, scaled by the dilution, gives the concentration directly — the calculation every cell-culture lab does daily. Enter your average count per large square.

Cells/mL = average count per large square × dilution factor × 10,000

Worked example

Worked example: an average of 45 cells per large square at a 1:20 dilution → 45 × 20 × 10,000 = 9,000,000 cells/mL.
