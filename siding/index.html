Siding Calculator

Siding is sold by the 'square' — 100 square feet of coverage — a unit that confuses everyone exactly once. Measure the walls, subtract the openings, add waste for cuts, and this gives squares to order and the panel count for standard vinyl profiles.

Net area = length × height − openings · Squares = net area × (1 + waste %) ÷ 100 · Panels ≈ area with waste ÷ 8 sq ft per 12-ft panel (8-inch exposure)

Worked example

Worked example: a 40 ft wall, 9 ft high, with 40 sq ft of windows and doors → 360 − 40 = 320 sq ft net. With 10% waste that is 352 sq ft = 3.52 squares (order 4), or about 44 standard vinyl panels.
