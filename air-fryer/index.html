Air Fryer Conversion Calculator

An air fryer is a small, fierce fan oven: the standard conversion drops the temperature by about 25 °F (10–15 °C) and the time by about 20%. This tool converts any oven recipe — and reminds you to check early.

Air fryer temp = oven temp − 25 °F (≈ −10 °C in Celsius recipes) · Air fryer time = oven time × 0.8

Worked example

Worked example: a recipe at 400 °F for 30 minutes → air fry at 375 °F for about 24 minutes, checking from 20. In Celsius: 200 °C for 30 min → 190 °C for ~24 min.
