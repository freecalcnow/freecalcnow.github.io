Compare Fractions Calculator

Fractions resist eyeballing — is 5/7 really bigger than 7/10? This puts up to three fractions on a common denominator, ranks them, and shows the decimal and percentage of each so the order is beyond argument.

Compare a/b and c/d by cross-multiplying: a×d versus c×b

Worked example

Worked example: 3/4 (0.750) beats 5/7 (0.714) beats 2/3 (0.667) — cross-check: 3×7 = 21 against 5×4 = 20, so 3/4 wins by a whisker.
