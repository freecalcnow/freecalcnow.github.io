Pocket Money Planner — Daily Spend & Savings for Students

How much can you spend each day — and still save? Enter your monthly pocket money, how many days you spend it, and what you want to save each day. This works out your daily spend, your monthly and yearly savings, and what is left to spend after saving.

Daily spend = monthly pocket money ÷ days. Monthly saving = daily saving × days. Left to spend = monthly money − monthly saving.

Worked example

Rs 3,000 a month over 22 days is Rs 136 a day. Save Rs 50 a day and you save Rs 1,100 a month — Rs 13,200 in a year — and still have Rs 86 a day to spend.

How to use

  1. Enter your pocket money per month and pick your currency.
  2. Enter how many days a month you spend money.
  3. Enter what you want to save each day — and read your plan.

Frequently asked questions

What if I save more than I get?

Then the 'left to spend' goes below zero. Lower the daily saving, or ask for a little more pocket money — the planner shows you the honest number either way.

Should I save every day, even small amounts?

Yes — small and regular beats big and rare. Rs 50 a day is Rs 1,500 a month, and you hardly feel it.

What is the money for?

Anything — a new phone, a gift, a trip, or just the habit of saving. A goal makes it much easier to keep going.
