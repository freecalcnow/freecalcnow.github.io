Vacation Budget Calculator

Trip costs hide in five places at once: getting there, sleeping, eating, doing things and moving around. Enter each piece once and see the real total — plus the per-person and per-day figures that make two trips honestly comparable.

Total = flights + (hotel per night × nights) + (food per person per day × days × people) + activities + local transport

Worked example

Worked example: 2 people, flights $350 each, hotel $120 × 5 nights, food $60 per person per day, $300 activities, $100 local transport → 700 + 600 + 600 + 300 + 100 = $2,300 — $1,150 each, $460 a day.

How to use

  1. Type your numbers in the boxes above.
  2. Tap Calculate — or just type, the answer updates as you go.
  3. Read the big answer and the breakdown table below it.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
