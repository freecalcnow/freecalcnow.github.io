Countertop Square Footage Calculator

Countertops are priced by the square foot, and the measurement is simple: length of run times depth (standard counters are 25.5 inches deep, islands more). This tool totals it, with an L-shape second run if you have one.

Area = Σ (run length × depth ÷ 12) · Cost = area × price per sq ft
