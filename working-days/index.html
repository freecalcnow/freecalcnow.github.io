Working Days Calculator

Deadlines live in working days, not calendar days. This counts the Mondays-to-Fridays between two dates (both ends included), and shows the weekends hiding inside the span. Public holidays vary by country, so they are not deducted — check your local calendar for those.

Count every date from start to end inclusive whose weekday is Monday–Friday

Worked example: Monday 16 November 2026 to Friday 20 November 2026 = 5 working days and no weekend days — one clean working week.
