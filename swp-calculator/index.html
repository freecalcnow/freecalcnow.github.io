SWP Calculator — How Long a Pot Lasts

The mirror of a SIP: you have a pot, it keeps earning a return, and you take a fixed amount out every month. The race is between growth and withdrawals — this runs it month by month until the pot empties or 40 years pass.

Each month: pot = pot × (1 + r ÷ 12) − withdrawal · repeated until the pot runs out
