Pixels & DPI Size Calculator

An image's print size is its pixels divided by the print resolution. 300 DPI is photo quality, 150 DPI looks fine at arm's length, and this tool shows the size at all three standards at once.

Physical size = pixels ÷ DPI · At 300 DPI (photo), 150 DPI (poster), 100 DPI (banner)

Worked example

Worked example: a 6000 × 4000 photo at 300 DPI prints at 20 × 13.3 inches (50.8 × 33.9 cm) — a proper large print. At 150 DPI it stretches to 40 inches wide.

How is this calculated?
