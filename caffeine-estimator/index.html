Caffeine in Your Cup Calculator

'A coffee' is not a dose: a single espresso carries about 63 mg, a large filter coffee can pass 300. Enter the drink, the size and how many, for the real milligrams — and the share of the widely used 400 mg daily guide for healthy adults.

Typical caffeine: espresso 63 mg per shot · filter coffee ≈ 12 mg per 100 ml · instant ≈ 8 mg per 100 ml (varies widely) · black tea ≈ 5 mg per 100 ml · cola ≈ 10 mg per 100 ml · energy drinks 32 mg per 100 ml

Worked example

Worked example: two double espressos (4 shots ≈ 252 mg) plus a 330 ml cola (33 mg) is 285 mg — 71% of the 400 mg daily guide before any afternoon tea. The same count of 'coffees' as small instants might be half that.
