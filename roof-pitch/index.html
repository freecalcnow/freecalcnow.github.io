Roof Pitch Calculator

Roofers quote pitch as inches of rise per 12 inches of run; architects quote degrees; drainage specs quote percent. Enter rise and run in any unit — all three come back.

Pitch = rise ÷ run × 12 (per 12) · Angle = arctan(rise ÷ run) · Slope % = rise ÷ run × 100

Worked example

Worked example: a rise of 6 over a run of 12 → a 6/12 pitch, 26.6°, a 50% slope. Most shingle roofs sit between 4/12 and 9/12.
