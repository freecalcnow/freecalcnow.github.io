Lumpsum Growth Calculator

A lumpsum is the simplest investment there is: one amount, left alone to compound. This projects it year by year in spirit — final value, growth, the inflation-adjusted (real) value, and how long doubling takes at your rate.

Future value = amount × (1 + r)^years · Real value = FV ÷ (1 + inflation)^years · Doubling time = ln 2 ÷ ln(1 + r)

Worked example

Worked example: 500,000 at 12% for 10 years → 500,000 × 1.12^10 = about 1,552,924. At 6% inflation its real value is about 867,000 of today's money.
