Stair Calculator

Stairs are a comfort formula before they're carpentry: the Blondel rule (2 × riser + tread ≈ 63 cm) is the stride your legs expect. Give the floor-to-floor height; the calculator picks the step count that lands closest to a comfortable riser, then works out the tread, run and stringer angle.

Steps = round(total rise ÷ target riser) · Actual riser = total rise ÷ steps · Comfort: 2 × riser + tread ≈ 63 cm
