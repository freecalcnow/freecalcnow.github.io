Wine SO₂ Calculator

Only the molecular fraction of free SO₂ actually protects wine, and that fraction collapses as pH rises. Enter your wine's pH and target protection level for the free SO₂ you need — then the potassium metabisulfite (KMBS) addition for your volume.

Free SO₂ needed = target molecular × (1 + 10^(pH − 1.81)) · KMBS grams = free SO₂ (mg/L) × litres ÷ 570 (KMBS is ~57% SO₂)

Worked example

Worked example: a white wine at pH 3.5 targeting 0.8 mg/L molecular → free SO₂ = 0.8 × (1 + 10^1.69) ≈ 40 mg/L. For 100 litres that is 40 × 100 ÷ 570 ≈ 7.0 g of KMBS.
