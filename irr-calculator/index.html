IRR Calculator

IRR is the discount rate that makes a project's NPV exactly zero — the yearly return baked into its cash flows. This finds it numerically from your upfront cost and the flows that follow.

IRR is the rate r where −initial + Σ flow_t ÷ (1 + r)^t = 0 (solved by bisection)
