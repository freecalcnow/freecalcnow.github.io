Net Present Value (NPV) Calculator

NPV answers the investor's core question: is this stream of future money worth more than it costs today? Each future cash flow is discounted at your required rate of return; a positive total means the project creates value at that rate.

NPV = Σ cash flow at year t ÷ (1 + r)^t · the first figure is year 0 (usually the upfront cost, entered negative)

Worked example

Worked example: −$1,000 today then $500 a year for 3 years at 10% → NPV $243.43. Positive, so at a 10% required return the project pays.
