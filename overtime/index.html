Worked example

Worked example: $25 an hour, 40 regular hours plus 10 overtime hours at time-and-a-half (1.5×) = $1,000 + $375 = $1,375 for the week. Those 10 extra hours paid $37.50 each, not $25.

Frequently asked questions

Is overtime taxed differently?

In most systems overtime is taxed as ordinary income at your marginal rate — it can push some income into a higher band, but you never take home less by earning more.

My employer averages hours over two weeks — is that allowed?

It depends on jurisdiction and sector. This tool prices a single pay period; if your arrangement differs, check your contract or local labour rules.
