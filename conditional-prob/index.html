Conditional Probability Calculator

Conditioning is updating: once B is known to have happened, the world shrinks to the cases where B holds, and A's chance inside that smaller world is P(A given B).

P(A|B) = P(A∩B) ÷ P(B)

Worked example

Worked example: if A and B happen together 12% of the time and B happens 30% of the time, then P(A|B) = 12 ÷ 30 = 40%.
