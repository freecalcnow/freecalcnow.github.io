Six-Minute Walk Test (Predicted Distance)

The 6-minute walk test measures functional exercise capacity: how far someone walks on the flat in six minutes. The Enright equations predict the healthy reference distance from height, age and weight — the yardstick a patient's actual distance is judged against.

Men: 6MWD = 7.57×height(cm) − 5.02×age − 1.76×weight(kg) − 309 · Women: 2.11×height − 2.29×weight − 5.78×age + 667

Worked example

Worked example: a 60-year-old man, 170 cm, 75 kg → predicted 545 m. The lower limit of normal is about 153 m below that.
