Skewness Calculator

Skewness measures a distribution's lopsidedness: zero for a symmetric set, positive when a long tail stretches right (a few big values), negative when it stretches left. The sample formula below includes the standard small-sample correction used by Excel and SPSS.

g₁ = [√(n(n−1)) ÷ (n−2)] × [Σ((x − x̄)/s)³ ÷ n]
