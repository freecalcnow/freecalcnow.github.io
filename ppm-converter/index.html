PPM Converter

Concentrations get quoted in whatever unit makes the number look tidy: percent for strong solutions, ppm for traces, mg/L for water. They are all the same fraction wearing different scales — 1% is exactly 10,000 ppm.

ppm = percent × 10,000 · For dilute water solutions, 1 ppm ≈ 1 mg/L (because a litre of water masses about a kilogram)

Worked example

Worked example: 0.05% is 500 ppm — a number that reads as 'almost nothing' in percent and 'five hundred parts' in ppm, which is exactly why industries pick the unit that suits the message.
