Storage Cost Calculator

Drives are priced by the box and die by the year; the honest unit is cost per terabyte per year of service. Enter a drive's size, price and expected life and get the figures that make a 4 TB bargain and a 16 TB 'expensive' drive directly comparable.

Cost per TB-year = price ÷ (size in TB × years of life) · Per GB-month = price ÷ (GB × months of life)

Worked example

Worked example: a 4 TB drive at 100 lasting 5 years costs 100 ÷ 20 = 5 per TB per year — about 0.0004 per GB per month. A cloud plan at 10 a month for 2 TB costs 60 per TB-year: twelve times more, which is the price of their redundancy and your convenience.
