One-Way ANOVA Calculator

Comparing two groups takes a t-test; comparing three or more takes ANOVA. Paste each group's numbers (separate groups with a semicolon) and it splits the variation into 'between groups' and 'within groups' — if the between-group spread dwarfs the within-group noise, the F statistic and p-value say the difference is real.

F = (SSB ÷ (k − 1)) ÷ (SSW ÷ (N − k)) · SSB = Σ nᵢ(meanᵢ − grand mean)² · SSW = Σ Σ (x − meanᵢ)²

Worked example

Worked example: groups 4,5,6,5 / 7,8,6,7 / 5,5,4,6 → F = 8.00 with (2, 9) degrees of freedom, p ≈ 0.010 — a significant difference at the 5% level.
