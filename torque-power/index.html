Torque to Power Calculator

Torque is how hard an engine twists; power is how fast it can do it. The two are locked together by rpm in one equation — the reason a high-revving small engine can out-power a lazy big one, and why dyno curves always cross at 5,252 rpm in imperial units.

Power (W) = torque (N·m) × angular speed (rad/s) · Angular speed = rpm × 2π ÷ 60

Worked example

Worked example: 300 N·m at 4,000 rpm → angular speed about 418.9 rad/s, power about 125.7 kW — roughly 168.5 horsepower.
