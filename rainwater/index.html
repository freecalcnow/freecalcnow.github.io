Rainwater Harvesting Calculator

One millimetre of rain on one square metre of roof is one litre of water — the rest is just your roof, your rainfall and how much the gutters actually catch. The result often surprises people.

Litres per year = roof area (m²) × rainfall (mm) × runoff coefficient

Worked example

Worked example: a 100 m² roof with 800 mm of yearly rain and a 0.85 runoff coefficient collects 68,000 litres a year — about 186 litres a day, enough for a garden, toilets and laundry many times over.
