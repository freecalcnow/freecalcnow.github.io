Fantasy Football Points Calculator

Paste a player's stat line and get the fantasy score in your league's flavour: reception points (PPR) and passing-touchdown value are the two settings that change everything, so both are selectable. Standard yardage scoring throughout: 1 point per 25 passing yards, 1 per 10 rushing or receiving yards.

Points = pass yds × 0.04 + pass TD × (4 or 6) − INT × 2 + rush/rec yds × 0.1 + rush/rec TD × 6 + receptions × PPR − fumbles lost × 2

Worked example

Worked example: a quarterback with 300 passing yards, 3 passing TDs (at 4 points), 1 interception and 20 rushing yards → 12 + 12 − 2 + 2 = 24.0 points. In a 6-point passing-TD league the same game scores 30.0.
