Karvonen Heart Rate Zones Calculator

The Karvonen method improves on simple % of max: it works from your heart-rate reserve (max minus resting), so two people with the same max but different resting rates get different, fairer zones. Enter both numbers for all five zones.

Target HR = resting + intensity % × (max − resting)
