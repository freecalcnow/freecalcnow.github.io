Reading Level Calculator (Flesch–Kincaid)

Paste any text and get its Flesch Reading Ease (0–100, higher is easier) and Flesch–Kincaid grade level (the US school grade needed to follow it). Both scores come from sentence length and syllables per word — the same formulas built into word processors.

Reading Ease = 206.835 − 1.015 × (words ÷ sentences) − 84.6 × (syllables ÷ words) · Grade = 0.39 × (words ÷ sentences) + 11.8 × (syllables ÷ words) − 15.59

Worked example

Worked example: plain web writing usually lands at Reading Ease 60–70 and grade 7–9. Legal contracts often score under 30 — grade 14+ — which is why nobody reads them.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
