Daily Water Intake Calculator

The standard guide: about 35 mL of water per kilogram of body weight per day, plus roughly 350 mL for every half hour of exercise. Shown in litres, glasses and bottles so it is easy to actually follow.

Base = weight (kg) × 35 mL · Exercise adds ≈ 350 mL per 30 minutes

Worked example

Worked example: a 70 kg person needs about 2.45 litres on a rest day; add an hour of exercise and it is about 3.15 litres — roughly 12–13 glasses spread across the day.
