Social Security Break-Even Calculator

Claiming early pays less every month for life; waiting pays more. The break-even age is where the bigger late cheque has repaid everything you gave up by waiting. This uses the standard US reduction and delayed-credit rules against your full-retirement benefit.

Early: −5/9% per month for the first 36 months early, −5/12% beyond · Late: +8% per year to 70

Worked example

Worked example: a $2,000 full-retirement benefit at 67 becomes $1,400 at 62 and $2,480 at 70. Waiting from 62 to 67 breaks even at about age 78–79; waiting to 70 breaks even against 67 at about 82–83.
