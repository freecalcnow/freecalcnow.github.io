Cooking Conversion Calculator

A cup of flour is not a cup of honey — volume-to-weight conversion only works with the ingredient’s density. Pick the ingredient, the amount and the units; the grams are right because the density is.

Grams = volume × ingredient density (g per cup) · 1 cup = 16 tbsp = 48 tsp = 236.6 mL · 1 oz = 28.35 g
