Air–Fuel Ratio & Lambda Calculator

Engines burn fuel by weight of air, and every fuel has one exact ratio where the oxygen precisely consumes it — stoichiometric. Richer makes power and soot; leaner makes economy and heat. Tuners speak lambda (1.0 = stoichiometric for any fuel) because raw AFR numbers change meaning with every fuel.

λ = actual AFR ÷ stoichiometric AFR · Petrol stoich 14.7 · Diesel 14.5 · E85 ≈ 9.8 · Methanol 6.4 · Ethanol 9.0 · Propane 15.5 · Rich: λ < 1 · Lean: λ > 1

Worked example

Worked example: a petrol engine measured at 12.5:1 is running λ = 12.5 ÷ 14.7 = 0.85 — classic full-power richness. The same 12.5:1 on E85 would be λ = 1.28, dangerously lean: identical AFR, opposite meaning. That is why lambda exists.
