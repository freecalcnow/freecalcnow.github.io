Day Rate Calculator

Contractors in the UK, Australia and much of Europe price by the day. Convert between hourly, daily and annual figures without losing the hours-per-day assumption that silently changes every answer.

Day rate = Hourly rate × hours per day · Annual = Day rate × days per week × weeks per year

Worked example

Worked example: $75 an hour on an 8-hour day is a $600 day rate. Four days a week for 48 weeks at that rate is $115,200 a year — the “4-day week” costs a full day-rate × 48 against the 5-day figure, and now you can see it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a standard day?

Most contracts mean 7.5–8 working hours. If a client expects 10, your effective hourly rate just fell by a fifth — renegotiate the day, not the rate.

Inside or outside tax rules?

Day rates are quoted gross. What you keep depends on your structure and jurisdiction — run the annual figure through the take-home estimator for a rough net.
