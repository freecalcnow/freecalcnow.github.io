Wind Load Calculator

Wind pressure grows with the square of speed, so a storm at twice the speed pushes four times as hard. This uses the standard dynamic pressure with a shape factor for a flat surface facing the wind — the first-pass number behind fence, sign and panel design.

Pressure = ½ × ρ × v² × shape factor · Force = pressure × area

Worked example

Worked example: 100 km/h wind on a 10 m² wall → pressure ≈ 473 Pa with a 1.0 shape factor, a force of about 4,730 N — like parking a small car's weight against it, sideways.
