Race Time Predictor (Riegel) Calculator

Peter Riegel's formula scales a race performance to other distances using the endurance exponent 1.06 — the standard predictor since 1977. Enter a recent race for predicted times at 5K, 10K, half and full marathon.

T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ ÷ D₁)^1.06
