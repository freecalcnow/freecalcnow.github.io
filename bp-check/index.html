Blood Pressure Checker

Two numbers: systolic (pressure when the heart beats) over diastolic (pressure between beats). The American Heart Association sorts readings into five bands; this tool places yours and shows the whole table so you can see how close the next band is.

Categories (AHA): Normal <120 and <80 · Elevated 120–129 and <80 · Stage 1: 130–139 or 80–89 · Stage 2: ≥140 or ≥90 · Crisis: >180 and/or >120. The worse of the two numbers sets the category.

Worked example

Worked example: 122/78 is 'Elevated' — the systolic is in the 120s while the diastolic stays under 80. Note that 120/80 itself counts as Stage 1, because the diastolic has reached 80 — the worse of the two numbers always sets the category.
