IQR & Outlier Calculator

Averages get dragged around by extremes; the IQR ignores them. It is the spread of the middle 50% of your data — and the standard 1.5 × IQR fence is how box plots decide which points earn the 'outlier' label.

IQR = Q3 − Q1 (Tukey hinges: medians of the upper and lower halves) · Fences at Q1 − 1.5×IQR and Q3 + 1.5×IQR

Worked example

Worked example: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80 → Q1 = 25, median = 45, Q3 = 65, IQR = 40, and nothing falls outside the fences.
