Salt Converter (Kosher ↔ Table)

A teaspoon of Diamond Crystal weighs 2.9 g; a teaspoon of table salt weighs 6 g — double the salt for the same spoon. Recipes written for one salt and cooked with another are quietly ruined. This converts by weight between the common salts, so the seasoning lands where the recipe aimed.

Grams = volume × grams-per-teaspoon of your salt · New volume = grams ÷ grams-per-teaspoon of the target salt (1 tbsp = 3 tsp, 1 cup = 48 tsp)

Worked example

Worked example: a recipe's 1 tablespoon of Diamond Crystal is 8.7 g of salt. In table salt that is only 1.45 teaspoons — use the recipe's tablespoon of table salt instead and the dish gets more than double the intended salt.
