Soil Swell Factor Calculator

Dug soil expands: a cubic yard in the ground (bank) becomes 1.1–1.65 loose yards in the truck, depending on the material. Haulage and disposal are billed on the loose volume — this converts, and counts the loads.

Loose volume = bank volume × (1 + swell %) · Loads = loose volume ÷ truck capacity (rounded up)

Worked example

Worked example: 100 bank yd³ of clay at 35% swell → 135 loose yd³ → twelve 12-yard truck loads (11.25 rounded up), not the nine the bank volume suggests.
