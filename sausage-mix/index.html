Sausage Seasoning Calculator

Serious sausage-making is done in percentages of the meat weight, not spoonfuls — it scales from 1 kg to 100 kg without drift. Salt around 1.8% seasons and binds; curing salt, where used, sits near 0.25% of the meat for Prague Powder #1. Enter your meat weight and percentages to get exact grams.

Each ingredient = meat weight × its % ÷ 100

Worked example

Worked example: 5 kg of meat at 1.8% salt, 0.25% cure #1 and 0.3% black pepper → 90 g salt, 12.5 g cure and 15 g pepper; the batch finishes at about 5,118 g.
