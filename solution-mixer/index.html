Mixing Two Solutions Calculator

Mixing solutions conserves solute: the final concentration is the volume-weighted average of the two. Enter both concentrations and volumes for the mixture's strength — the tool behind standardising, blending and tank mixing.

Final C = (C₁V₁ + C₂V₂) ÷ (V₁ + V₂)
