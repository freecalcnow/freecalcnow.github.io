Perpetuity Calculator

A perpetuity pays the same amount every year, forever — some old government bonds and ground rents really work this way. Its value today is surprisingly simple: the payment divided by the rate. Add growth and the payments rise each year, which is worth more — as long as the rate beats the growth.

Present value = payment ÷ rate · Growing perpetuity: payment ÷ (rate − growth)

Worked example

Worked example: $5,000 a year forever at a 5% rate is worth $5,000 ÷ 0.05 = $100,000 today. Growing at 2% a year it is worth $5,000 ÷ (0.05 − 0.02) = $166,667.
