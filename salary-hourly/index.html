Salary ↔ Hourly Calculator

The question behind a million job offers: “$50,000 a year is how much an hour?” Enter either side — the other side, and every pay period in between, fills in.

Hourly = Annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year) · Annual = Hourly × hours per week × weeks per year

Worked example

Worked example: $50,000 a year at 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year = $24.04 an hour. Working 37.5 hours a week instead? The same salary is $25.64 an hour — hours are half the answer, which is why this tool asks for them.

How to use

  1. Type your numbers in the boxes above.
  2. Tap Calculate — or just type, the answer updates as you go.
  3. Read the big answer and the breakdown table below it.

Frequently asked questions

Why do some sites say $50k = $25 an hour?

They assume roughly 2,000 working hours a year (40 hrs × 50 weeks). Neither answer is wrong — they are using different hours. Always check the hours behind the headline number.

Salaried but I work unpaid overtime?

Enter your real hours, not your contracted ones. A $50,000 salary at a true 50 hours a week is $19.23 an hour — a very different job.
