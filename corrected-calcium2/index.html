Corrected Calcium Calculator

About 40% of blood calcium rides on albumin, so a low albumin makes total calcium look low when the active calcium is fine. The Payne correction restates calcium at a standard albumin.

Corrected Ca (mg/dL) = measured Ca + 0.8 × (4.0 − albumin g/dL) · In mmol/L: + 0.02 × (40 − albumin g/L)
