Flow Rate Converter

Pumps, taps, rivers and IV drips all speak flow — in incompatible units. Enter one rate and read it in litres, gallons, cubic metres and cubic feet, per second, minute and hour.

Base unit: litres per second · 1 US gallon = 3.78541 L · 1 ft³ = 28.3168 L
