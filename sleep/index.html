Sleep Calculator

Sleep runs in roughly 90-minute cycles, and waking mid-cycle is what makes a full night feel broken. Enter your wake-up time; the bedtimes below count back in whole cycles, plus 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Bedtime = wake time − (cycles × 90 minutes) − 15 minutes to fall asleep

Worked example

Worked example: waking at 7:00 → bedtimes of 21:45 (6 cycles, 9 hours of sleep), 23:15 (5 cycles) or 00:45 (4 cycles). The 6-cycle option is the one to aim for.
