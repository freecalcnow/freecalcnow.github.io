Normal Distribution Probability Calculator

Heights, test scores, measurement errors: the bell curve is everywhere. Give it a mean and standard deviation and this finds the probability of landing between any two values — plus the tails on each side.

z = (x − mean) ÷ SD · P(between) = Φ(z₂) − Φ(z₁), Φ = the standard normal cumulative

Worked example

Worked example: IQ-style scores (mean 100, SD 15) — the chance of landing between 85 and 115 is 68.27%, the famous 'within one standard deviation' share.
