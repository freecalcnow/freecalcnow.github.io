Deck Screw Calculator

Decking is fixed with two screws at every joist crossing. Count the boards from the deck width and board size, count the joists from the deck length and spacing, multiply — then add waste for splits and drops, and convert to the boxes screws are actually sold in.

Boards = deck width ÷ (board width + gap) · Screws = boards × joists crossed × 2 × 1.10 waste

Worked example

Worked example: a 16 × 12 ft deck with 5.5-inch boards (¼-inch gap) and joists every 16 inches → 26 boards × 13 joists × 2 = 676 screws; +10% → 744, so three 350-count boxes (or one 1,050-count tub, if stocked).
