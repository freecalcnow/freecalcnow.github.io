RLC Impedance Calculator

In AC, inductors and capacitors fight back with reactances that point in opposite directions — at one special frequency they cancel exactly, leaving only the resistance: resonance. This computes impedance, phase angle and current at your frequency, plus that resonant point.

X(L) = 2πfL · X(C) = 1 ÷ (2πfC) · Z = √(R² + (X(L) − X(C))²) · f₀ = 1 ÷ (2π√(LC))

Worked example

Worked example: R = 50 Ω, L = 10 mH, C = 100 nF at 1 kHz → X(L) = 62.8 Ω, X(C) = 1,592 Ω, Z ≈ 1,530 Ω (capacitive). Resonance sits at 5,033 Hz, where Z falls to just 50 Ω.
