Stock Average (Average Down) Calculator

Buy 50 shares at $120 and 50 more at $90 and your break-even is not $105 by luck — it is exactly the cost-weighted average, $105. This tool works it out for two buys and shows where you stand against today's price.

Average cost = (shares₁ × price₁ + shares₂ × price₂) ÷ (shares₁ + shares₂)

Worked example

Worked example: 50 shares at $120 plus 50 at $90 cost $10,500 in total — an average of $105 a share. At a $110 market price you are $500 (4.8%) ahead.
