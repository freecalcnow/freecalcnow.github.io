Bond Yield to Maturity (YTM) Calculator

A bond's coupon is fixed, but its price moves — so the return you actually get is the yield to maturity: the rate that makes the price equal to every future coupon plus the face value, discounted. This solves it exactly, by iteration.

Price = Σ coupon ÷ (1 + YTM ÷ m)^t + face ÷ (1 + YTM ÷ m)^(m × years)
