Defrost Time Calculator

Thawing has two safe lanes: the fridge (slow, hands-off) and cold water (faster, needs attention). This tool times both from the weight — the room-temperature counter is not a third lane.

Fridge: ~24 hours per 4–5 lb (use 4 lb to be safe) · Cold water: ~30 minutes per lb, water changed every 30 minutes

Worked example

Worked example: a 15 lb turkey → fridge: about 90 hours (3¾ days — start Sunday for Thursday); cold water: about 7.5 hours of attended thawing.
