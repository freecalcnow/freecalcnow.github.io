Colour Contrast Checker

Text is readable when its contrast against the background is high enough — WCAG sets the bar at 4.5:1 for normal text. Enter two hex colours and get the exact ratio and the pass/fail at every level.

Relative luminance from sRGB (linearised) · Ratio = (lighter + 0.05) ÷ (darker + 0.05)

Worked example

Worked example: black (#000000) on white (#FFFFFF) is the maximum, 21:1. Mid-grey #777777 on white is 4.48:1 — just short of AA for normal text.
