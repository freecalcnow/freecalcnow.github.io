Commute Time Per Year Calculator

Forty minutes each way sounds minor. Multiplied by working days it becomes one of the largest time costs in a life. Enter your one-way time and working pattern for the yearly and career totals.

Yearly hours = one-way minutes × 2 × days per week × working weeks ÷ 60
