Gelatin Converter

Gelatin is sold by weight (powder) or by sheet (leaf), and graded by bloom — its setting strength. Converting honestly means matching strength, not just mass: a gram of 260-bloom platinum leaf sets harder than a gram of 125-bloom bronze.

Equivalent mass = mass × (bloom of yours ÷ bloom of theirs) · Sheets = equivalent mass ÷ grams per sheet

Worked example

Worked example: 10 g of 200-bloom powder = 10 g of gold leaf (200 bloom, 2 g per sheet) → 5 sheets. In platinum (260 bloom) you would need only 7.7 g — about 4.5 sheets.
