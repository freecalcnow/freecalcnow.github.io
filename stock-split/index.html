Stock Split Calculator

A split cuts the same pie into more, thinner slices: a 3-for-1 split triples your share count and thirds the price, leaving your total holding worth exactly what it was. A reverse split (1-for-5) does the opposite. This shows both sides of the trade.

New shares = shares × (split numerator ÷ denominator) · New price = price × (denominator ÷ numerator)
