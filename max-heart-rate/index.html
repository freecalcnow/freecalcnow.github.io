Maximum Heart Rate & Zones Calculator

The old 220 − age formula was never meant for individuals. The Tanaka formula (208 − 0.7 × age), fitted across thousands of tested adults, is the better estimate. Zones below use the standard percentage bands for training.

HRmax ≈ 208 − 0.7 × age (Tanaka) · Zones = fixed % bands of HRmax
