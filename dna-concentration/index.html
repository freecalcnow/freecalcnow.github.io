DNA Concentration from A260 Calculator

UV absorbance at 260 nm is the fastest DNA measurement in the lab: one absorbance unit corresponds to 50 µg/mL of double-stranded DNA (40 for RNA, ~33 for single-stranded DNA). Add your dilution and this gives the stock concentration and total yield — plus the A260/A280 purity ratio.

Concentration = A260 × factor × dilution · purity: A260/A280 ≈ 1.8 for clean DNA

Worked example

Worked example: A260 of 0.8 on a sample diluted 1:50 → 0.8 × 50 × 50 = 2,000 µg/mL = 2 µg/µL in the stock.
