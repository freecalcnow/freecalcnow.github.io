Parkland Formula Calculator (Burn Fluids)

Major burns leak plasma for hours, and the Parkland (Baxter) formula sizes the IV crystalloid needed to stay ahead of it: 4 ml per kilogram per percent of body surface burned, half of it inside the first 8 hours counted from the burn — not from arrival.

Total fluid = 4 ml × weight (kg) × %TBSA burned (2nd/3rd degree only) · First half in 8 hours from the time of the burn, second half over the next 16 hours

Worked example

Worked example: a 70 kg adult with 40% burns needs 4 × 70 × 40 = 11,200 ml of lactated Ringer's in 24 hours — 5,600 ml in the first 8 hours (700 ml/hour from the burn time) and 350 ml/hour after.
