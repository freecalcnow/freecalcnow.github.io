Bacterial Growth Calculator

Bacteria multiply by doubling: one becomes two, two become four. Enter the starting count, the doubling time and the elapsed time for the final count and the number of generations — growth that outruns intuition every time.

Final = start × 2^(time ÷ doubling time) · Generations = time ÷ doubling time
