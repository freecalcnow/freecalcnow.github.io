Mole Fraction Calculator

Mole fraction is composition counted in particles: component A's moles divided by all the moles present. It is the natural unit for vapour pressures, gas mixtures and solutions where molecules, not grams, do the work. The fractions always sum to exactly 1.

xA = nA ÷ (nA + nB) · xB = 1 − xA

Worked example

Worked example: 2 mol of A with 3 mol of B → xA = 0.4000 (40%) and xB = 0.6000 (60%).
