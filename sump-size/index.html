Sump Pump Size Calculator

The honest way to size a sump pump is to measure the inflow: with the pump off, time how fast water rises in the pit during wet weather. Pit area × rise rate gives gallons per minute arriving; the pump must beat that figure at your discharge head height, with margin.

Inflow (gal/min) = pit area (in²) × rise (in/min) ÷ 231 · Recommended pump = inflow × 1.5 safety margin, rated at your head height

Worked example

Worked example: an 18-inch pit (area ≈ 254 in²) rising 2 inches in a minute → 254 × 2 ÷ 231 ≈ 2.2 gal/min inflow. A pump delivering at least ~3.3 gal/min (200 gal/hr) at the discharge head covers it — most ⅓ HP pumps do far more; the method matters in fast-rising pits.
