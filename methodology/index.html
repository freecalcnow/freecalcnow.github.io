How we calculate

Every calculator on FreeCalcNow shows its formula on its own page, with a worked example using real numbers so you can check the maths yourself.

Formulas follow the standard textbook and professional conventions for each field (for example, the loan payment formula P·r·(1+r)ⁿ/((1+r)ⁿ−1) for monthly payments). Currency conversions use exact unit factors; temperature and other non-linear conversions use their exact formulae.

Results are illustrations based on the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates, taxes or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
