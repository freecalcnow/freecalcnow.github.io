Battery Charge Time Calculator

Charging is never as fast as dividing capacity by current — the current tapers as the battery fills, and conversion wastes a slice as heat. This estimates the full charge time from capacity and charge current with an efficiency allowance, for the charge range you actually use.

Charge time = capacity needed (mAh) ÷ (charge current × efficiency) · Capacity needed = total capacity × (target % − starting %)

Worked example

Worked example: a 10,000 mAh power bank charging at 2,000 mA and 85% efficiency, from empty to full → 10,000 ÷ 1,700 ≈ 5.9 hours. From 20% to 80% — the kind range for battery health — about 3.5 hours.
