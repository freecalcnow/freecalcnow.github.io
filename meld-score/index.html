MELD Score Calculator

MELD (Model for End-Stage Liver Disease) turns three blood results into a score from 6 to 40 that ranks how urgently a patient needs a liver transplant. Higher is sicker; transplant lists are ordered by it.

MELD = 3.78 × ln(bilirubin) + 11.2 × ln(INR) + 9.57 × ln(creatinine) + 6.43 — labs floored at 1.0, creatinine capped at 4.0 (and set to 4.0 on recent dialysis), result rounded and held between 6 and 40

Worked example

Worked example: bilirubin 2.5, creatinine 1.8, INR 1.6, no dialysis → MELD 21, in the band where roughly 1 in 5 patients died within 3 months in the original studies.
