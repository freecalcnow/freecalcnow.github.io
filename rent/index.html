Rent Affordability Calculator

The classic guide is the 30% rule: rent should take no more than 30% of gross income. Lenders and landlords also look at total debt, so this tool checks both — and works backwards from a rent you are eyeing to the income it needs.

Max rent (30% rule) = 0.30 × gross monthly income · Debt check: rent ≤ 0.36 × income − other debts · Income needed = rent ÷ 0.30

Worked example

Worked example: $5,000 a month gross → up to $1,500 of rent by the 30% rule. With $300 of other debts, the 36% total-debt check allows $1,500 too (0.36 × 5,000 − 300). A $1,800 rent would need about $6,000 a month of income.
