Dew Point Calculator

Dew point is the temperature at which air can no longer hold its moisture — and the comfort number weather apps hide. High dew points feel oppressive whatever the temperature says.

Magnus formula: dew point = T × saturation adjustment from relative humidity (accurate within ~0.4 °C for normal weather)
