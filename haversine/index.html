Haversine Distance Calculator

Latitude and longitude are angles, not distances — the haversine formula turns them into the true shortest path over the sphere: the great-circle distance that flights (roughly) follow and 'as the crow flies' means.

a = sin²(Δlat÷2) + cos(lat₁) × cos(lat₂) × sin²(Δlon÷2) · Distance = 2 × R × atan2(√a, √(1−a)) · R = 6,371 km

Worked example

Worked example: London (51.5074, −0.1278) to New York (40.7128, −74.0060) is about 5,570 km great-circle — the number behind the roughly 7-hour flight, and noticeably less than adding up the map's apparent east–west gap, because the route arcs north over the Atlantic.
