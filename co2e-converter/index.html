CO₂e Emissions Converter

A kilogram of CO₂e means nothing to anyone. Converted, it becomes a kilometre driven, a fraction of a flight, a tree-year of absorption. This tool translates any footprint figure into the standard comparison units used by the UK government conversion factors.

Equivalents (approximate, widely used factors): average petrol car ≈ 0.17 kg CO₂e per km · One tree absorbs ≈ 21 kg per year · London–New York economy seat ≈ 980 kg · Average home's annual energy ≈ 3,400 kg

Worked example

Worked example: a footprint of 2,500 kg CO₂e equals about 14,700 km of driving (0.17 kg/km), roughly two and a half London–New York flights, or the absorption of about 119 trees working for a year.
