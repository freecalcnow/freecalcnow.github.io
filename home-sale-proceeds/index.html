Home Sale Proceeds Calculator

The sale price is the headline; the proceeds are the truth. Between them sit the remaining mortgage, the agents' commission and the seller's closing costs. This shows your net cheque and your true equity percentage before you sign a listing.

Net proceeds = sale price − mortgage owed − (price × commission %) − (price × closing %) − other costs

Worked example

Worked example: sell at $450,000 with $280,000 still owed, 5.5% commission and 1.5% other closing costs → fees of $31,500 and net proceeds of $138,500.
