Break-Even ROAS Calculator

A ROAS of 3 sounds great until your margin says you needed 4. Break-even ROAS is set entirely by gross margin: at a 25% margin you must return $4 of revenue per $1 of ads just to stand still. Work it out before the campaign, not after the invoice.

Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin (as a decimal) · Maximum CPA = average order value × gross margin %

Worked example

Worked example: a store with a 35% gross margin breaks even at a ROAS of 1 ÷ 0.35 = 2.86. On a $70 average order it can pay at most $24.50 per sale to ads — every CPA below that is profit, every one above is a loss dressed as growth.
