Blood Volume Calculator (Nadler)

An average adult carries roughly 5 litres of blood, but the real figure scales with body size. The Nadler formula — the standard estimate used in transfusion and blood-donation medicine — works it out from height and weight, and splits it into plasma and red cells by your haematocrit.

Nadler (male) = 0.3669 × height³ + 0.03219 × weight + 0.6041 · (female) = 0.3561 × height³ + 0.03308 × weight + 0.1833, height in metres, result in litres

Worked example

Worked example: a man of 175 cm and 70 kg → about 4.82 litres of blood, of which roughly 2.65 litres is plasma at a haematocrit of 45%.
