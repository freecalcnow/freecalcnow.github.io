Two-Proportion z-Test Calculator

Two groups, two success rates — the statistic behind every A/B test readout. This pools the proportions under the null hypothesis, computes the z statistic and two-tailed p-value, and adds the confidence interval for the difference itself.

z = (p̂₁ − p̂₂) ÷ √(p̂(1 − p̂)(1/n₁ + 1/n₂)), with p̂ the pooled rate

Worked example

Worked example: 45 of 400 (11.25%) versus 62 of 420 (14.76%) → z ≈ −1.45, p ≈ 0.15 — a gap this size turns up by chance about 1 time in 7, so it is not proof yet.
