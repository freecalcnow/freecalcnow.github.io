Party Drinks Quantity Calculator

The standard catering rule: one drink per guest per hour, with the first hour counting double for many crowds — this tool uses the steady version and splits it across beer, wine, spirits and soft drinks in the usual proportions, then converts to bottles and cans to buy.

Total drinks = guests × hours × ~1 · Bottles of wine = wine drinks ÷ 5 glasses · Beer = cans/bottles as counted
