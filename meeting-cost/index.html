Meeting Cost Calculator

Meetings feel free because nobody writes a cheque — but six salaries in a room for an hour is a real purchase. This adds it up: the cost of one meeting, the burn rate per minute, and what a weekly repeat quietly costs a year.

Cost = people × average hourly cost × hours · Per minute = people × hourly cost ÷ 60

Worked example

Worked example: 6 people at an average $55 an hour for 60 minutes = $330. The same meeting weekly costs $17,160 a year.
