HbA1c to Average Glucose Converter (eAG)

HbA1c is a percentage; glucose meters speak mg/dL. The ADA's ADAG study linked them with one equation, so a lab A1c can be read as the average glucose it came from — the number that makes an A1c result feel real.

eAG (mg/dL) = 28.7 × A1c − 46.7 · A1c = (eAG + 46.7) ÷ 28.7 · mmol/L = mg/dL ÷ 18.016

Worked example

Worked example: an A1c of 6.5% (the diabetes threshold) → 28.7 × 6.5 − 46.7 = about 140 mg/dL, or 7.8 mmol/L. An A1c of 7.0% ≈ 154 mg/dL.
