Skinfold Body Fat Calculator (Jackson–Pollock)

Calipers pinch subcutaneous fat at standard sites; the Jackson–Pollock 3-site equations convert the sum into body density, and the Siri equation turns density into body fat percentage. Men's sites are chest, abdomen and thigh; women's are triceps, suprailiac and thigh.

Men: density = 1.10938 − 0.0008267·Σ + 0.0000016·Σ² − 0.0002574·age · Fat % = 495 ÷ density − 450 (Siri)

Worked example

Worked example: a 30-year-old man with chest 10, abdomen 15, thigh 12 (sum 37) → density ≈ 1.072, body fat ≈ 11.2%.
