Fuel Economy Converter (All Units)

The world quotes fuel economy in four incompatible dialects, and one of them (litres per 100 km) runs backwards — lower is better. This converter translates all four at once, using the exact gallon definitions, so a US window sticker and a European brochure finally agree.

L/100km = 235.215 ÷ mpg (US) · mpg (UK) = mpg (US) × 1.20095 · km/L = 100 ÷ L/100km

Worked example

Worked example: 30 mpg (US) is 7.84 L/100km, 12.75 km/L and 36.0 mpg in the UK — the same car, four numbers, because the UK gallon is bigger.
