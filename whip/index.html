WHIP Calculator (Baseball)

WHIP counts the baserunners a pitcher allows per inning: under 1.10 is excellent, over 1.40 is trouble. This tool adds the strikeout and walk rates that explain the WHIP.

WHIP = (walks + hits) ÷ innings pitched · K/9 = strikeouts × 9 ÷ innings · Note: innings like 6.1 mean 6⅓, not 6.1

Worked example

Worked example: 180 innings, 160 hits, 50 walks, 200 strikeouts → WHIP 1.167, K/9 exactly 10.0, BB/9 2.5 — a front-line starter's line.
