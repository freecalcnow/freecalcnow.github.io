Musical Note Frequency Calculator

Every musical note is a frequency. In equal temperament each semitone multiplies the frequency by the twelfth root of 2, anchored on A4 (MIDI 69) at 440 Hz. Enter a MIDI note number for its name, exact frequency and wavelength in air.

f = 440 × 2^((n − 69) ÷ 12) · Wavelength = speed of sound (343 m/s) ÷ f

Worked example

Worked example: MIDI 60 is middle C (C4) → 440 × 2^(−9/12) = 261.63 Hz, wavelength about 1.31 m. MIDI 69 (A4) is exactly 440 Hz.
