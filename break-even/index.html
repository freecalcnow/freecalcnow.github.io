Break-Even Calculator

Break-even is the number of units you must sell to cover every cost — below it you lose money on every sale’s shortfall, above it each sale adds its contribution to profit.

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (price per unit − variable cost per unit)

Worked example

Worked example: $5,000 a month of fixed costs, a $50 price and $30 variable cost per unit → each sale contributes $20, so break-even is 250 units a month, or $12,500 of revenue.
