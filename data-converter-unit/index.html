Data Size Converter

A '1 TB' drive shows as about 931 GB on your computer because makers count in powers of 1,000 and computers in powers of 1,024. This converts both ways so the missing space is explained.

Decimal: 1 KB = 1,000 bytes, 1 MB = 1,000 KB · Binary: 1 KiB = 1,024 bytes, 1 MiB = 1,024 KiB
