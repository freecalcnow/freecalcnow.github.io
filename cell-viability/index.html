Cell Viability Calculator

Viability is the first quality number on any cell culture: of the cells counted (usually by dye exclusion — live cells keep the dye out), what share are alive? Seeding, freezing and experiments all key off it.

Viability % = live cells ÷ (live + dead cells) × 100

Worked example

Worked example: 850 live and 150 dead in a count → 850 ÷ 1,000 = 85% viable. Most culture work wants 90%+ before an experiment.
