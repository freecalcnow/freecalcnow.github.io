Turkey Size Calculator

Turkey maths has three questions: how big a bird, how long to thaw it, and how long to roast. All three scale from the guest count and how serious you are about leftovers.

Bird weight = guests × per-person amount (450 g plain, 680 g for generous leftovers) · Fridge thaw ≈ 24 hours per 2–2.3 kg (4–5 lb) · Roast ≈ 14 min per lb at 165 °C / 325 °F, unstuffed

Worked example

Worked example: 10 guests with leftovers planned → 10 × 0.68 = a 6.8 kg (15 lb) bird, needing about 3–4 days of fridge thawing and roughly 3½ hours in the oven, plus resting.
