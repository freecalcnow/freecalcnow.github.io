Child Height Predictor

Genetics loads the dice: the mid-parental height is the standard first estimate of a child's adult stature, used by paediatricians worldwide. It comes with a wide honest range — most children land within about ±8.5 cm of it.

Boy: (father + mother + 13 cm) ÷ 2 · Girl: (father + mother − 13 cm) ÷ 2 · Expected range ± 8.5 cm

Worked example

Worked example: father 178 cm, mother 165 cm → a son's mid-parental height is 178 cm (range about 169.5–186.5 cm); a daughter's is 165 cm.
