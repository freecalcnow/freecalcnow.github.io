Backfill Calculator

Backfill is what is left of the hole after the pipe, duct or footing is in it. Ordering the full trench volume again wastes money and trucking; ordering too little stops the job. This nets the structure out and adds a compaction allowance.

Backfill = trench volume − structure volume · Pipe volume = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × length · Order ≈ net × 1.12 for compaction & loss

Worked example

Worked example: a trench 100 ft × 2 ft × 3 ft = 600 ft³ (22.2 yd³); a 12-inch pipe through it displaces 78.5 ft³ (2.9 yd³) → net 19.3 yd³, order about 21.6 yd³.
