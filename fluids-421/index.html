4-2-1 Maintenance Fluids Calculator

Holliday and Segar's 1957 rule estimates a patient's hourly water needs from weight alone: 4 ml for each of the first 10 kg, 2 ml for the next 10, and 1 ml for every kilo after that. It remains the default maintenance-fluid calculation in paediatrics and adult wards alike.

Hourly rate = 4 ml/kg for the first 10 kg + 2 ml/kg for the next 10 kg + 1 ml/kg for each kg above 20 · Daily = the same rule as 100 / 50 / 20 ml/kg/day

Worked example

Worked example: a 26 kg child needs (10 × 4) + (10 × 2) + (6 × 1) = 66 ml/hour — about 1,580 ml a day. A 70 kg adult needs 40 + 20 + 50 = 110 ml/hour, roughly 2.6 litres a day.
