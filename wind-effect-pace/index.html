Wind Effect on Running Pace Calculator

Air resistance is a small share of running cost — until the wind picks up. This estimates the pace cost of a headwind or tailwind from wind speed and your pace, using the standard drag model.

Extra power ≈ ½ × ρ × CdA × (v + w)² × w relative terms — simplified: cost scales with the square of apparent wind; a tailwind returns only about half of what the same headwind takes
