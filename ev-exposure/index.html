Exposure (EV) Calculator

Exposure Value compresses aperture and shutter speed into one number describing light: EV 15 is bright sun (the Sunny 16 rule), EV 8 a well-lit interior, EV 2 a night street. Give any aperture/shutter pair to get its EV, then read off equivalent settings that hold the same exposure.

EV = log₂(N² ÷ t) at ISO 100 · each EV step halves or doubles the light

Worked example

Worked example: f/16 at 1/125 s → EV ≈ 15 — full sunshine. The same EV at f/2.8 needs about 1/4000 s.
