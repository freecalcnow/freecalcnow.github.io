Laundry Cost Calculator

Nobody budgets for laundry, which is why it is a perfect small leak: a dollar a load, five loads a week, is $260 a year before the machine itself wears out. This prices a load properly — energy, water, detergent — and scales it to your household's real habit.

Cost per load = (kWh per load × electricity rate) + (litres per load ÷ 1000 × water rate per m³) + detergent per load

Worked example

Worked example: 2.0 kWh at $0.30, 50 litres of water and $0.25 of detergent → $0.95 a load. Five loads a week makes about $247 a year.
