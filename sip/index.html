SIP Calculator

A SIP invests the same amount every month, so you buy more units when prices dip and fewer when they rise. This shows the future value of that habit: your contributions, the growth on top, and the total — assuming a steady average return, which real markets never quite deliver.

Future value = P × [((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i), where i = annual return ÷ 12 and n = months

Worked example

Worked example: $500 a month for 15 years at 12% a year → you put in $90,000 and it grows to about $252,288 — the growth ($162,288) ends up bigger than everything you paid in.
