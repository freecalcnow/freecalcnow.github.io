Expected Value (EV) Calculator

Expected value is the average outcome over many repeats. A bet can be likely to win and still be a bad bet — EV is the test that separates the two.

EV = (win probability × amount won) − (lose probability × amount lost) · Fair odds = 1 ÷ probability
