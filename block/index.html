Concrete Block Calculator

Block counts are wall area divided by block-plus-mortar area — but the mortar joint is where estimates go wrong. This tool includes the joint, rounds up to whole blocks, and adds your waste allowance on top.

Blocks = wall area ÷ ((block length + joint) × (block height + joint)), rounded up, × (1 + waste %)

Worked example

Worked example: a 10 m × 3 m wall with 400 × 200 mm blocks and 10 mm joints → each block covers 0.41 × 0.21 m → 349 blocks, 384 with 10% waste.

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
