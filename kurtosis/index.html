Kurtosis Calculator

Skewness measures lopsidedness; kurtosis measures the tails. A high-kurtosis dataset looks calm in the middle and then produces wild outliers — the shape behind financial crashes and 'impossible' measurements. Paste a list of numbers to get kurtosis and excess kurtosis.

Kurtosis = m₄ ÷ m₂², where m₄ and m₂ are the 4th and 2nd central moments · Excess kurtosis = kurtosis − 3

Worked example

Worked example: for 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42 the kurtosis is about 2.76 — excess −0.24, platykurtic: a little flatter than a normal distribution, with slightly thinner tails.
