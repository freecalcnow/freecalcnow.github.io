Darts Average & Checkout Calculator

A darts average is points scored per three darts thrown. Enter legs won in a 501 match with total darts thrown (or points and darts directly) for your average — and the textbook finishes for the scores players actually face.

3-dart average = total points ÷ darts thrown × 3
