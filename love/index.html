Love Calculator

The playground classic, done properly: both names are written with the word “loves”, the letters are counted, and the counts are folded together pair by pair until a two-digit percentage remains. The same two names always give the same score.

Counts of each letter in “name₁ loves name₂” are added in mirrored pairs, again and again, until two digits remain — that is the percentage

Worked example

Worked example: the method is the one from school notebooks — write the names, count the letters, keep adding mirrored counts until only two digits are left. Those two digits are the match.
