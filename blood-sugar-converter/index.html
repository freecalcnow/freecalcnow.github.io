Blood Sugar Converter (mg/dL ↔ mmol/L)

Two units divide the world's glucose meters: mg/dL (US and parts of Asia) and mmol/L (most everywhere else). The factor is 18 — a reading of 100 mg/dL is 5.6 mmol/L.

mmol/L = mg/dL ÷ 18 · mg/dL = mmol/L × 18
