Child Adult-Height Predictor

The mid-parental (Tanner) method is the estimate paediatricians actually use: average the parents' heights, add 13 cm for a boy or subtract 13 cm for a girl. Most children land within about 8.5 cm of the figure — genetics loads the dice, it does not fix the roll.

Boy: (father + mother + 13) ÷ 2 · Girl: (father + mother − 13) ÷ 2 · Usual range: estimate ± 8.5 cm

Worked example

Worked example: father 180 cm, mother 165 cm → a son's estimate is (180 + 165 + 13) ÷ 2 = 179 cm, with a usual range of about 170.5 to 187.5 cm. A daughter's estimate is 166 cm.
