Average Speed Calculator

The classic trap: drive somewhere at 60 and back at 80 and your average is not 70. Average speed is total distance over total time, and the slower leg eats more time than the fast one saves. Enter both legs and see the honest figure.

Average speed = (distance₁ + distance₂) ÷ (time₁ + time₂)

Worked example

Worked example: 60 km in 1 hour, then 40 km in half an hour → 100 km in 1.5 hours = 66.67 km/h. Averaging the two leg speeds (60 and 80) would wrongly give 70.
