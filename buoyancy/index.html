Buoyancy Calculator

Archimedes' principle: a fluid pushes up on a submerged object with a force equal to the weight of the fluid displaced. If that force beats the object's own weight, it floats — with exactly enough of itself underwater to displace its weight.

F = ρ × V × g (ρ = fluid density, V = submerged volume, g = 9.81)

Worked example

Worked example: 0.01 m³ submerged in fresh water → 98.1 N of upthrust, the weight of 10 kg of water.
