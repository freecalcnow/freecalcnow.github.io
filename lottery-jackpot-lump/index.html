Lottery Lump Sum vs Annuity Calculator

The headline jackpot is the annuity total, not the prize. The lump sum is smaller, taxed at once, and still often wins on present value — this compares both honestly at your discount rate and tax rate.

Lump sum ≈ jackpot × cash ratio (often ~45–50%) · Annuity PV = yearly payment × annuity factor at your discount rate · Both shown after the tax rate you enter
