TV Viewing Distance Calculator

Two film-and-broadcast standards answer 'how far should the sofa be?' SMPTE suggests the screen fill about 30° of your view for everyday watching; THX suggests 36° for a cinema feel. Enter the diagonal and get both distances, plus the screen's real width and height.

Screen width = diagonal × 0.8716 (16:9) · Distance = (width ÷ 2) ÷ tan(angle ÷ 2)

Worked example

Worked example: a 55-inch TV is 47.9 in wide. SMPTE's 30° puts you 7.45 ft away; THX's 36° puts you 6.15 ft away.
