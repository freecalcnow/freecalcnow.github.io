Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Enter the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) and Naegele’s rule gives the estimated due date, the likely conception date, and — for today — how far along the pregnancy is and which trimester.

Due date = LMP + 280 days (40 weeks) · Conception ≈ LMP + 14 days

Worked example

Worked example: an LMP of 1 October 2026 → due date 8 July 2027. Only about 1 in 20 babies arrive on the exact day — the due date is the centre of a normal window of several weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Why 280 days?

Naegele’s rule counts 40 weeks from the LMP, assuming ovulation on day 14 of a 28-day cycle. A dating ultrasound in the first trimester is more accurate and overrides this estimate.

How many babies arrive on their due date?

Only about 4–5%. Most arrive within two weeks either side — which is why clinicians treat the due date as a window, not an appointment.
