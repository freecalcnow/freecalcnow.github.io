Barbell Plate Calculator

Loading maths under gym lighting: subtract the bar, halve it, then greedily stack the biggest plates first. This lists the plates for EACH side, the exact total they make, and the closest loads if your target cannot be built exactly.

Per side = (target − bar) ÷ 2, filled largest-first from the plate set

Worked example

Worked example: 100 kg on a 20 kg bar → 40 kg per side: one 25 and one 15 per side. Total loaded: exactly 100 kg.
