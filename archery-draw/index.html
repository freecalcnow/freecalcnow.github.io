Arrow Length & Spine Calculator

An arrow must clear the bow at full draw with the point safely past the riser — too short is a genuine injury risk — and its stiffness (spine) must match the bow's draw weight or accuracy dissolves. This tool sizes both from your draw length and bow.

Draw length ≈ arm span (fingertip to fingertip) ÷ 2.5 · Arrow length = draw length + 1 to 2 inches of safety past the rest · Spine charts: heavier draw weight and longer arrows both need stiffer (lower-number) spine

Worked example

Worked example: an arm span of 178 cm (70 in) gives a draw length of about 28 in, so arrows of 29–30 in. At a 45 lb draw weight and 29 in arrows, the charts call for roughly a 400 spine — going to 60 lb would need nearer 340.
