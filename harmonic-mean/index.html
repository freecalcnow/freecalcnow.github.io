Harmonic Mean Calculator

Average 30 km/h going and 60 km/h returning and the true average speed is 40, not 45 — rates need the harmonic mean: the count divided by the sum of reciprocals. This tool shows all three means side by side; for positive numbers it is always harmonic ≤ geometric ≤ arithmetic.

Harmonic mean = n ÷ (1/x₁ + 1/x₂ + … + 1/xₙ)
