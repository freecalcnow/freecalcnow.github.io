Cricket Strike Rate & Economy Calculator

Cricket's two headline rates: a batsman's strike rate (runs per 100 balls) and a bowler's economy (runs per over) and strike rate (balls per wicket). Overs are entered in cricket notation — 9.3 means 9 overs and 3 balls, not 9.3 overs.

Batting SR = runs ÷ balls × 100 · Economy = runs ÷ overs (overs = full overs + balls ÷ 6) · Bowling SR = balls bowled ÷ wickets
