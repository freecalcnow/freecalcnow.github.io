Battery Bank Size Calculator

Battery banks are sized by three numbers: what you use daily, how many sunless days to survive, and how deep the chemistry may be drained. This turns them into kWh and amp-hours at your system voltage.

Nominal capacity = daily use × autonomy days ÷ depth of discharge · Ah = kWh × 1000 ÷ system voltage
