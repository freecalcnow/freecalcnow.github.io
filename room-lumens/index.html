Room Lighting (Lumens) Calculator

Lighting is planned in lux — lumens landing on each square metre — and rooms differ five-fold: a bedroom glows at 100 lux while a kitchen worktop wants 300 or more. Enter the room and its job, and get the total lumens and a bulb count.

Total lumens needed = room area (m²) × target lux for the room's use · Bulbs = total ÷ lumens per bulb

Worked example

Worked example: a 4 × 5 m kitchen (20 m²) at 300 lux needs 6,000 lumens — eight 800-lumen bulbs, or fewer, brighter fittings plus under-cabinet strips for the worktops.
