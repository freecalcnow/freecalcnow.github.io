Acceleration Calculator

Acceleration is how fast velocity changes. From a start velocity, an end velocity and a time, you get the acceleration and the distance travelled while it happened.

a = (v₁ − v₀) ÷ t · Distance = v₀t + ½at²

Worked example

Worked example: a car going from 0 to 27.8 m/s (100 km/h) in 10 seconds accelerates at 2.78 m/s² and covers 139 metres doing it.
