UK Take-Home Pay Calculator

Your UK salary after income tax and employee National Insurance for 2025/26 (England, Wales and Northern Ireland rates). It applies the £12,570 personal allowance — including the taper that removes £1 of allowance per £2 earned over £100,000 — then the 20/40/45% bands and 8%/2% NI.

Tax on bands above the personal allowance · NI 8% from £12,570 to £50,270, then 2%

Worked example: a £50,000 salary → £7,486 income tax and £2,994.40 National Insurance, leaving £39,519.60 a year (£3,293.30 a month).
