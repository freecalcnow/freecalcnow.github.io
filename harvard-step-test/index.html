Harvard Step Test Calculator

Step up and down for up to five minutes, then count recovery pulses: fitter hearts slow down faster. The Harvard index has graded fitness since the 1940s with nothing but a bench and a stopwatch.

Index = (duration in seconds × 100) ÷ (2 × sum of three 30-second recovery pulse counts at 1, 2 and 3 minutes after)
