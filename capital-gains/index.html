Capital Gains Tax Estimator

Capital gains tax is charged on the profit when you sell an asset — shares, crypto, a second property — not on the whole sale price. This estimator works out the gain, applies the rate you select, and shows the after-tax proceeds.

Gain = sale price − selling costs − cost basis (what you paid + buying costs) · Tax = gain × your rate

Worked example

Worked example: bought for $10,000, sold for $25,000 with $200 of fees → gain $14,800. At the 15% long-term rate the tax is $2,220 and you keep $22,580 of the proceeds.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
