Loan Comparison Calculator

A lower monthly payment often just means a longer loan — and more interest in total. This puts two offers side by side and judges them on the only number that cannot be spun: the total cost.

Payment = P × r(1 + r)^n ÷ [(1 + r)^n − 1] for each loan · Total cost = payment × months

Worked example

Worked example: $20,000 at 8% for 5 years costs $405.53 a month and $4,331.80 of interest. The same $20,000 at 6.5% for 7 years is only about $297 a month — but $4,948 of interest. Cheaper monthly, dearer overall.
