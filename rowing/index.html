Rowing Split Calculator

Rowers live by the split: the time each 500 m would take at your current pace. Power on an erg follows the physics of drag — watts grow with the cube of speed — which is why small split gains cost so much. This tool converts any distance and time into split and watts (the Concept2 relationship).

Split = time ÷ (distance ÷ 500) · Watts = 2.8 × (500 ÷ split in s)³
