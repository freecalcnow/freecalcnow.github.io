Ovulation Calculator

Ovulation happens about 14 days before your next period — not 14 days after your last one, which is why cycle length matters. Enter the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length for the fertile window, ovulation day and next period date.

Ovulation = LMP + (cycle length − 14) days · Fertile window = 5 days before ovulation to 1 day after · Due date if conceived = ovulation + 266 days
