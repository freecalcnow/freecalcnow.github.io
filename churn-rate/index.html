Churn Rate Calculator

Churn is the silent growth-killer: at 5% monthly churn you must replace over half your customers every year just to stand still. This tool converts a period's losses into monthly and annual churn, retention, and the average customer lifetime they imply.

Churn = customers lost ÷ customers at start · Monthly churn from a period = 1 − (1 − period churn)^(1 ÷ months) · Average lifetime = 1 ÷ monthly churn

Worked example

Worked example: starting a quarter with 2,000 customers and losing 240 gives a quarterly churn of 12% — about 4.2% a month, a 76% chance of keeping a customer for a full year, and an average lifetime of roughly 24 months.
