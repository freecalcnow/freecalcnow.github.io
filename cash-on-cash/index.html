Cash-on-Cash Return Calculator

Cash-on-cash return ignores the loan balance and the property price — it asks only: for the cash that left your bank account (deposit, closing costs, repairs), how much cash comes back each year?

Cash-on-cash = annual pre-tax cash flow ÷ total cash invested · Cash flow = NOI − annual debt service

Worked example

Worked example: $24,000 NOI minus $15,500 of loan payments leaves $8,500 a year. On $65,000 of cash in, that is a 13.08% cash-on-cash return.
