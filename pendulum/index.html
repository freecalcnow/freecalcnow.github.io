Pendulum Calculator

A pendulum's swing depends on its length and gravity — famously, not on its weight or (for small swings) how wide it swings. That reliability is what made pendulum clocks the best timekeepers for three centuries.

Period T = 2π × √(L ÷ g), with g = 9.81 m/s² on Earth · Frequency = 1 ÷ T

Worked example

Worked example: a 1-metre pendulum takes 2.01 seconds per full swing — just over 2 seconds, which is why grandfather-clock pendulums are about a metre long.
