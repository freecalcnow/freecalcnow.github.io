How to use this table

  1. Borrowing? Look for the lowest green number in the loan column — then check the total cost in the Loan Comparison Calculator.
  2. Saving? Look for the highest green number in the savings or FD column — then see what it grows to in the Compound Interest Calculator.
  3. Always ask the bank for the full cost: processing fee, insurance and early-repayment charges are not in this table.
