Law of Sines Calculator

The law of sines links every side of a triangle to the angle opposite it: a ÷ sin A = b ÷ sin B = c ÷ sin C. Know one complete pair and one more piece, and the rest of the triangle opens up.

a ÷ sin A = b ÷ sin B · So b = a × sin B ÷ sin A · And C = 180° − A − B, c = a × sin C ÷ sin A

Worked example

Worked example: side a = 10 opposite A = 30°, with B = 45° → b = 10 × sin 45° ÷ sin 30° ≈ 14.14. Then C = 105° and c ≈ 19.32.
