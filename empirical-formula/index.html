Empirical Formula Calculator

Burn a compound, measure what fraction is carbon, hydrogen, oxygen and nitrogen, and the simplest whole-number formula falls out of the mole ratios. This tool does the dividing and the rounding honestly.

Moles of each element = % ÷ atomic mass · Divide all by the smallest · Scale to whole numbers

Worked example

Worked example: 40.0% C, 6.7% H, 53.3% O → moles 3.33 : 6.65 : 3.33 → ratio 1 : 2 : 1 → CH₂O — the empirical formula of glucose (whose true formula is C₆H₁₂O₆).
