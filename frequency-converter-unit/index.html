Frequency Converter

A motor spinning at 3,600 RPM, a 50 Hz mains supply and a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi signal are all frequencies. This converts between Hz, kHz, MHz, GHz and RPM.

RPM = Hz × 60 · kHz = Hz ÷ 1,000 · Period (seconds) = 1 ÷ Hz
