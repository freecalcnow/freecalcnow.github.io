Ring Size Converter

Rings are sized by letters in the UK, numbers in the US and millimetres in the EU. Measure the inside diameter of a ring that fits (or wrap a strip of paper around the finger for circumference) and convert.

Circumference = π × diameter · US size ≈ (circumference in mm − 36.5) ÷ 2.55 · EU size = circumference in mm
