Wavelength & Frequency Calculator

Frequency and wavelength are joined by the speed of light: multiply them and you always get c. Convert either way — the maths behind radio bands, Wi-Fi channels and colours of light.

λ = c ÷ f · f = c ÷ λ · c = 299,792,458 m/s

Worked example

Worked example: a 100 MHz FM radio wave has a wavelength of about 3 metres — which is why FM aerials are that size. A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi signal: just 12.5 cm.
