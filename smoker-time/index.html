Smoker Time Calculator

Low-and-slow timing runs on hours per pound at a given pit temperature — a planning estimate that gets dinner on the table in the right half-day. The meat is done by temperature and tenderness, never by the clock alone.

Time ≈ weight × hours-per-pound for the cut at ~225–250 °F · Hotter pits (275 °F) run about 25% faster

Worked example

Worked example: a 12 lb brisket at 225 °F × 1.5 h/lb → about 18 hours, plus rest. Start the night before — every experienced pitmaster does.
