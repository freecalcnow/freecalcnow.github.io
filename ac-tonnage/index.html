AC Tonnage Calculator

Air conditioners are sized in 'tons' of cooling — one ton removes 12,000 BTU an hour. The honest method is a Manual J load calculation; the quick method used for first estimates is BTU per square foot, adjusted for climate, sun and ceiling height. This does the quick method and tells you plainly where it stops.

BTU ≈ area × climate factor (cool 20, moderate 25, hot 30 per sq ft) × sun and ceiling adjustments · Tons = BTU ÷ 12,000

Worked example

Worked example: 1,500 sq ft in a moderate climate → 1,500 × 25 = 37,500 BTU ≈ 3.1 tons, so a 3-ton unit is the nearest standard size. Bigger is not better: an oversized unit short-cycles and leaves the air humid.
