CAPM Calculator

The Capital Asset Pricing Model says an investment should pay for two things: the time value of money (the risk-free rate) and its risk compared with the market (beta). It is the standard way to estimate a cost of equity.

Expected return = risk-free rate + beta × (market return − risk-free rate)

Worked example

Worked example: risk-free 4%, beta 1.2, market return 10% → 4 + 1.2 × 6 = 11.20% expected return.
