Plastic Use Estimator

Plastic habits are invisible week to week and startling year to year. Enter your weekly bottles, bags and coffee cups and this annualises them — counts, an approximate weight using typical item masses, and the honest scale of one person's throwaway stream.

Yearly items = weekly items × 52 · Weight uses typical masses: bottle ≈ 25 g, bag ≈ 6 g, cup + lid ≈ 13 g

Worked example

Worked example: 7 bottles, 5 bags and 3 cups a week → 780 items a year weighing about 12.7 kg — one person's small mountain.
