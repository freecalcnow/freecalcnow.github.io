hCG Doubling Time Calculator

In a healthy early pregnancy, hCG rises steeply — classically doubling about every 48 hours at low levels, then slowing as levels climb. Two blood results and the hours between them give the actual doubling time and the percentage rise, the numbers clinicians compare against the expected minimum rise.

Doubling time = hours between tests × ln 2 ÷ ln(second ÷ first)
