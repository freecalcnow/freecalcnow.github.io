Crowdfunding Fee Calculator

Campaign pages show the gross. Platforms take about 5%, payment processing takes roughly 3% plus a small fee from every single backer — so a campaign with many small backers leaks more than one with a few large ones. This shows the net, and works backwards to the goal that nets what you need.

Net = raised − platform % − payment % − (fixed fee × backers) · Goal to set = (amount you need + fixed fee × backers) ÷ (1 − platform % − payment %)

Worked example

Worked example: raising $10,000 from 120 backers at 5% platform and 2.9% + $0.30 payment fees nets $9,174. To actually net $10,000 you would need to set the goal at about $10,897.

Frequently asked questions

How is this calculated?

With the standard formula shown above — the same one used in textbooks and professional tools. Every input is labelled, so you can check the working yourself.

How exact is the result?

Exact for the numbers you enter. Real-world results can differ where fees, rounding, changing rates or measurement error apply — each tool states its main assumptions.
