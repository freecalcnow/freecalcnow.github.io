Boil-Off & Gravity Calculator

The boil concentrates wort: water leaves as steam, the sugars stay. Enter pre-boil volume, boil length and your system's evaporation rate for the post-boil volume and the gravity shift — the difference between hitting and missing your target OG.

Loss = pre-boil volume × evaporation rate × hours · Post-boil gravity points = pre-boil points × (pre volume ÷ post volume)

Worked example

Worked example: 6.5 gallons at 1.050 boiled 60 minutes at 8%/hr → loss 0.52 gal → 5.98 gallons at 1.054 (50 points × 6.5/5.98 = 54.4).
