Car Lease Calculator

A lease payment is two things: the depreciation you use up, and a finance charge quoted as a 'money factor' instead of an APR. This tool shows both parts, and converts the money factor to the APR dealers avoid quoting.

Monthly depreciation = (adjusted cap cost − residual) ÷ term · Finance charge = (cap cost + residual) × money factor · APR ≈ money factor × 2400

Worked example

Worked example: a $40,000 car negotiated to $38,000, $695 acquisition fee, $2,000 down, 55% residual, money factor 0.00125 over 36 months → about $482 a month before tax: $408 of depreciation and $73 of finance charge.
