Circle of Fifths Key Finder

Pick a major key and read off everything the circle of fifths connects to it: how many sharps or flats its signature has, which notes they are, its relative minor, and the dominant and subdominant keys either side of it on the circle.

Each step clockwise adds a sharp (G, D, A, E, B, F#, C#); each step back adds a flat (F, Bb, Eb, Ab, Db, Gb, Cb) · Relative minor = tonic − 3 semitones
