UTM Link Builder

UTM parameters are how analytics tells a Facebook ad from a newsletter link. Hand-typing them invites typos that silently split your reports; this builds the tagged URL for you — parameters encoded, empty ones skipped, ready to paste into the ad, email or post.

Tagged URL = base URL + ?utm_source=…&utm_medium=…&utm_campaign=… (+ term & content when filled)

Worked example

Worked example: base https://example.com/spring-sale with source facebook, medium social and campaign spring_sale becomes https://example.com/spring-sale?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spring_sale.
